# AI trust crisis

## Definition

The AI trust crisis is the loss of user confidence that occurs when people are uncertain whether their data is used to train AI models. It frames privacy and data handling as a trust issue for AI products.

## Key Characteristics

- Triggered by uncertainty about whether user data is used for AI model training, not necessarily by confirmed misuse.
- Users are highly sensitive to any doubt about how training data is used.
- Treats privacy and data-handling practices as a matter of trust rather than only compliance.
- Strong user reactions can follow even when the uncertainty is about data use, as in the [[Dropbox]] case.

## Applications

- Evaluating how an AI product communicates its data-use and model-training policies.
- Assessing user reaction to changes in terms of service or data handling that touch on AI training.
- Designing privacy disclosures and consent flows for AI features that process user content.
