# annotated presentation format

## Definition

The annotated presentation format is the author's recurring practice of publishing detailed written notes alongside a conference talk, so that the talk's content can be read in full as well as watched. The author applies it to conference talks and groups the resulting posts under the `annotatedtalks` tag on their blog.

## Key Characteristics

- Pairs a talk with long-form written notes published alongside it.
- Used for conference talks, not for other kinds of writing.
- Collected on the author's blog under the `annotatedtalks` tag.
- Applied as a standing publishing habit rather than a one-off choice.

## Applications

- Publishing notes for the author's [[PyCon US 2024]] keynote once the video is live on YouTube. The author plans to use the format for this talk.
- Making a talk's material searchable and readable for people who did not attend or do not watch video.
- An example annotated presentation is the talk 'Catching up on the weird world of LLMs', which reached over 17,000 views on YouTube.
- The annotations for these presentations were produced with a custom annotation tool built with help from GPT-4.
