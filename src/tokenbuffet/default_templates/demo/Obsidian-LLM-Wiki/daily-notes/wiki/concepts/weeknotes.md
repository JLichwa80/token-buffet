# weeknotes

## Definition

Weeknotes are short, regular blog posts in which a person summarizes their recent work, releases, and notable activity. They function as a running log of the author's output over time, and the author typically tags them on their site to form a recurring series.

## Key Characteristics

- Short and published on a regular cadence, typically weekly
- Summarize recent work, such as software releases, blog entries, and small discoveries (TILs)
- Often tagged on the author's site, which marks them as a recurring series
- May open with an acknowledgement of lateness when posts have been skipped
- Can include commentary on current events alongside the author's own output
- Each weeknotes edition follows a consistent structure of thematic sections, with entries, releases, and TILs listed under their own headings.
- A weeknotes edition typically covers the posts written in that period, new projects or newsletters, releases, and TIL articles.
## Applications

- Personal and professional logs of a developer's or writer's output
- Public progress reports on open-source projects and releases
- Archives that show how an author's work evolves across weeks and months
- Simon Willison publishes a recurring "weeknotes" series on his blog, and each post links to the tag page that collects the series.
