# Slop

## Definition

Slop is a term for unwanted AI-generated content. The source gives it as a new name for this kind of material, linked to a blog post from May 2024 that introduces the term.

## Key Characteristics

- Refers to unwanted content produced by generative AI, as opposed to a neutral description of AI output.
- Introduced in May 2024 through a blog post, which the source links to.
- Appears in the source only as a blog entry title ("Slop"), so the source does not elaborate on the term.
- Part of the author's broader commentary on how generative AI output is affecting online content.

## Applications

- Labeling low-quality or unwanted AI-generated text, images, or other media online.
- Discussing how generative AI output shapes the quality of online content.
