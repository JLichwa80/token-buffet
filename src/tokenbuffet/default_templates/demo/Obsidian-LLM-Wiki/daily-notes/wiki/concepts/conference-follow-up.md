# conference follow-up

## Definition

Conference follow-up is a personal technique for turning contacts made at a conference into concrete next steps. Instead of relying on a later message to re-establish contact, the person books a future conversation with each contact during the event, using a calendar. The technique addresses the common failure of never following up after meeting people.

## Key Characteristics

- Converts a vague intention to follow up into a scheduled, dated conversation.
- Happens on the spot, while the contact is still engaged, rather than afterward.
- Uses a calendar as the commitment mechanism.
- Adapted from a Y Combinator demo day practice.
- Produces a pipeline of lined-up conversations rather than a list of names.

## Applications

- After a conference, the author lined up conversations with newsrooms of varying sizes following [[NICAR]].
- Useful for anyone who meets many prospects, sources, or collaborators at events and tends to lose the thread afterward.
