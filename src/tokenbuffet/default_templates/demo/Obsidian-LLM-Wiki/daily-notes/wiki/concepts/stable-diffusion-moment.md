# Stable Diffusion moment

> Stub created by the ingest candidate gate (prose+named) — [[sources/2023-03-22-weeknotes_1bbf6a]] names this without treating it. Will be filled by the next ingest of a source that does.

The 'Stable Diffusion moment' is the author's framing of a turning point in large language model development, in which open and accessible models begin to spread widely in the way Stable Diffusion did for image generation. Willison uses the phrase in the title of his March 11th post on large language models and again in the title of the first newsletter edition. The idea signals that LLMs are moving from closed, expensive systems to models that can be run and modified by individuals. It underpins the source's broader argument that AI development is accelerating. The phrase connects the blog's AI coverage with the newsletter's content.
