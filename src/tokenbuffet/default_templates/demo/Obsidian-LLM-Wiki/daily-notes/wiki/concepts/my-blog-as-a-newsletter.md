# My blog as a newsletter

## Definition

My blog as a newsletter is the practice of delivering a blog's posts to readers by email. The author implemented it as a Substack newsletter, reaching 640 subscribers by the time of the post. The newsletter is mostly blog content, with a short paragraph or two of added context about the week's themes.

## Key Characteristics

- Delivers existing blog posts by email rather than through a separate feed.
- Aimed at readers who do not use feed readers but do read email newsletters.
- Mostly reuses blog content, with a short editorial introduction on the week's themes.
- Published about once a week, and only when there is material to send.
- Earlier editions are linked from the original post.
- Substack has no API, so content is moved over with a semi-automated copy-and-paste workflow, supported by the [[blog-to-newsletter]] notebook.

## Applications

- Turning a regular blog into an email publication without changing the blog's main format.
- Reaching readers who prefer email over RSS or other feed readers.
- Using a weekly digest of posts, such as [[Weeknotes]], as newsletter content.
