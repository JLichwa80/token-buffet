# GPTs

## Definition

GPTs are customizable configurations of ChatGPT that OpenAI announced at its DevDay event. They let users create and share their own custom versions of ChatGPT.

## Key Characteristics

- Announced by [[OpenAI]] as the biggest news at [[OpenAI DevDay]]
- Allow users to build customized versions of ChatGPT
- Can be shared with other users
- Are the main DevDay topic that [[Simon Willison]] examines in depth
- Simon Willison spent a week exploring them and wrote about his findings in a separate post, titled to frame GPTs as "ChatGPT in a trench coat"

## Applications

- Creating a tailored ChatGPT for a specific task, audience, or domain
- Sharing a custom ChatGPT configuration with others
- Evaluating what GPTs actually are: a configured layer on top of ChatGPT rather than a fundamentally new model (the framing of Willison's post)
