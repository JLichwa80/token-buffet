# Imitation intelligence

## Definition

Imitation intelligence is the title of [[Simon Willison]]'s invited keynote at [[PyCon US 2024]]. The talk framed [[Large Language Models]] as systems that imitate intelligence rather than possess it, and argued that the Python community has a responsibility to help steer the technology in a positive direction.

## Key Characteristics

- Presented as an invited keynote at [[PyCon US 2024]]
- Summarizes the author's understanding of [[Large Language Models]] gathered over the preceding couple of years
- Centers on the social and ethical role of developers who work with AI
- Argues that the Python community is uniquely placed to influence the direction of the technology

## Applications

- Conference coverage that connects PyCon US 2024 to discussions of responsible AI development
- Framing for developers who want to consider the social impact of the tools they build with language models
- Context for the author's broader writing on language models
