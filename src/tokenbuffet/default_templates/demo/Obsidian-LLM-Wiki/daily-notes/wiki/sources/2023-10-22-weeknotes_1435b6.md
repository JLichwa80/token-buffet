## Core Content

Simon Willison's weeknotes from **22 October 2023** describe a slow two weeks for coding, mainly because of two conference appearances. He gave the talk "Embeddings: What they are and why they matter" at [[entities/pybay-2023|PyBay 2023]], covering [[concepts/word-embeddings|word embeddings]], and the closing keynote at the [[entities/ai-engineer-summit|AI Engineer Summit]]. Most of the post covers [[entities/datasette|Datasette]], which is being prepared for a 1.0 release. [[entities/alex-garcia|Alex Garcia]] is moving non-metadata settings out of the [[concepts/datasette-metadata|Datasette metadata]] system into a new datasette.yaml or datasette.json file. [[entities/cameron-yick|Cameron Yick]] contributed a [[concepts/javascript-plugin-api|JavaScript plugin API]] that adds custom panels and column menu actions, so that [[concepts/datasette-plugins|Datasette plugins]] can work together in the interface. The post also lists the datasette-llm-embed 0.2 and datasette 0.64.5 releases and a TIL on deploying the CLIP embedding model on Fly.

## Main Points

- **Slow coding period:** Two conference appearances, a talk at PyBay 2023 and the closing keynote at the AI Engineer Summit, reduced building and coding time.
- **Metadata cleanup:** Alex Garcia is moving permissions, allow blocks, canned queries and other non-metadata settings into a new datasette.yaml or datasette.json file ahead of Datasette 1.0.
- **JavaScript plugin API:** Cameron Yick's contribution adds custom panels above the table view, with tabbed switching when several plugins share the area, and extra options in the column action menu.
- **Remaining work:** The `datasette publish` command must be upgraded to deploy instances using the new configuration file before an alpha release can ship.
- **Releases:** datasette-llm-embed 0.2 and datasette 0.64.5 were released, and a TIL covered deploying the CLIP embedding model on Fly.
