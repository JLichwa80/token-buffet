## Core Content

In these weeknotes, [[entities/simon-willison|Simon Willison]] covers a busy period. He describes his first YouTube livestream, a live-coded session with [[entities/val-town|Val Town]] founder [[entities/steve-krouse|Steve Krouse]]. The pair built a [[concepts/rag|RAG]] system over his blog using SQLite [[concepts/full-text-search|full-text search]] rather than [[concepts/vector-search|vector search]], with [[entities/claude-3-5-sonnet|Claude 3.5 Sonnet]] as the language model. He also gave the opening keynote at the [[entities/ai-engineer-worlds-fair|AI Engineer World's Fair]] on short notice.

On the commercial side, [[entities/datasette-cloud|Datasette Cloud]] is leaving preview, and [[entities/stripe|Stripe]] billing is targeted for the end of July. Working with [[entities/alex|Alex]], he is finalising the [[concepts/json-api|JSON API]] planned for [[entities/datasette-1-0|Datasette 1.0]]. The post also covers blog upgrades and closes with the releases of [[entities/datasette|Datasette]] 0.64.8 and [[entities/llm-claude-3|llm-claude-3]] 0.4.

## Main Points

- **RAG demo:** Willison livestreamed a live-coded build of search-based RAG over his blog with Steve Krouse of Val Town.
- **Retrieval choice:** He prefers RAG built on SQLite FTS to embedding-based vector search, which he finds easier to reason about and cheaper to add to an existing site.
- **Keynote:** He gave the opening keynote at the AI Engineer World's Fair on short notice, after being asked at lunchtime two days earlier, and said he had about a day to prepare.
- **Billing:** Datasette Cloud is moving out of preview, with a target of starting billing via Stripe at the end of July 2024. Subscription management is being outsourced to Stripe's customer portal.
- **JSON API:** Work on the stable JSON API for Datasette 1.0 is ongoing. Alex is implementing the design documented in issue #2360, with plans to ship an alpha.
- **Model:** Claude 3.5 Sonnet, released the same day as the livestream, is described as Willison's favourite LLM. He also used its artifacts feature for prototyping and for a keynote visualization.
- **Releases:** The post lists datasette 0.64.8 and llm-claude-3 0.4, which adds support for Claude 3.5 Sonnet.
