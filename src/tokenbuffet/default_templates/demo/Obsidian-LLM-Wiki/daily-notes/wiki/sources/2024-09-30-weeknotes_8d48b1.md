## Core Content

Simon Willison's September 2024 weeknotes cover a busy month of travel, speaking, podcasts, and open source releases. He made two trips: a short visit to Lisbon for the [[entities/python-software-foundation|Python Software Foundation]] board retreat, and a trip to Durham, North Carolina for [[entities/djangocon-us-2024|DjangoCon US 2024]], where his talk was about plugin systems. In keeping with what he calls **conference-driven development**, he wrote and released [[entities/djp|DJP]], a [[concepts/plugin-system|plugin system]] for [[entities/django|Django]], in preparation for that talk. He also appeared on three podcasts: [[entities/the-software-misadventures-podcast|The Software Misadventures Podcast]], [[entities/twiml|TWIML]], and the inaugural episode of [[entities/pragmatic-engineer-podcast|Pragmatic Engineer Podcast]] hosted by [[entities/gergely-orosz|Gergely Orosz]]. All three involved [[concepts/using-llms-for-code|LLMs]] in some way. The post closes with a long list of blog posts and releases across [[entities/datasette|Datasette]], the [[entities/llm|llm]] command-line tool, shot-scraper, and many Django plugins. The post shows how his open source work, conference speaking, and media appearances fed into one another during the month.
