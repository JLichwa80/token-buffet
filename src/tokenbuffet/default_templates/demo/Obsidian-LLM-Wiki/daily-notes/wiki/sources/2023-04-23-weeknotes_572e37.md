## Core Content

This weeknotes post from Simon Willison's blog, dated 23 April 2023, covers a busy week of speaking and site work. Willison gave an online keynote, "Big Opportunities in Small Data", at [[entities/citus-con|citus-con]] on Tuesday, then flew to Salt Lake City for [[entities/pycon|pycon]], where he ran a three-hour workshop called "Data analysis with SQLite and Python". After a several-month hiatus, he added three new museums to [[entities/niche-museums|niche-museums]] and upgraded its photo galleries to use [[entities/photoswipe|photoswipe]]. That gallery work led him to write a TIL about [[concepts/exif-data|exif-data]]. The post also links to an analysis of the RedPajama-Data-1T training set ([[entities/redpajama-data-1t|redpajama-data-1t]]) and a demo of [[entities/web-llm|web-llm]] running [[entities/vicuna-7b|vicuna-7b]] in a browser, which reflects his interest in [[concepts/large-language-models|large-language-model]] work.
