# GPT-4o

## Description

GPT-4o is a large language model from OpenAI and one of the major new model releases of May 2024. According to the author, it costs half as much as GPT-4 Turbo, runs roughly twice as fast, and appears slightly more capable. The author reports strong results using it for structured data extraction through [[datasette-extract]]. Several of the author's releases supported it, including [[llm]] 0.14 and datasette-enrichments-gpt 0.5. It serves as a central example of the model-level developments the author reviews in the source post.
