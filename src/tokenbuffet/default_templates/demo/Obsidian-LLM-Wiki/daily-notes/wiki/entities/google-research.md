# Google Research

## Description

Google Research is the research division of Google. In the source, it is the originating party for a PDF paper titled "SQL Has Problems. We Can Fix Them: Pipe Syntax In SQL", which describes a new pipe syntax for SQL. Simon Willison ran that PDF through [[Gemini]] 1.5 Pro to produce an HTML version, which he called a pretty great initial result. Within six hours of his post, his HTML conversion of the paper ranked third on Google for the paper's title, and he then set it to noindex. The pipe syntax material itself is covered in [[Pipe Syntax in SQL]].
