# Newsroom Robots

## Description

Newsroom Robots is an AI-in-journalism podcast hosted by Nikita Roy. [[Simon Willison]] recorded an episode with her a couple of weeks before the blog post that referenced it, and the conversation was split into two parts. Part One covers [[OpenAI]]'s new features and the security risks of large language models, and it became the basis for two of Willison's blog entries. Part Two covers how [[Datasette]] helps with investigative reporting, and Willison considers it the best audio description of Datasette he has produced so far.
