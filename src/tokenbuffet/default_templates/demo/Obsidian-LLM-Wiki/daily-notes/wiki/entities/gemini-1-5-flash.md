# Gemini 1.5 Flash

## Description

Gemini 1.5 Flash is a large language model from Google, released in May 2024. The source describes it as inexpensive, with pricing close to [[Claude 3 Haiku]]. It ranked ninth on the LMSYS leaderboard, above Llama 3 70b, and supports up to one million tokens of context. It was added to the [[llm]] command-line tool through the [[llm-gemini]] plugin. It is one of the two main models covered in the source's May 2024 coverage, alongside [[GPT-4o]].
