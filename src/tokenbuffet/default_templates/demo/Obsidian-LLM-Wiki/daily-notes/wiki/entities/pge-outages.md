# pge-outages

## Description

pge-outages is a scraper project built by Simon Willison to capture power outage data from [[PG&E]], the utility serving the San Francisco Bay Area. Willison revisited an older PG&E outage scraper after severe storms hit the area in early February 2024. PG&E's outage map had changed and broken the original scraper a couple of years earlier. The new version was running in time to record outages during the storms. The project is listed under the miscellaneous projects section of the [[sources/2024-02-09-weeknotes_1bab97|February 2024 weeknotes]].
