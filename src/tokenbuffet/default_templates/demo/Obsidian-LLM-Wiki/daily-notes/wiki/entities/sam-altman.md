# Sam Altman

## Description

Sam Altman is the leader of [[OpenAI]], and the board of directors of the non-profit that controls OpenAI fired him in the week before the 2023-11-22 weeknotes were published. The board cited a vague accusation that he was not consistently candid in his communications with the board. Simon Willison describes the firing as the event that made the final days of that week "completely wild" for OpenAI. The situation was still unresolved four days later, and the post includes a note about the news article covering the dismissal. Willison's account of the firing is a secondary item in the post, which is mainly about his conference and Datasette work.
