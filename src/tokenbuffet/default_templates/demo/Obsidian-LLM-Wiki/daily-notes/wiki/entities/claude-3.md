# Claude 3

## Description

Claude 3 is the family of AI models from [[Anthropic]] that were new at the time of Simon Willison's 16 March 2024 weeknotes. Willison accessed them through the [[llm-claude-3]] plugin for his [[LLM]] command-line tool. He reports that the Opus model feels better than [[GPT-4]] for most tasks he has tried. He also says the Haiku model is slightly cheaper than [[GPT-3.5 Turbo]] and offers image input at the lowest price point he has seen. The post lists the Claude 3 models among the most exciting developments in its release notes.
