# Mixtral 8x22B

## Description

Mixtral 8x22B is Mistral's latest openly licensed model, released on April 9, 2024 as a 281GB magnet BitTorrent link. It is significantly larger than [[Mixtral 8x7B]], Mistral's previous best open model. When the author wrote about the release, no one had yet been seen running it, but the author expected it to perform extremely well given the quality of the original Mixtral. It was one of the three major LLM releases covered in the same 24-hour review as [[Google Gemini Pro 1.5]] and [[GPT-4 Turbo]], and it was released by [[Mistral]].
