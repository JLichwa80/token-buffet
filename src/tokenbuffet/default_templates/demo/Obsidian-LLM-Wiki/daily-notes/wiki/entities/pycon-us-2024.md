## Description

PyCon US 2024 was the annual Python community conference, held in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, in May 2024. Simon Willison attended and gave an invited keynote on the Saturday morning, titled "Imitation intelligence" ([[Imitation intelligence]]), which argued that the Python community can help steer [[Large Language Models]] in a positive direction. Willison called the conference really great and noted that the 2025 edition would be held in the same venue. He also said [[Datasette]] should take part in the conference sprints in future, since the project was now mature enough for that. The video of the talk had not been published when the post was written.
