# Half Moon Bay

## Description

Half Moon Bay is the coastal town that the author, Simon Willison, calls his hometown. It served as the test area for the [[Protomaps]] and [[PMTiles]] experiment, in which the author extracted a building-outline-level vector map covering the entire town into a 2MB file. The resulting map also includes business markers from Overture Maps and is published at simonw.github.io/hmb-map. It is the concrete example behind the claim that vector maps can be self-hosted, and it demonstrates that a custom subset of a larger Protomaps map can cover just one small area.
