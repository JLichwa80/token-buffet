# 123-reg

## Description

123-reg is a UK domain registrar, now a subsidiary of GoDaddy. Simon Willison had his simonwillison.net domain registered with it. After a missed renewal payment put the domain into parked mode, Willison regained control of the domain with the registrar's help. He credits the company's well-documented domain recovery policy and its support team for restoring control reasonably promptly. The only delay came from its UK-based account recovery team, which works in a different timezone from the author. Willison cites the episode as a cautionary example of domain management.
