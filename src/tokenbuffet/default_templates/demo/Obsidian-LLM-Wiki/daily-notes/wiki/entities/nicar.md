# NICAR

## Description

NICAR is a conference where data journalists gather. Simon Willison attended the 2024 edition, which he called fantastic. He and Alex ran a successful workshop on [[Datasette]] and [[Datasette Cloud]], and he gave a lightning talk demonstrating two new GPT-4 powered Datasette plugins, [[datasette-enrichments-gpt]] and [[datasette-extract]]. The conference was the main driver of his release schedule in the weeks before it, as he polished Datasette Cloud and released Datasette 1.0a12 and 1.0a13. His [[conference follow-up]] approach, booking calls with contacts on the spot, produced many conversations with newsrooms afterward. The weeknotes covering its aftermath are titled after the conference.

NICAR 2024 is the annual data journalism conference, held in Baltimore and hosted by Investigative Reporters and Editors (IRE).
Before the conference, Simon Willison planned to spend most of his time in the hallway track, talking informally with people about [[Datasette]], [[Datasette Cloud]], and how the Datasette ecosystem could best support their work.
