# Catching up on the weird world of LLMs

## Description

"Catching up on the weird world of LLMs" is a talk given as the closing keynote at [[North Bay Python]] on a Sunday in early August 2023. The talk summarizes the last few years of development in the field of [[Large Language Model|large language models]] in about 40 minutes. The author later published an annotated transcript of the talk, including slides, links, and notes. The source treats the talk as an example of the [[annotated-presentation-format]] the author has used for several talks.
