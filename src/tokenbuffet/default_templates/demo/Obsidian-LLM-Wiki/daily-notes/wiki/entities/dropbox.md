# Dropbox

## Description

Dropbox is a cloud storage company. In the AI trust crisis blog post, it serves as a cautionary example rather than a subject of analysis. The author says Dropbox learned the hard way that users are highly sensitive to uncertainty about whether their data is used to train a model. Its experience illustrates the trust problem the post discusses. The episode shapes the post's broader argument about [[AI trust crisis]].
