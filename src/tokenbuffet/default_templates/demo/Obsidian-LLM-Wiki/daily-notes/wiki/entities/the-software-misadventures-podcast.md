# The Software Misadventures Podcast

## Description

The Software Misadventures Podcast is a podcast on which Simon Willison appeared in September 2024. In that episode he talked about his career to date. It was one of three podcast appearances he made that month, and all three involved LLMs in some way. Willison published blog notes from the appearance on September 10, 2024. The episode is part of his September 2024 set of podcast engagements alongside [[TWIML]] and the [[Pragmatic Engineer Podcast]].
