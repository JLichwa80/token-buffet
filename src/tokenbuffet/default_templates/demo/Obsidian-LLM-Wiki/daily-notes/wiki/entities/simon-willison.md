# Simon Willison

## Description

Simon Willison is a software developer and the author of the weeknotes in [[sources/2024-07-02-weeknotes_55c009]]. He created [[Datasette]] and the llm-claude-3 plugin, and he runs the simonwillison.net blog. In this period he livestreamed a RAG build with [[Steve Krouse]] on [[Val Town]] and gave the opening keynote at the [[AI Engineer World's Fair]] on short notice. He also worked on billing and API design for [[Datasette Cloud]], made several blog upgrades including a 2003-style homepage and redesigned tag clouds, and reported his experience with [[Claude 3.5 Sonnet]] and his views on RAG versus embedding-based search.

He also built Datasette Enrichments, a plugin framework for running transformations or data fetches, such as geocoding addresses, executing a GPT prompt, or applying a regular expression, against selected rows, and wrote extensive documentation to help others build enrichments. In December 2023 he shipped sqlite-chronicle 0.2, reported that `INSERT OR REPLACE INTO` queries did not behave as he expected (see issue #7), and described the library as still pretty experimental. He also appeared on the Newsroom Robots podcast, and that appearance became the basis for two blog entries on prompt injection and the OpenAI board.
