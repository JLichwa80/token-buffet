# AI Engineer World's Fair

## Description

The AI Engineer World's Fair is a conference for AI engineering. [[Simon Willison]] gave its opening keynote on 27 June 2024, on short notice after the scheduled OpenAI speaker cancelled. He had also delivered the closing keynote at the event's previous edition in October 2023. His 2024 talk, "Open challenges for AI engineering," reviewed advances in the field over the previous eight months and posed new challenges to the audience. Willison considered the talk a success and reported strong feedback from attendees.
