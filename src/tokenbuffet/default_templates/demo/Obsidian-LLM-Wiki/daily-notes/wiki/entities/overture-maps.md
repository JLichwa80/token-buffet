# Overture Maps

> Stub created by the ingest candidate gate (prose+named) — [[sources/2023-10-30-weeknotes_d4a215]] names this without treating it. Will be filled by the next ingest of a source that does.

Overture Maps is an open map dataset that supplies business listing data used in the author's Half Moon Bay map. The author exported the places data from Overture Maps to SQLite and displayed the business markers on the custom vector map. The dataset is a concrete data resource rather than a concept, and it is referenced as a supporting data source for the self-hosted map project. Its role here is to supply the points of interest layered on top of the PMTiles basemap.
