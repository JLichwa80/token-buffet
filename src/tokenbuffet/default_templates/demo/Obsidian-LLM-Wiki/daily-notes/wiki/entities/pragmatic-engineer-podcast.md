# Pragmatic Engineer Podcast

## Description

The Pragmatic Engineer Podcast is a software engineering podcast hosted by [[Gergely Orosz]]. Its inaugural episode, published in September 2024, featured [[Simon Willison]] as the guest. The episode covered open source, the impact of AI tools on the software industry, and other topics relevant to modern engineering. Snippets from the conversation were shared on Twitter, including a discussion of RAG. Willison describes this appearance as one of three podcast appearances in his post, which is related to [[Using LLMs for code]].
