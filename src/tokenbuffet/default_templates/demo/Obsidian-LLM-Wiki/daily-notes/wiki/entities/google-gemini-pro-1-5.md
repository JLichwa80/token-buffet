# Google Gemini Pro 1.5

## Description

Google Gemini Pro 1.5 is a GPT-4 class large language model from Google, with a 1 million token context window. It reached general availability on April 9, 2024, with no waitlist, and according to the source it was available to anyone in 180 countries, though not in Europe or the UK as far as the author could tell. Its API includes a free tier of up to 50 requests a day, rate limited to 2 per minute, with paid pricing above that. The model gained audio input and system prompt support, which the author later used through the llm-gemini plugin. The author judged its pricing favorable compared with [[GPT-4 Turbo]] and [[Claude 3 Sonnet]].
