# Nous Research

## Description

Nous Research is an organization that released a preliminary report PDF about [[DisTro]], its technology for distributed training of LLMs over low-bandwidth connections. The report is referenced in the 2024-09-06 weeknotes, where Simon Willison describes it as a case of a research PDF being converted into a more accessible format. He ran a prompt using [[Gemini]] 1.5 Pro to convert the report into a Markdown version, which he published as a gist and which he says even handled tables. In the source, Nous Research is linked to the DisTro project through this report, and the Gemini-based conversion is the example of PDF-to-Markdown work that the post highlights.
