# AI Engineer Summit

## Description

The AI Engineer Summit is a conference where [[Simon Willison]] gave the closing keynote, held a couple of days after [[PyBay 2023]]. In the keynote he covered the summit itself and the previous year of AI developments. He published detailed slides and an annotated transcript for the talk, titled "Open questions for AI engineering." The event was one of the two conference appearances that slowed his work during the two weeks covered by the source post.
