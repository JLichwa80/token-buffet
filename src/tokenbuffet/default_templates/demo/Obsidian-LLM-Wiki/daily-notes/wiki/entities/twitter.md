# Twitter

## Description

Twitter is the social media platform that Simon Willison cites as the reason he started his [[Substack]] newsletter. He began the newsletter a few months before March 2023, when Twitter appeared likely to collapse under new management. The newsletter was a way to keep readers who do not use a feed reader and rely on Twitter for news. The source frames Twitter's instability as a motivation for diversifying his audience channels. Twitter is mentioned only as context for this decision and is not analyzed further. See [[My blog as a newsletter]] for the broader decision to publish through a newsletter.
