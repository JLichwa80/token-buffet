## Core Content
In this keynote address, [[eric|Jamie]], a former portfolio manager at [[fidelity|Fidelity]] now with Canby Capital, challenges the traditional wealth management approach to fixed income. He critiques the reflex to place high-net-worth investors into 100% home-state [[municipal-bond|municipal bonds]], showing that tax-exempt debt is subject to [[insensitive-demand|insensitive demand]] and inelastic supply. As a result, national munis or taxable alternatives like [[corporate-bond|corporate bonds]] often yield higher after-tax returns. While alternative debt like [[private-credit|private credit]] offers high headline yields, it carries distinct liquidity and risk profiles. Drawing an analogy to how [[direct-indexing|direct indexing]] transformed equities, the presentation argues that pooled vehicles fail to accommodate an individual's unique [[tax-persona|tax persona]]. Instead, scalable technology and [[systematized-customization|systematized customization]] enable [[separately-managed-account|separately managed accounts]] to continuously optimize across 1.5 million OTC bond opportunities, dismantling [[tax-trap|the tax trap]] to deliver after-tax alpha.

## Main Points
- **The Tax Trap in Practice:** Advisors routinely treat fixed income as a passive "set-it-and-forget-it" allocation, leaving significant after-tax alpha on the table.
- **Home-State Muni Fallacy:** Over a 25-year period, national municipal bonds outperformed Massachusetts municipal bonds on an after-tax basis for in-state residents about 53% of the time due to price distortions and inelastic local supply.
- **Taxable Debt Viability:** High-yielding taxable securities, such as corporate bonds and Treasuries, can deliver higher net yields than municipal debt even after factoring in full taxes.
- **Structural Inefficiency of Pooled Funds:** Mutual funds and ETFs cannot harvest capital losses for individual tax offsetting or adapt to a client's multi-layered tax profile.
- **OTC Market Alpha via Customization:** The fragmented 1.5 million CUSIP bond market rewards active search; algorithmic automation allows scalable SMA management tailored to each investor's specific tax persona.
