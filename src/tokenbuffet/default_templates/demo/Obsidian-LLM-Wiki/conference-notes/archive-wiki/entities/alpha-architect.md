## Description
Alpha Architect is an asset management firm specializing in quantitative investment strategies and ETF architectures. Led by a quantitative, PhD-heavy team, the firm acts as a sponsor and operational leader for Section 351 ETF exchanges, partnering with ETF Architect to hold a 42% market share in the space (representing 33 of 76 launches). The firm provides destination ETFs, such as AAUB, allowing financial advisors to transition appreciated, ossified client portfolios into diversified ETF positions without triggering immediate capital gains taxes. Alpha Architect supports both syndicated 351s (open to all advisors) and closed 351s (advisor-branded destination ETFs), backed by proprietary four-step software workflows that optimize portfolios for statutory diversification rules and facilitate custodian asset transfers.
