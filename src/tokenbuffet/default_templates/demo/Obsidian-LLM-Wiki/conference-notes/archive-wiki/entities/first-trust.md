## Description
First Trust is an asset management firm known for distributing exchange-traded products and unit investment trusts. Alongside [[vest]], the firm helped institutionalize options-based derivative strategies within open-ended registered investment companies under the Investment Company Act of 1940. By packaging derivatives inside 1940 Act fund vehicles, First Trust helped resolve compliance hurdles that historically limited financial advisors from utilizing options directly. This structural packaging facilitated scalable access to hedging and outcome-oriented strategies, contributing to the expansion of defined-outcome and buffer ETFs.
Furthermore, collaborative initiatives between First Trust and exchanges helped advance critical operational standards—including extended trading hours and in-kind derivative transfers—which subsequently drove substantial asset flows into buffer and defined-outcome fund categories.
