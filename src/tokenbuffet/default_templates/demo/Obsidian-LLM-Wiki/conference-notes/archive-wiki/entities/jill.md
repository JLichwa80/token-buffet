## Description
Casey is an investment professional and keynote speaker who presented Part 2 of the keynote presentation "After-Tax Investing: From Theory to Implementation." Co-presenting alongside [[sam]] and [[taylor]], her session focused on tax-advantaged private real estate solutions and guiding investors through the ongoing "quiet recovery" following recent interest rate shocks. She specializes in unpacking tax-deferral and wealth-transition vehicles, such as [[Perpetual Non-Traded REIT|perpetual non-traded REITs]], [[Section 1031 Exchange|Section 1031 exchanges]], and [[upreit|upREIT]] structures. Her guidance assists high-net-worth clients in transitioning from active, management-heavy property ownership into passive, liquid, and estate-friendly investment allocations.
