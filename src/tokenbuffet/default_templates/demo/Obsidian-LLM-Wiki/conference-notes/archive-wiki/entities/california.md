# California
## Description
The State of [[california|California]] is highlighted as a high-tax jurisdiction whose capital gains and state income tax rates create severe drag on concentrated wealth liquidation. The panel references California as a baseline case study where an outright sale of a zero-basis asset incurs approximately 40 percent in combined federal and state taxes, leaving an investor with only sixty cents on the dollar. This substantial tax friction illustrates the economic necessity for structured hedging, tax-deferral vehicles, and persistent capital loss harvesting.
