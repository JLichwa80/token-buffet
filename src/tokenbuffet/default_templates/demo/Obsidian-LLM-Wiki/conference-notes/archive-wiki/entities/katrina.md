## Description
Sam is a CPA and keynote speaker who delivered Part 1 of the keynote presentation "After-Tax Investing: From Theory to Implementation," focusing on foundational tax-aware public market strategies. She advocates for a taxes-first philosophy, arguing that taxes should not be treated as an afterthought or secondary wrapper, but rather integrated at the outset by adjusting capital market assumptions for tax drag at the asset class level. She champions the foundational role of municipal bonds as powerful tools for risk-averse, high-bracket investors and highlights preferred securities yielding Qualified Dividend Income (QDI) taxed at preferential 0–20% rates. Additionally, she shares a cautionary family narrative regarding her grandfather Herr Schmaltz and grandmother Riley holding a concentrated position in Wachovia stock to illustrate the risks of unmanaged concentrated wealth.
