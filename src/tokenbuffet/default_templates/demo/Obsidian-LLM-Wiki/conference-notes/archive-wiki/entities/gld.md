GLD (SPDR Gold Shares) is a commodity-backed exchange-traded fund holding physical gold bullion, cited as an example of an asset ineligible for contribution into an equity Section 351 exchange. Because commodity trusts possess distinct tax and legal structures, they cannot be exchanged tax-free alongside corporate equities into an ETF corporation. Its specific exclusion illustrates the regulatory boundaries governing non-stock holdings during tax-deferred transition operations. Understanding what cannot be deposited into a Section 351 ETF is essential for advisors auditing client portfolios prior to transfer.
> "Eligibility: Only U.S. persons (not corporations) can contribute exchange-traded stocks and ETFs. Mutual funds, crypto, commodities (GLD), government securities, and cash are not allowed." — [[wiki/sources/at this tax conference-keynote_-tax-aware-portfolio-transitions-and-derivative-etf-risks-s_682211]]
