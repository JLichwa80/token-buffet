## Description
The White House represents the executive branch of the United States federal government and plays a pivotal role in high-stakes negotiations over major federal tax legislation. During key tax policy debates, policymakers such as Senator [[Steve Daines]] brought research and macroeconomic data from the [[Tax Foundation]] directly to executive leadership to push for pro-growth tax policies. This direct engagement was instrumental in shifting executive discussions toward ensuring that vital economic provisions, such as full expensing and capital investment deductibility, were granted [[permanency|permanency]] in the final legislative compromise.
