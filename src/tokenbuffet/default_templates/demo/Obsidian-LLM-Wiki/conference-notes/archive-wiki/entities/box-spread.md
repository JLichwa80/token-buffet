## Description
A box spread is a delta-neutral four-legged options combination used to borrow or lend synthetic cash at fixed rates. It serves as an efficient method to obtain liquidity and generate capital losses when an equity downside hedge is unnecessary. In wealth planning, it is often evaluated alongside [[Variable Prepaid Forward]] structures as an alternative tool for raising liquidity. However, a box spread does not hedge single-stock downside tail risk or mitigate constructive sale issues on concentrated holdings.
