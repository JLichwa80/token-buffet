## Description
Cboe is a major financial derivatives exchange operator that facilitates trading in equity and index options. The exchange serves as a critical institutional collaborator with ETF sponsors like [[vest]] and [[First Trust]] to modernize derivative market infrastructure. To enhance the tax efficiency and liquidity of option-based funds, Cboe adapted its trading framework to permit in-kind options creation and redemption mechanisms. Additionally, Cboe extended options trading hours to 4:15 p.m. to align directly with standard equity ETF close procedures, resolving a key operational friction in portfolio transitions.
