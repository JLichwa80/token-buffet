## Description
Rob Arnott is an American investor, author, and the founder and chairman of Research Affiliates. A pioneer in quantitative investing and tax-aware strategies, he appeared as a featured guest speaker following Aaron's keynote presentation to offer a historical perspective on tax drag. Arnott reflected on his seminal 1993 study co-authored with Tad Jordanrey examining 25 years of mutual fund performance across 4,000 funds. His work decisively demonstrated that active management alpha is heavily eroded by taxes and management fees, resulting in net fund performance that significantly trails benchmark returns.
