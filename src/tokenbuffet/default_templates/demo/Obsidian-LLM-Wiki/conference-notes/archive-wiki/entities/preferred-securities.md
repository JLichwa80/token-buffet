## Description

Preferred securities are an often-overlooked component of tax-aware public-market portfolios, especially for risk-averse, high-tax-bracket clients. Sam emphasized that preferreds deserve attention alongside [[municipal-bond|municipal-bond]] because about half of the preferred securities universe produces [[Qualified Dividend Income]], which is taxed at lower 0-20% rates. A skilled manager can construct a preferreds portfolio that delivers a high percentage of its distribution as QDI, improving its [[Taxable Equivalent Yield]]. In this framework, preferred securities support the broader [[Taxes-First Philosophy]] by making tax characteristics part of portfolio construction from the beginning rather than treating taxes as an afterthought.
