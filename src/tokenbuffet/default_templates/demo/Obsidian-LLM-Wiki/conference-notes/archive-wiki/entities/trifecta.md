# Trifecta
## Description
Trifecta is an academic and practitioner research paper that develops core concepts in factor investing and quantitative portfolio construction. Authored or co-authored by the keynote speaker, the paper notably introduces the framework of [[fundamental-growth|Fundamental Growth]]. Within the presentation, it serves as foundational empirical literature demonstrating that selecting and weighting index constituents by actual accounting growth rather than standard valuation multiples improves portfolio outcomes. Alongside [[active-side-of-indexing|The Active Side of Indexing]], it forms a central pillar of the speaker's analytical toolkit for implementing [[fundamental-indexing|Fundamental Indexing]].
