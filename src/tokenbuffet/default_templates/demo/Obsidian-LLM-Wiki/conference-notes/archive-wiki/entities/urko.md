## Description
Taylor is a fintech leader, former [[brevan-howard]] executive, and keynote speaker who delivered Part 3 on integrated, tech-enabled implementation during the at this tax conference keynote on after-tax investing. His core philosophy is to meet clients where they are—frequently holding [[concentrated-wealth|concentrated stock]], illiquid assets, or proceeds from major liquidity events—and transition them toward an ideal target allocation in a tax-efficient manner. He introduces the technology layer developed at Brevan Howard and now incorporated into the [[nuveen]] as a whole-portfolio system that can be funded with any marginable asset, targets custom allocations, executes daily tax-loss harvesting, utilizes proprietary signals to avoid crowded trades, and automates transitions and after-tax reporting. For large capital gains, he deploys [[tax-aware-long-short-equity|tax-advantaged long-short strategies]] where short positions generate losses to offset gains from appreciated assets, calibrating leverage to dictate the speed of a [[tax-neutral-transitions|Tax-Neutral Transitions]] along a defined [[deleveraging-path]] (transitioning from temporary postures like 250/150 toward target mixes such as 115/15). In a personal case study following the sale of Brevan Howard, an aggressive [[275-long-275-short|275/275]] market-neutral portfolio deployed over five months neutralized 75% of his personal capital gains tax bill, prompting audible gasps from the audience. Within the three-part presentation structure, his implementation focus directly succeeds [[sam]]'s foundation on public market solutions and [[casey]]'s segment on private real estate vehicles.
