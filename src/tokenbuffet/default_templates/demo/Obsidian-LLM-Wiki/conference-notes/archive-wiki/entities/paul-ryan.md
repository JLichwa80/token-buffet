## Description
Paul Ryan is an American politician who served as the Speaker of the United States House of Representatives during the formulation and passage of the [[Tax Cuts and Jobs Act]]. During the legislative debate, Ryan reached out directly to the [[Tax Foundation]] to evaluate the macroeconomic and growth implications of implementing a temporary corporate tax rate cut. Informed by their analysis that temporary rate reductions provide no long-term incentive for capital formation or economic growth, he steered House leadership to prioritize and secure a permanent corporate tax rate cut in the final legislation.
