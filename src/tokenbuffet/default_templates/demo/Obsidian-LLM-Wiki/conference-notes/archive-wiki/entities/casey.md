# Casey

## Description

Casey is the second keynote speaker and discusses tax-advantaged private real estate strategies. She describes a recovery in private real estate and explains perpetual non-traded REITs, property exchanges, and a route from a Delaware Statutory Trust into a REIT. Her examples emphasize tax deferral, income, liquidity terms, and a possible shift from active property management to passive ownership. Casey extends the keynote’s tax-aware approach beyond public-market assets and is related to [[sam]] and [[taylor]].
