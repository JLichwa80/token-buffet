# Riley

## Description

Riley is identified in the source as Herr Schmaltz’s widow and the person who inherited or held the family’s concentrated Wachovia position. In 2006, she was reportedly offered a choice between selling and incurring a large capital gains tax or continuing to hold the stock. She chose to hold; the source says the stock later fell sharply and was sold for $7 during the 2008 crisis. Her story illustrates the human cost of lacking a tax-aware transition option.
