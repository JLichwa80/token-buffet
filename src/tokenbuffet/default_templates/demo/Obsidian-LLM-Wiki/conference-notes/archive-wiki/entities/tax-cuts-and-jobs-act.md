## Description
The Tax Cuts and Jobs Act (TCJA) is a landmark federal tax reform law enacted under the Trump administration that fundamentally altered United States corporate and individual income taxation. Shaped substantially by years of prior intellectual groundwork and economic modeling from the [[Tax Foundation]], the legislation served as a prominent real-world testbed for pro-growth tax policy. During legislative negotiations, then-Speaker of the House [[Paul Ryan]] consulted the Tax Foundation regarding the economic impact of corporate rate reductions; the Foundation's analysis demonstrating that temporary cuts provide zero long-term growth directly led to the establishment of a permanent corporate tax rate cut in the final bill. While the corporate rate reduction was made permanent, critical provisions regarding capital investment and [[R&D Expensing]] were enacted with sunset clauses, prompting subsequent multi-year advocacy campaigns to achieve full [[permanency]].
