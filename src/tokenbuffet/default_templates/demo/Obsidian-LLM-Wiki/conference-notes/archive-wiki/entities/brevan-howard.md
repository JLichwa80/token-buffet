## Description

Brevan Howard is an investment management entity whose proprietary quantitative tools and technological assets were integrated into the [[nuveen]] wealth platform to power customized, tax-neutral portfolio transitions. Its technology provides advanced capabilities that de-risk portfolios, defer or offset substantial tax bills, and systematically transition clients toward target asset allocations. The firm was also referenced by speaker [[taylor]] as the subject of a major corporate sale that generated a significant mid-year capital gain, which was subsequently managed using tech-enabled long-short strategies.

The tools now associated with Nuveen support tax-aware implementation through tax-loss harvesting, custom allocations, and after-tax reporting.
