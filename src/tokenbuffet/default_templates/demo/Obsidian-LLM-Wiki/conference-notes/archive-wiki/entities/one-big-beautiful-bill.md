## Description
"One Big, Beautiful Bill" refers to a comprehensive legislative vehicle and political negotiation framework used to package major federal tax provisions into a single sweeping measure. In the context of federal tax policy debates following the [[Tax Cuts and Jobs Act]], it represents a high-stakes legislative opportunity targeted by think tanks and advocates to secure structural, pro-growth policies such as [[permanency]] for [[R&D Expensing]] and capital investment deductions. The concept illustrates legislative leverage within omnibus negotiations, where individual lawmakers can condition their support for the broader package on key structural reforms. Notably, Senator [[Steve Daines]] leveraged this dynamic by taking his stance directly to the [[White House]], using research from the [[Tax Foundation]] to successfully ensure the Senate version of the bill permanently protected core investment provisions.
