# Sam

## Description

Sam is the keynote’s first speaker and presents foundational public-market approaches to after-tax investing. She argues that tax effects should be considered from the start of portfolio construction and highlights municipal bonds and preferred securities as tools for tax-sensitive investors. She shares a family story about her grandfather’s concentrated bank stock, later held by his widow [[riley]], illustrating the consequences of being left with only the choice to sell or hold. Her section sets up the later discussion of tax-neutral transitions.
