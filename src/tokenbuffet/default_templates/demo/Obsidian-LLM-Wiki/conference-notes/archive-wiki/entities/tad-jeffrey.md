Tad Jordanrey is an investment researcher and co-author alongside Rob Arnott of the seminal 1993 research paper titled 'Is Your Alpha Big Enough to Cover Its Taxes?'. Their collaboration examined decades of mutual fund performance data, establishing early quantitative benchmarks for how fees and taxes degrade pre-tax alpha. In the keynote, Jordanrey's work is recognized as having inadvertently helped spark the modern revolution in after-tax wealth management. His historical contributions highlight the enduring challenge of tax friction in active management.
