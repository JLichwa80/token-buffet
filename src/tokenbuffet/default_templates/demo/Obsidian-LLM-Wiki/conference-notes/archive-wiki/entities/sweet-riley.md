# Sweet Riley

## Description

Sweet Riley is the affectionate name used in the keynote’s retelling of Sam’s family story about her grandfather’s concentrated bank-stock holding. After her husband died, she chose to keep the appreciated shares rather than sell them and pay a large capital-gains tax. The stock later fell sharply during the 2008 crisis and was eventually sold for $7 per share. Her experience illustrates the personal stakes of concentrated wealth and motivates discussion of tax-aware transition strategies.
