In the keynote, Morningstar is cited as the source tracking the dataset of approximately 4,000 mutual funds analyzed over a 25-year period in Rob Arnott's 1993 paper on active fund tax drag. The data demonstrated that active mutual funds consistently lagged pre-tax benchmarks and deteriorated dramatically after taxes and liquidation.
