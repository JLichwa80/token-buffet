## Description
Steve Daines is a United States Senator representing Montana who served as a key legislative champion for pro-growth tax policies. Armed with economic research and macroeconomic modeling from the [[Tax Foundation]], he took a firm stand on major tax legislation known as the "one big, beautiful bill," refusing to support the package unless it secured permanency for pro-growth capital investment and [[R&D Expensing]]. Daines elevated this position directly to the [[White House]], successfully shifting the policy debate and ensuring that the Senate version of the legislation enacted permanent expensing provisions into law.
