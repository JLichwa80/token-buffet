Peter Lynch is a legendary investor and former mutual fund manager renowned for his tenure running Fidelity's Magellan Fund. In this presentation, he is quoted offering a striking observation to Jamie regarding the vastness and fragmentation of fixed income markets. Lynch compared evaluating debt to turning over 1.5 million rocks every day, highlighting that most market participants lack the operational capacity to examine every security.
