## Description
A Delaware Statutory Trust (DST) is described here as an intermediate vehicle in the [[upreit]] or 721 exchange pathway. In Casey’s framework, an investor can roll proceeds from a property sale into a DST, use that structure to move from active real estate management toward passive ownership, and defer taxes during the transition. The DST stage initially has a two-to-three-year hold period before the investor may move into a perpetual non-traded REIT. Once in the REIT, the investor may gain monthly liquidity of up to 2% of NAV and may be able to pass down operating units to heirs, creating an estate-planning advantage.
Legally recognized as a trust entity that holds fractional real estate ownership, a DST qualifies as eligible replacement property under IRS Section 1031. Following the initial hold period, these fractional DST interests are specifically rolled into operating partnership units of the perpetual non-traded REIT.
