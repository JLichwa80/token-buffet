## Description
Vanguard is referenced as the approving authority behind the dual share class structure pioneered by [[FM Investments]]. FM Investments describes itself as "first on the Vanguard-approved dual share class," a structure in which a single portfolio supports multiple wrappers—both a mutual fund and an ETF—that share the same NAV and the same investor rights. Vanguard's approval functions as the foundational validation that allows ETF trading efficiency to flow through to mutual fund buyers, with the firm framing it as the basis on which "the investor always wins." Vanguard's products in this context include Treasury ETFs spanning the yield curve ("T-Bill through thirty"), offering tax-efficient, high-trading-efficiency access. This marks the first Vanguard-approved/issued application of the dual share class concept.
