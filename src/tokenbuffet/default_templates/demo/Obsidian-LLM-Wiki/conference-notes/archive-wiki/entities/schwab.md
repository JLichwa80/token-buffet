## Description
Schwab is a major financial services firm, custodian, and asset manager that operates as the largest vendor of fundamental index strategies, managing over $100 billion in Research Affiliates Fundamental Index (RAFI) ETFs and mutual funds. In asset management and transition workflows, custodians like Schwab facilitate tax-efficient operations such as mapping over original tax lots with identical dates and cost bases under new ETF tickers during Section 351 exchanges. Schwab also serves as a key live vehicle provider for fundamental indexing through offerings like the Schwab Fundamental U.S. Large Company Index ETF (FNDX). Furthermore, empirical tax-alpha simulations co-conducted by Schwab and Research Affiliates across non-overlapping market cycles between 2008 and 2023 demonstrated approximately 170 basis points of tax alpha from the active trading side of cap-weighted US large-cap indexing and approximately 190 basis points for RAFI.
