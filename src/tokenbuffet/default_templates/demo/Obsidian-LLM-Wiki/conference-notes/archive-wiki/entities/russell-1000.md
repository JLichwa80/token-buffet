The Russell 1000 is a widely recognized benchmark index representing the large-cap segment of the U.S. equity universe. In the context of the simulations presented, it serves as the benchmark against which the portfolio archetypes maintain a market beta of approximately 1.0. For long-only direct indexing portfolios, managers target roughly 1% uninformed tracking error relative to this index for tax-loss harvesting purposes. In long-short variants, informed tracking error models deviate around this benchmark to harvest alpha and drive tax benefits.
