## Description
Jamie is a seasoned portfolio manager at [[canopy-capital]] and the keynote speaker of "The Tax Trap: What Everybody Gets Wrong About Bonds." He previously managed approximately $18 billion in fixed income assets at [[fidelity]], an experience that underpins his critique of the industry's legacy "buy-and-hold" and static home-state municipal bond strategies. Passionate about uncovering hidden bond market arbitrage, he highlights market inefficiencies across the 1.5 million CUSIP bond universe and a $40 trillion debt landscape. Jamie advocates for technology-enabled, systematized customization to deliver individualized, tax-optimized fixed income management at scale rather than treating bonds as an afterthought.
