The Millionaire Tax refers to high-income surtaxes enacted by specific states (such as Massachusetts) on top earners, which drastically increase marginal tax rates. In the presentation, it is highlighted as a primary factor defining a client's personalized tax persona and driving tax sensitivity. Although intended to incentivize buying local municipal bonds to shelter income, the speaker explains that the resulting surge in demand can distort prices. Consequently, even investors subject to these surtaxes may achieve higher net returns by purchasing out-of-state or taxable securities.
