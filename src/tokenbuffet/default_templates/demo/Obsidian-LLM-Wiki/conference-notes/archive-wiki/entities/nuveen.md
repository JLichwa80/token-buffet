## Description

Nuveen is an asset management firm that incorporates advanced, tax-advantaged investment solutions and proprietary technologies previously developed by Brevan Howard. Operating as a unified, tech-enabled "whole portfolio" platform, it provides integrated, dynamic, and personalized after-tax portfolio management for high-net-worth clients facing concentrated stock positions or major liquidity events. By combining automated transitions, direct indexing, and daily tax-loss harvesting with after-tax reporting, the platform enables customized, tax-neutral transitions designed to de-risk portfolios, defer or offset substantial tax bills, and systematically transition clients toward their ideal asset allocation.
