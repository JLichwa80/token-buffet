# T-Bill and TBMax
## Description
T-Bill and TBMax are [[FM Investments]] Treasury products used in the at this tax conference, 2026 presentation as the concrete worked example of the [[Dual Share Class]] concept. The two are plotted as a two-line chart that overlaps on total return, and the speaker uses that overlap to prove an equivalent investor experience across wrappers — one portfolio held in a mutual fund and in an ETF at the same NAV, cited as the first [[vanguard]]-approved dual share class. They sit within FM's Treasury ETF lineup spanning the yield curve, described as running "T-Bill through thirty," with tax-efficient, high-trading-efficiency access. In the talk's action items, the T-Bill/TBMax pair is named as a candidate holding when migrating clients to dual share class products. The same short-end Treasury exposure also reappears as the asset at the center of FM's [[tokenization|tokenized T-Bills]] proposal.
