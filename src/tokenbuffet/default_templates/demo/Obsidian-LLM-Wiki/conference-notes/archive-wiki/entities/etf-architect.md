# ETF Architect
## Description
ETF Architect is a white-label exchange-traded fund operational platform affiliated with [[alpha-architect|Alpha Architect]]. The organization provides the infrastructure, regulatory compliance, operational workflows, and custodial mechanics required to structure and launch independent and syndicated ETFs such as [[aaub|AAUB]]. Within industry transactions, the platform is recognized for executing 33 out of 76 total [[section-351-exchange|Section 351 Exchange]] transitions, facilitating tax-advantaged portfolio conversions. It actively coordinates institutional operations between registered investment advisors, custodian transfer agents, and ETF trust administrators.
