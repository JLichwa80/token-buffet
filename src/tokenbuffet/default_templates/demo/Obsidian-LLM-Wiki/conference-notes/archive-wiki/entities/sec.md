## Description
The SEC (Securities and Exchange Commission) is the U.S. securities regulator cited in the source as having "set up the rules where the investor always wins." Following a recent executive order, the SEC was pushed to immediately create tokenization schemes, forming the policy tailwind behind [[fm-investments]]'s tokenization push. The source frames SEC rulemaking as the arena within which FM engineers its tax and operational advantages, converting regulatory complexity into durable portfolio efficiencies. Acting on this momentum, FM was preparing to file a second application the day after the presentation. The firm's first [[dual-share-class]] concept was also noted as Vanguard-approved/issued, situating SEC-sanctioned wrapper structures at the center of FM's product strategy.
As the primary federal regulatory agency overseeing securities markets and fund distribution, the SEC enforces distribution rules under the Investment Company Act of 1940, defining the statutory framework that governs fund flows and mandates the operational mechanisms asset managers must navigate.
