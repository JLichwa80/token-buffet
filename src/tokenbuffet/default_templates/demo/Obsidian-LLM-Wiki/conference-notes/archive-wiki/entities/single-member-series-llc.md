## Description
A single-member series LLC is a flexible legal entity structure utilized to house tax-aware quantitative long-short allocations for individual taxable investors. It serves as an accessible vehicle that eliminates individual and partnership-style [[at-risk limitations]], enabling the efficient pass-through of realized capital losses. Within the [[ETS Playbook]] framework, the vehicle functions alongside [[Separately Managed Account|separately managed accounts (SMAs)]] to transform institutional-grade tax-management strategies into personal wealth engines. It provides investors with the optionality to fund accounts with cash or appreciated single-name securities, transitioning exposures tax-efficiently while generating persistent capital losses to shield gains.
Structurally, the vehicle establishes segregated liability protections for each individual series under a master entity umbrella. This architecture allows investors to run tailored investment portfolios with direct pass-through taxation for immediate loss utilization while sidestepping onerous partnership-level tax restrictions.
