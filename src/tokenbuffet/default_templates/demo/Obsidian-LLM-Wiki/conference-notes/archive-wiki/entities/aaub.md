## Description
AAUB is a low-cost (9.45 bps), large-cap, market-cap-weighted U.S. equity ETF operated by [[alpha-architect]] that serves as a primary "own-everything" receiving vehicle in tax-aware [[section-351-exchange]] transitions. Designed to replace fragmented, appreciated, and ossified taxable portfolios without triggering current tax liabilities, it operates alongside related sibling funds. Upon completion of the syndicated contribution window, custodians such as [[schwab]] execute [[tax-lot-mapping]], mapping original client tax lots directly over to AAUB shares with identical acquisition dates and cost basis.
