## Description
FM Investments is the asset-management firm whose representative delivers the presentation "Wrappers, Washington, and What's Next: Turning Tax Policy into Portfolio Power." The firm builds products that exploit the gap between Wall Street and Washington to create [[Tax Alpha]]—turning complicated, often temporary tax rules into enduring portfolio advantages. It positions itself in the "policy engine room," arguing that its DC proximity and a background in diplomacy and government give it an edge translating tax policy into product. FM pioneered the first [[vanguard]]-approved and issued [[Dual Share Class]] concept—bringing ETF trading efficiency to mutual fund buyers by letting mutual funds and ETFs share a single portfolio with the same NAV and same rights—and offers Treasury ETFs across the yield curve ("T-Bill through thirty"), illustrated by a two-line chart of [[t-bill]] and [[tbmax]] overlapping on total return to prove an equivalent investor experience. It is actively advancing [[Tokenized T-Bills|tokenization]], filing a second application with the [[sec]], and markets the [[cpmgcphy|cpmgcphy]] "income without income" strategy. FM's mission is to convert regulatory complexity into tax alpha through intelligent [[investment-wrappers]] and innovation so the "investor always wins."
The firm's tokenization strategy specifically targets collapsing operational costs, tightening bid-ask spreads, and unlocking 24/7 liquidity—all while preserving or improving tax outcomes. FM Investments focuses on long-term investors with 20–30 year investment horizons who seek sophisticated [[Tax Alpha]] strategies. The underlying mission addresses a world where tax policy is written in fragments, sunsets on scheduled dates, and gets implemented through millions of words of regulation—making intelligent [[investment-wrappers]] and innovation essential for investors to secure enduring advantage.
FM Investments describes the gap between Wall Street and Washington as a rich field of arbitrage, developing products designed to exploit that policy space.
The presentation connects the firm's strategy to 351/1031 rules, AMT, step-up basis, in-kind creation and redemption, proxy funds, and the "sawtooth pattern."
The presentation "Wrappers, Washington, and What's Next: Turning Tax Policy into Portfolio Power" is recorded in a note dated at this tax conference at 13:36, lasting 25 minutes and 14 seconds, and filed under Tax Management Conference.
The presentation pictures that gap as “230 miles and an infinite number of misunderstandings,” and frames the firm’s message with the catchphrases “Washington designed your portfolio,” “Harry Potter-length tax code,” “the spider’s web of acronyms,” “race to die,” “Cobra effect,” and “ETF not done yet.”
