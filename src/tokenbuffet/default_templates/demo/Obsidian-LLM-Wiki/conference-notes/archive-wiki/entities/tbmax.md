## Description
TBMax is a mutual fund share class issued by [[FM Investments]] as part of its dual share class Treasury strategy. Structured under the pioneering dual share class model originally popularized by [[vanguard]], it pairs directly with a sister Treasury ETF share class (such as [[t-bill]]) to share an identical underlying portfolio, net asset value (NAV), and shareholder rights. In empirical presentations, a two-line total return chart demonstrates overlapping performance between TBMax and its ETF counterpart, proving that mutual fund investors receive an equivalent experience while accessing ETF-grade trading and tax efficiencies. Financial advisors utilize products like TBMax to map and migrate existing client mutual fund holdings into dual share structures to harvest tax alpha.
