## Description
Vest is an asset management firm specializing in target outcome, buffered, and derivative ETF solutions. Partnering with [[first-trust]]] the firm played an instrumental role in popularizing defined-outcome strategies and solving structural friction points for derivative-based funds. Notably, Vest spearheaded operational innovations by collaborating directly with [[cboe]] to enable in-kind transfers of options contracts, preserving the structural tax efficiency of exchange-traded funds, while also advocating for extended market trading hours. By packaging complex option overlays and outcome-oriented hedging strategies inside 1940 Act mutual funds and ETFs, Vest helped financial advisors bypass traditional advisor-level options licensing and compliance hurdles.
