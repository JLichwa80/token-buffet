# Taylor

## Description

Taylor is the keynote’s third speaker and presents an integrated, technology-enabled approach to implementation. He describes a platform that accepts marginable assets, targets a custom allocation, harvests tax losses, and automates transitions and reporting. He explains how long-short strategies can help manage concentrated stock positions or gains from a liquidity event, while emphasizing the need for a planned path to reduce leverage. As a personal example, he says a leveraged portfolio offset 75% of his capital gains bill in five months.
