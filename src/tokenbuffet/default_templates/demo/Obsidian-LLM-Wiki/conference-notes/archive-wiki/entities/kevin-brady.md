## Description
Kevin Brady is a former United States Representative from Texas who served as Chairman of the House Committee on Ways and Means during the drafting and passage of the 2017 [[Tax Cuts and Jobs Act]]. In policy discussions surrounding federal tax reform, he highlighted the critical role of external analytical groundwork in shaping legislative strategy. Brady notably praised the [[Tax Foundation]], describing their data-driven macroeconomic analysis as the pivotal piece that enabled lawmakers to evaluate tax legislation through the lens of economic growth rather than short-term political messaging. His leadership emphasized grounding major tax policy debates in long-term capital formation and pro-growth principles.
