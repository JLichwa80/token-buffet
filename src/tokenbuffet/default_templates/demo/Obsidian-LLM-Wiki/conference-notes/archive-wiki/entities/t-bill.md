# T-Bill
## Description
T-Bill is the short end of the treasury [[etfs|ETF]] range [[FM Investments|FM Investments]] describes as running “T-Bill through thirty,” with tax-efficient access and high trading efficiency. In the at this tax conference, 2026 talk it is the worked example of a [[Dual Share Class|Dual Share Class]] pair with [[tbmax|TBMax]]: one portfolio in a [[Mutual Funds|mutual fund]] and an ETF, tied to the first [[vanguard|Vanguard]]-approved dual share class concept. A two-line chart of T-Bill and [[tbmax|TBMax]] is said to overlap on total return, and the speaker uses that overlap as proof that the two wrappers give investors an equivalent experience. The action items then tell advisors to map client holdings to available dual share products, naming the T-Bill/[[tbmax|TBMax]] pair, and to migrate where the pairing fits. The same short-term treasury exposure reappears as [[Tokenized T-Bills|Tokenized T-Bills]], a proposal to remove counterparties, improve NAV efficiency, and enable fractional spreads and 24/7 trading. In this talk T-Bill is both a live dual-share exhibit and the asset at the center of the firm’s next wrapper.
