## Description
The Tax Foundation is a non-partisan think tank that provides critical, data-driven tax analysis and macroeconomic modeling to policymakers who often lack the time and policy resources for deep expertise. The organization operates behind the scenes to lay intellectual groundwork for [[Pro-Growth Reform]], which it evaluates using macroeconomic models measuring policy impacts on [[Capital Formation]] and [[Labor Supply]]. During the enactment of the [[Tax Cuts and Jobs Act]], the Foundation directly influenced legislative strategy when leaders like [[Paul Ryan]] and [[Kevin Brady]] relied on its modeling to demonstrate that temporary rate cuts yield no long-term growth, prompting the push for a permanent corporate rate cut. In addition, the organization conducts multi-year education campaigns across both political parties to socialize high-impact tax priorities, including fighting for [[permanency]] on capital investment cost recovery and full R&D deductibility within proposals like the [[one-big-beautiful-bill|one-big-beautiful-bill]].
