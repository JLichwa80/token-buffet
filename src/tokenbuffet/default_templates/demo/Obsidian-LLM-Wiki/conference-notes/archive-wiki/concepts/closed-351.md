## Description
Closed 351s are bespoke Section 351 exchange transactions executed exclusively for a single wealth advisory firm to establish a proprietary, advisor-branded ETF for its own clients. Unlike syndicated exchanges that aggregate assets from multiple unrelated advisors, closed 351 structures cater to large practices possessing sufficient aggregate client volume to seed a private fund launch independently. Participating taxable clients contribute appreciated individual equity holdings and ETFs directly into the new fund in exchange for newly minted ETF shares without triggering immediate capital gains realization. This mechanism enables registered investment advisory firms to internalize customized asset management while resolving legacy tax friction and portfolio ossification across their client base.
