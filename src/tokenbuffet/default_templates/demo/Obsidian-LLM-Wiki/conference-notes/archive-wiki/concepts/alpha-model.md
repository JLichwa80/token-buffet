## Description
An alpha model is a quantitative forecasting framework used by investment managers to predict asset returns, evaluate securities, and construct active long and short positions. In tax-aware long-short investing, the alpha model serves as the primary engine driving informed tracking error (such as 2%, 4%, or 6% across 150/50, 200/100, and 250/150 portfolio archetypes) to maximize pretax return while deferring gains and realizing losses under portfolio constraints. Rather than relying on uninformed tracking error purely for tax-loss harvesting, manager skill expressed through the alpha model establishes high active risk and outperformance that continually unbalances portfolio leverage. This dynamic triggers continuous position creation, replenishing cost basis and establishing fresh opportunities for tax-loss harvesting across both long and short extensions.
