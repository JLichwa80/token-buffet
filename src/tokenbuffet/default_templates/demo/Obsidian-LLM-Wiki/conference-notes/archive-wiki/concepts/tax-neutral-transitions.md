# Tax-Neutral Transitions

## Description

Tax-neutral sell-down is a strategy for reducing an appreciated or concentrated position while offsetting the capital gains tax impact of the sale. In the at this tax conference, 2026 keynote on after-tax investing, it is presented as the alternative Sam wishes Riley had been offered instead of choosing between selling and paying a large tax bill or continuing to hold concentrated risk. The strategy is enabled by tax-advantaged long-short methods, where short positions can generate tax losses that offset gains from selling appreciated assets. It functions as a transition path that de-risks a portfolio, defers or offsets taxes, and helps move a client toward a more appropriate allocation. The keynote frames this as an example of how modern technology and platforms such as Taylor and the Brevan Howard/Nuveen platform can create better options for clients with concentrated wealth.
Technically, this execution relies on physical or synthetic tax-loss harvesting mechanisms—most notably long-short equity structures—to match realized capital gains dollar-for-dollar with harvested losses as assets are reallocated into a diversified target portfolio.

The transition can be paced over time through customized leverage and coordinated with the client’s broader portfolio plan.
