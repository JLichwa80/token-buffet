# After-Tax Wealth Accounting
> Stub created by the ingest candidate gate (aside+covered) — [[wiki/sources/at this tax conference-keynote_-tax-aware-long-short-investing-pretax-alpha-and-after-tax-_108d27]] names this without treating it. Will be filled by the next ingest of a source that does.
After-tax wealth accounting is an evaluation framework that sums terminal pretax portfolio wealth and the cumulative value of harvested tax benefits, compounding those benefits at the portfolio's pretax rate of return. Because tax savings realized from loss harvesting can be reinvested into the portfolio, they generate substantial compounding benefits across multi-year investment horizons. This metric serves as the primary output that a [[tax-engine|tax engine]] is designed to maximize. Traditional performance metrics frequently overlook the time value and compounding capacity of realized tax losses. In leveraged long-short strategies, superior manager skill accelerates both the pretax return and the tax benefit engine, multiplying terminal after-tax wealth relative to lower-skilled or long-only alternatives. In leveraged long-short portfolios over 15-year periods, the combination of compounding pretax returns and expanding tax alpha produces exponential wealth differentials—reaching up to 3x in 200/100 structures and 6x to 7x in 250/150 structures between low and high information ratios.
> "Definition: Pretax wealth plus the time-valued sum of tax benefits, compounded at the portfolio’s pretax rate." — [[wiki/sources/at this tax conference-keynote_-tax-aware-long-short-investing-pretax-alpha-and-after-tax-_108d27]]
