## Description
A Constructive Sale is a tax classification governed by IRC Section 1259, under which an investor who eliminates substantially all risk of loss and opportunity for gain on an appreciated financial position is treated as having executed an immediate taxable sale. Avoiding constructive sale treatment represents the first of the two major tax frictions when monetizing low-basis, concentrated wealth, alongside minimizing ongoing tax drag in the reinvested portfolio. Monetization strategies like Variable Prepaid Forwards circumvent constructive sale rules by utilizing collars with variable delivery formulas that preserve meaningful price exposure. Structuring hedges around these rules provides certainty, hedges volatility, and unlocks liquidity without triggering prohibitive immediate capital gains recognition.
