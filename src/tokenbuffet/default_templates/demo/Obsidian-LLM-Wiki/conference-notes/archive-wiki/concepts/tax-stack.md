## Description
The tax stack is an advisor's comprehensive, multi-layered operational and technological infrastructure designed to manage household-level after-tax wealth. Serving as an after-tax implementation platform analogous to an enterprise technology stack, it provides the missing execution layer that standard advisor technology stacks lack. It enables practitioners to diagnose tax drag, structure strategies around the holistic family balance sheet, and implement client-specific portfolios down to the tax-lot level. Furthermore, it integrates critical capabilities such as direct indexing, transition management, and governance, marking a foundational shift from product-focused delivery to institutional after-tax architecture.
