## Description

An upREIT, or 721 exchange, is a tax-advantaged real estate transition structure for investors with appreciated investment property. Casey describes it as a pathway for moving from direct, active property ownership into a more passive, professionally managed structure while retaining income and deferring capital gains taxes. The process can involve selling or contributing property into a [[Delaware Statutory Trust]], then eventually rolling into a [[Perpetual Non-Traded REIT]]. At the REIT stage, the structure may also provide liquidity and estate planning benefits. In the broader after-tax investing framework, the upREIT sits alongside [[section-1031-exchange|section-1031-exchange]] strategies and other [[Tax-Advantaged Private Real Estate]] tools for helping clients transition illiquid or highly appreciated assets more tax efficiently.
