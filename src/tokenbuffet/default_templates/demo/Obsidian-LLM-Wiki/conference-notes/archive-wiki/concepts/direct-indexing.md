## New Information (5-28 at this tax conference Keynote The Tax Trap in Bond Investing)

- Direct indexing is the established equity-side practice of owning portfolios security by security and managing them for each investor's taxes. Jamie of Canby Capital uses it as the benchmark and precedent for the fixed-income customization he argues bonds still lack, and the source treats it as an equity practice Canby itself does not run. (This source adds a concrete definitional role for direct indexing as the equity precedent for systematized after-tax ownership, which the existing page does not state.)
- The bond market is ripe for a technological and strategic revolution similar to what direct indexing brought to equities, in contrast to static fixed-income portfolios that lack the sophisticated tax management already common on the equity side. (The keynote's contrast between the equity direct-indexing shift and neglected bond portfolios is a new descriptive fact about why the practice matters.)
