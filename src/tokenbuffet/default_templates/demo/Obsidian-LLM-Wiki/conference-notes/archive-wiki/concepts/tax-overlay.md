## Description
A tax overlay is a portfolio management layer applied across active or passive investment strategies to optimize client-specific after-tax outcomes and control tax friction without compromising underlying investment discipline. Operating as an essential component within an after-tax implementation platform (the "tax stack"), it enables advisors to systematically coordinate tax-lot selection, harvest losses, and manage tracking error relative to target model weights. When added to active models, tax overlays generate meaningful tax alpha—yielding approximately 50 to 100 basis points in tax alpha for ETF and fund models. By automating execution across the household while allowing advisors to retain judgment over strategic levers, the overlay bridges active asset management with deliberate, engineered tax design to mitigate hidden tax drag.
