## Description
Protectionism is an economic policy regime utilizing tariffs, quotas, and trade barriers to shield domestic industries from foreign competition while generating federal revenue. Within contemporary fiscal debates, it represents one of the primary macro forces shaping legislative and tax priorities alongside partisanship and populism. Lawmakers increasingly lean on tariffs to address persistent budget shortfalls, elevating duties to historic levels. However, relying on protectionist trade barriers introduces significant economic distortions and supply chain inefficiencies that complicate long-term fiscal stability.
