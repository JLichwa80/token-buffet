## Description
The Single Counterparty Trap describes the acute execution and pricing vulnerability that occurs when a fund using over-the-counter (OTC) derivatives—such as swaps or structured products—relies exclusively on a single trading dealer. Without multi-dealer competitive tension to enforce fair bid-ask spreads and reasonable inventory pricing, the lone dealer has no economic incentive to provide competitive execution, leaving the strategy vulnerable to severe pricing extraction colloquially known on Wall Street as "ripping your face off." Fiduciaries and advisors vetting structured products or derivative-based strategies should demand transparency into dealer relationships and treat single-dealer architectures as an immediate red flag.
