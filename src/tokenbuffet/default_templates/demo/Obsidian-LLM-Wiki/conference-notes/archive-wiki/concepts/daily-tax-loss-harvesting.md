## Description
Daily tax-loss harvesting is an automated algorithmic process that monitors investment portfolios on a daily basis to identify and sell securities trading at a loss, immediately replacing them with correlated alternative assets while carefully navigating wash-sale restrictions. Rather than treating tax management as an episodic year-end task or an afterthought, this systematic strategy continuously accumulates capital loss reserves to offset realized gains throughout the year. Within the [[brevan-howardnuveen-platform|Brevan Howard/Nuveen platform]], daily tax-loss harvesting operates alongside proprietary stock-selection signals and risk models to power customized, tax-neutral portfolio transitions. This approach serves as a core technological pillar of integrated after-tax portfolio management, enabling advisors to de-risk concentrated client positions and methodically reallocate capital without incurring massive tax drag.
