# Private Real Estate Recovery

## Definition

A market view presented in the keynote that private real estate is recovering from an interest-rate shock. Casey describes the repricing as an attractive entry point, while noting that the source does not specify the period covered or provide supporting performance data.

- The recovery is framed as following an interest-rate shock.
- The reported returns and entry-point characterization are not accompanied by underlying performance data in the source.
