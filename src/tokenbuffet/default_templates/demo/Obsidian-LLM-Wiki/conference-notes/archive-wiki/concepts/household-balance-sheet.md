## Description
The household balance sheet represents the holistic, multi-account view of a family's complete assets, liabilities, and legal planning structures across taxable accounts, trusts, retirement accounts, 529 plans, donor-advised funds (DAFs), business interests, and private stock. Rather than treating portfolios as isolated pre-tax products or standardized models, wealth managers use this overarching perspective to diagnose multi-layered tax drag and coordinate asset location, risk budgets, gain budgets, and transition paths simultaneously. Managing exposures across all linked accounts and tax statuses enables advisors to design client-specific implementations at the tax-lot level, aligning portfolio construction directly with family-level after-tax outcomes.
