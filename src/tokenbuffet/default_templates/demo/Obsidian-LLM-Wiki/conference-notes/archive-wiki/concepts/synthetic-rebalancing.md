## Description
Synthetic Rebalancing is a tax-aware portfolio transition strategy designed for wealth holders with concentrated, highly appreciated stock positions, such as pre-IPO shares. Under this structure, an investor establishes an options collar around the concentrated equity holding to mitigate downside exposure while executing synthetic borrowing against the position at favorable institutional financing rates. The borrowed liquidity is then immediately deployed into a diversified multi-asset portfolio, achieving broad risk reduction and asset reallocation while deferring the capital gains tax realization on the underlying shares.
