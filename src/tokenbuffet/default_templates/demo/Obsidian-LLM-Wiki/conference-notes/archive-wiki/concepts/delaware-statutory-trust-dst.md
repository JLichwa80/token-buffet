# Delaware Statutory Trust (DST)

## Definition

A Delaware Statutory Trust (DST) is an intermediate real estate investment structure in the upREIT path. In the described strategy, an investor can roll property-sale proceeds into a DST and later into a perpetual non-traded REIT.

## Key Characteristics

- The initial DST investment is described as having a two-to-three-year hold.
- It can support a transition from active property management toward passive ownership while retaining income and deferring taxes.

## Applications

An investor may use a DST as an intermediate step after selling property, before moving into a perpetual non-traded REIT through an upREIT (721 Exchange) strategy.
