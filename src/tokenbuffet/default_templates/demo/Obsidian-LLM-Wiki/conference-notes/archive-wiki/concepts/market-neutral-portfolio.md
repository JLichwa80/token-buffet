# Market-Neutral Portfolio

## Definition

A market-neutral portfolio is a long-short investment structure designed to balance long and short exposure. In Taylor’s example, it used 275 of long positions and 275 of short positions, with leverage used to accelerate efforts to offset a large capital-gains tax bill.

## Key Characteristics

- Taylor established the portfolio after his company was sold, when he faced a large capital gain and had five months left in the year.
- The source says the strategy offset 75% of his capital-gains bill during that period.
- The example illustrates a potential tax-offset application of leverage; the source provides no further risk or performance details.

## Applications

This example shows how a leveraged long-short portfolio may be used in an effort to offset capital gains within a limited time period.
