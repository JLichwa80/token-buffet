## Description
Transitions are roadmaps is an after-tax portfolio methodology that rejects simplistic, binary mandates—such as selling everything immediately and incurring massive upfront tax costs, or rigidly staging sales evenly over arbitrary timeframes. Instead, it treats portfolio migration as an engineered, multi-step journey designed around the client's family balance sheet, risk budgets, and gain budgets. Under this framework, the advisor acts as an after-tax architect, analyzing legacy holdings at the tax-lot level, benchmarking against target models, systematically harvesting available tax losses, and pairing those losses against realized gains over an agreed horizon. This structured, lot-level process systematically eliminates tax drag while methodically transitioning legacy assets toward an optimal destination portfolio.
