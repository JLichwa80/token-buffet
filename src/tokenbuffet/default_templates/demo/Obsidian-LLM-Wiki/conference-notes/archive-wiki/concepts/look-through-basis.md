## Description
Look-Through Basis is an assessment mechanism applied during a Section 351 exchange where an exchange-traded fund contributed to the corporate transaction is evaluated based on its underlying constituent securities and their respective portfolio weights, rather than treating the ETF ticker as a single concentrated asset. Under federal tax rules, this method ensures that contributed appreciated ETF positions can be tested against diversification constraints without violating portfolio concentration thresholds. By decomposing the fund into its constituent holdings, the look-through treatment allows advisors and investors to execute compliant, tax-deferred ETF-for-ETF swaps.
