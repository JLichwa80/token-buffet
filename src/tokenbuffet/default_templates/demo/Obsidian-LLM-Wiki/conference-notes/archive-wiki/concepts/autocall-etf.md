## Description
An Autocall ETF is an emerging financial product innovation that packages complex structured notes and autocallable payoff profiles into a transparent, liquid 1940 Act exchange-traded fund wrapper. Historically, structured notes have imposed heavy operational burdens, credit risk exposures, and administrative complexities on wealth management practices and advisors. By securitizing these outcome-oriented payouts inside an exchange-traded vehicle, fund sponsors seek to solve a significant operational headache while democratizing access and streamlining portfolio administration.
