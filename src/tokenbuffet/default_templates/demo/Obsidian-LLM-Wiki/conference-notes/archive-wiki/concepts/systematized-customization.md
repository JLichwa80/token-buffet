## Description
Systematized Customization is an operational and technological framework that leverages algorithms and proprietary software to execute bespoke, individualized fixed income portfolio management at institutional scale. Grounded in the principle that "if you can code it, you can customize it," this methodology replaces legacy, manual trading routines with automated mathematical optimization to solve distinct client portfolio problems simultaneously. Rather than forcing accounts into static "common denominator" models or single-product strategies, the system continuously evaluates vast market inventories against each client's specific tax persona, cash flow requirements, and risk tolerances. By actively optimizing across municipal, corporate, and treasury allocations, it unlocks after-tax alpha historically neglected in bond investing and enables advisors to manage thousands of unique portfolios efficiently.
