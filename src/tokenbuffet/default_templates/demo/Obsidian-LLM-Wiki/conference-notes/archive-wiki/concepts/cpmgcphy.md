## Description
CPMG (often paired with CPHY) is a specialized bond strategy developed by [[fm-investments|FM Investments]], engineered to generate high-yield fixed-income total returns while eliminating taxable ordinary income distributions for investors. By exploiting 1940 Act distribution mechanics and ETF accounting, the strategy captures daily NAV accrual and capital appreciation instead of paying cash yields. It operates via modern dividend avoidance, systematically rotating holdings out of bond ETFs into proxy funds such as AGG during a precise two-day window around ex-dividend dates and returning post-distribution. This process produces a characteristic sawtooth NAV pattern while preventing Form 1099 ordinary income distributions and automated dividend reinvestment plan (DRIP) events. By converting accrued yield into capital appreciation, the strategy allows long-term investors holding positions for at least 366 days to convert bond gains into long-term capital gains while harvesting tax alpha.
