# Carried interest

A share of profits earned by general partners of private equity, venture capital, and hedge funds, historically taxed at capital gains rates rather than ordinary income. The presentation characterizes political threats to eliminate or reform carried interest as a perennial nonstarter in Congress. Because of narrow congressional majorities, key swinging districts, and lobbying dynamics, sweeping changes to carried interest remain unlikely. Recognizing this political reality helps managers distinguish durable tax realities from political rhetoric.
