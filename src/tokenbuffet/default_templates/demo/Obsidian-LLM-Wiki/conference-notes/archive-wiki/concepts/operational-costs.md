# operational costs
## Definition
Operational costs are the administrative, settlement, processing, and infrastructure expenses associated with delivering and trading investment products.
## Key Characteristics
- Include administrative, settlement, processing, and infrastructure expenses.
- Can be reduced through product and infrastructure design, including tokenization.
- Lower costs may contribute to tighter spreads, improved access, and continuous liquidity.
- Support potential tax and portfolio advantages when integrated with efficient investment structures.
## Applications
Operational cost analysis helps evaluate the efficiency of investment products and trading systems. In tokenized markets, reducing these costs may improve execution, broaden investor access, support 24/7 liquidity, and increase ETF trading efficiency.
Within this framework, operational cost reduction is part of the mechanism by which financial innovation improves market structure and investor outcomes.
