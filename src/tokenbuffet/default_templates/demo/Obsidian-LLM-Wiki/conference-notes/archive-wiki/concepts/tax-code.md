## Description
The tax code is the large body of statutory and regulatory rules governing tax treatment and influencing portfolio construction. The presentation describes it as a “Harry Potter-length tax code,” with millions of words of regulation, including 7.7 million cited words that representatives now use ChatGPT to help parse for constituents. This complexity is an enduring feature of long-term investing, where tax policy is written in fragments, sunsets on schedules, and implemented through extensive regulation. It forms the policy foundation connecting , [[amt]], [[investment-wrappers]], and [[tax-alpha]] The gap between Wall Street and [[washington-state]] creates opportunities for [[fm-investments]] to use intelligent wrappers, dual share class structures, and tokenization to pursue tax alpha over a 20–30 year investment horizon.
The tax code is portrayed as an acronym-heavy design environment for tax-aware products, not merely as a compliance burden.
