## Description
Budgets, Not Gaps is an after-tax wealth management and advisory framework that replaces passive reactions to tracking error with explicit, client-negotiated constraints. Instead of treating deviations from benchmark models as gaps to be closed reactively, advisors collaborate proactively with clients and CPAs to define quantitative parameters across gain, risk, time, and liquidity budgets. Specifically, this structure establishes distinct limits for recognized capital gains, acceptable tracking error against target models, the duration of transition roadmaps, and explicit liquidity budgets to cover annual cash withdrawals. By framing portfolio transition trade-offs through intentional allocations rather than default responses, advisors eliminate friction while accommodating complex household balance sheets where the standard [[no-cash-flow-assumption]] fails.
