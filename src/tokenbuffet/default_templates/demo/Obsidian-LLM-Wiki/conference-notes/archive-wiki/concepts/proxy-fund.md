## Description
A Proxy Fund is an alternative, highly correlated exchange-traded fund or asset used as a temporary holding vehicle during ex-dividend windows in modern dividend-avoidance strategies. In strategies such as CPMG/CPHY, portfolio managers rotate capital from the primary income asset into the proxy fund for a brief window—typically two days around the ex-date—to avoid receiving taxable ordinary income distributions while maintaining continuous asset class exposure. Once the distribution event concludes, capital rotates back into the primary vehicle, preserving continuous NAV accrual at minimal roll cost and capturing price appreciation over dividend capture.
