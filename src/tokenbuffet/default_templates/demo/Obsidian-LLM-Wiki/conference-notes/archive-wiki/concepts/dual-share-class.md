## Description
Dual share class is a financial product structure pioneered by [[fm-investments|FM Investments]] that combines the trading efficiency and liquidity of exchange-traded funds (ETFs) with the tax advantages and operational characteristics of mutual funds. The structure works by offering two share classes of the same underlying portfolio: one share class trades on an exchange like an ETF, while another is offered as a traditional mutual fund, allowing different investor segments to access identical holdings with optimized tax and operational outcomes. The mechanism leverages in-kind creation and redemption processes to minimize taxable transactions and preserve step-up basis treatment, reducing the tax friction that typical mutual fund transactions introduce. By delivering ETF trading efficiency to mutual fund buyers, dual share class structures capture [[tax-alpha|tax alpha]] while maintaining continuous liquidity and removing the friction of traditional fund transfers. FM Investments is advancing this innovation further through [[tokenization|tokenization]] to collapse operational costs, tighten spreads, and unlock 24/7 liquidity—all while preserving or improving tax outcomes. This design allows retail and institutional investors to benefit from tax optimization without sacrificing market access or liquidity.
More broadly, a dual share class structure gives an investment vehicle multiple share classes with distinct trading, ownership, or operational characteristics. By combining the tax or operational behavior of different investment formats, it can support long-term portfolio efficiency.
As a wrapper innovation, the dual share class approach can improve investment implementation without requiring investors to abandon familiar mutual fund exposure.
