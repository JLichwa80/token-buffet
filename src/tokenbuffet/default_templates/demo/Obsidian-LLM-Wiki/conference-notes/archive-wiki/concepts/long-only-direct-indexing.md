## Description
Long-only direct indexing is a portfolio archetype that holds a direct basket of individual equities (typically 100% long and 0% short) designed to track a broad benchmark, maintaining a beta of approximately 1 to the [[Russell 1000]] with an uninformed tracking error around 1%. The portfolio's primary objective is to defer capital gains and harvest realized tax losses within tracking error constraints to enhance after-tax wealth. Over extended investment horizons, however, the accumulation of embedded gains causes these portfolios to ossify or lock up, progressively diminishing future tax-loss harvesting opportunities. Crucially, higher manager pretax alpha exacerbates this lock-up effect in long-only structures by inflating [[Built-in Gains]] without the benefit of position creation, which reduces available loss-harvesting potential and net tax benefits compared to tax-aware long-short strategies.
