## Description
Household-Level Diversification represents the statutory threshold criteria required for an investor's contributed assets to qualify under Section 351 tax-deferred rules. Qualification is assessed across the contributing household rather than across the collective fund level. To satisfy the requirements, the largest single contributed position must not exceed 25% of total contributed assets, and the combined top five positions cannot exceed 50%. Optimization software and automated tools frequently offer "push-button" reweighting to rebalance client portfolios to meet these strict 25%/50% limits prior to deposit.
