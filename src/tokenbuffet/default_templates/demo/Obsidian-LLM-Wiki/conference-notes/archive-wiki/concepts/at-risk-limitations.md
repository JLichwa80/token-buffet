## Description
At-risk limitations are tax provisions—most notably under IRC Section 465—that restrict an investor's ability to deduct tax losses from an investment or business activity to the capital amount they personally have at economic risk. Historically, structured investment vehicles and partnerships could run afoul of these rules or create bottlenecks that delay loss deductions. However, modern implementations of quantitative, tax-aware long-short strategies circumvent partnership at-risk bottlenecks by wrapping the execution within individual vehicles like [[Separately Managed Account|Separately Managed Accounts (SMAs)]] or [[Single-Member Series LLC|single-member series LLCs]]. By packaging institutional-style strategies into individual accounts without triggering at-risk limitations, taxable investors can immediately harvest and consume persistent capital losses to offset portfolio gains and monetize low-basis assets tax-efficiently.
