## Description
Oscillation Risk describes the structural vulnerability and asymmetric payoff profile inherent in fully covered call investment strategies during periods of market whipsawing. In volatile environments, a 100% covered call fund participates fully in market declines net of the modest option premium collected, yet has its upside potential capped when markets sharply rebound. This recurring "down one, up zero" dynamic exposes investors to hidden structural dangers, systematically impairing and eroding the fund's Net Asset Value (NAV) over successive volatility cycles. Financial advisors and investors are specifically cautioned against marketing gimmicks surrounding high-yield derivative-based ETFs that mask this destructive mechanism.
