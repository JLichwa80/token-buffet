## Description
A tax-free policy loan is a borrowing mechanism that enables policyholders to access cash surrender value from an insurance contract without triggering a taxable distribution, constructive sale, or surrender event. In institutional private placement life insurance (PPLI) structures, policy loans serve as a primary lifetime liquidity engine against "skinny," institutionally priced policies that carry no surrender charges. Because loan proceeds bypass current income recognition and can remain outstanding until ultimate settlement against the death benefit, they preserve continuous tax-sheltered compounding and eliminate ongoing tax drag across decades of accumulation.
