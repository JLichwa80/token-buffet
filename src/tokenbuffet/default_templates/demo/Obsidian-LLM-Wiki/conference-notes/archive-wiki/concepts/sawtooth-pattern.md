## Description
The Sawtooth Pattern describes the classic jagged price chart trajectory of dividend- or income-distributing funds, characterized by steady daily price increases from accrued income followed by sharp periodic drops on ex-dividend dates when payouts occur. In traditional fund wrappers, this distribution cycle creates regular taxable events for investors. Alternative portfolio structures and strategies like [[cpmgcphy|cpmgcphy]] contrast with this jagged pattern by converting distributions into a continuous, compounding upward slope, replacing the distribution drops with a smoothed [[Total Return]].
