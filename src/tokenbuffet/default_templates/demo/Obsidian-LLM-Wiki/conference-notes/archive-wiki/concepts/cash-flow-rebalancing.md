## Description
Cash-flow rebalancing is a portfolio implementation playbook where target asset allocations are maintained and adjusted primarily through regular cash contributions, withdrawals, and distributions rather than active trading. Directing incoming cash flows into underweight asset classes and funding redemptions from overweight positions minimizes the realization of taxable capital gains. This process is highlighted as a critical, highly repeatable operational lever for generating positive [[Tax alpha|tax alpha]], enabling investors to harvest [[Rebalancing alpha|rebalancing alpha]] without incurring punitive transaction taxes.
