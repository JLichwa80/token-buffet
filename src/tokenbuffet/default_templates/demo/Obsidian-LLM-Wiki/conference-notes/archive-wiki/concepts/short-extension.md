# Short Extension
## Definition
Short extension refers to the component of a long-short portfolio where short positions are established against collateralized assets to express negative views and fund additional long exposure. In tax-aware portfolio management, short extensions serve a structural dual role by generating pre-tax alpha and providing asymmetric tax-loss harvesting opportunities due to their negative correlation to the broader market.
Unlike long-only implementations where bearish insights can only be reflected by underweighting benchmark constituents, short extensions enable the unconstrained expression of negative alpha, unlocking active short views that remain inaccessible in long-only portfolios.
