## Description
Rebalancing alpha refers to the excess return generated through a disciplined, rules-based contrarian process that capitalizes on market mispricings and subsequent [[Mean Reversion]]. Rather than stemming purely from factor exposure, it is earned by trimming assets that experience unvalidated price surges and adding to assets undergoing price declines without fundamental deterioration. Both [[Fundamental Indexing]] and [[Equal weight]] strategies share this core return engine, representing the active trading mechanism embedded within non-cap-weighted index implementations. By systematically exploiting market overshooting and price noise over long horizons, this disciplined rebalancing captures incremental gains whenever market prices diverge from economic reality and later mean-revert.
