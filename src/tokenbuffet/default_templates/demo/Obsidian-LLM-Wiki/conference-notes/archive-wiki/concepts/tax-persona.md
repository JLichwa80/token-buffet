## Description
A Tax Persona represents an investor's precise, multi-layered financial fingerprint defined by their unique combination of federal, state, and local tax rates, including specific surtaxes such as the Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT) and millionaire taxes. In fixed income portfolio construction, security selection must be directly calibrated to this individualized profile rather than relying on generic tax brackets or one-size-fits-all assumptions. Traditional pooled investment vehicles and static strategies—such as reflexively allocating 100% to home-state municipal bonds—frequently fail because they cannot accommodate an investor's distinct tax reality. Modern technology and scalable customization now enable mass-customized, tax-optimized bond portfolios across municipal, corporate, and treasury securities that were historically accessible only to multi-billionaires.
