# State Tax Regime Sensitivity
> Stub created by the ingest candidate gate (aside+covered) — [[wiki/sources/at this tax conference-panel-discussion_-variable-prepaid-forwards-tax-aware-long-short-an_58d676]] names this without treating it. Will be filled by the next ingest of a source that does.
State tax regime sensitivity describes the principle that jurisdictional differences in state tax codes can dictate or disrupt the efficacy of tax-efficient wealth management strategies. The panel uses Washington State as a key case study, noting that its standalone capital gains tax does not conform to federal rules and disallows short-term loss offsetting against long-term gains. Consequently, loss-harvesting long-short strategies provide federal relief but fail to offset state capital gains liabilities in non-conforming jurisdictions.
