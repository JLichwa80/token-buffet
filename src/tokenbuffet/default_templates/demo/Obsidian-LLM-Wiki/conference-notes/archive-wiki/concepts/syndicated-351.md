## Description
A Syndicated 351 Exchange is an open, pooled transaction model where multiple independent financial advisory firms aggregate qualifying client accounts into a single public ETF launch. It provides a compliant, operationalized, "push-button" mechanism to transition appreciated, ossified client holdings into low-cost, diversified ETFs without triggering immediate capital gains taxes. Unlike closed 351 exchanges—which are private transactions executed exclusively for a single advisory firm's proprietary, branded ETF—syndicated structures pool assets across firms to achieve greater economies of scale, lower account minimums, and fixed contribution deadlines. Pioneered by Alpha Architect through products such as AAUB, this structure allows any qualified advisor to participate and concurrently resolve portfolio tax frictions across multiple client households.
