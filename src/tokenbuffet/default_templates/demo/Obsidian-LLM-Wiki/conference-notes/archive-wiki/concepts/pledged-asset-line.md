# Pledged Asset Line
> Stub created by the ingest candidate gate (prose+named) — [[wiki/sources/at this tax conference-keynote_-tax-aware-portfolio-transitions-and-derivative-etf-risks-s_682211]] names this without treating it. Will be filled by the next ingest of a source that does.
A Pledged Asset Line (PAL) is a traditional securities-backed lending facility enabling investors to borrow cash against the collateral value of their taxable brokerage accounts. The keynote positions synthetic borrowing via an option box spread as a superior institutional alternative to PALs for concentrated stock owners. While PALs incur ordinary variable interest rates, synthetic box borrowing allows borrowing costs to be realized as capital losses, yielding significantly lower after-tax financing rates.
> "Investigate using "synthetic borrowing" via an option box as an alternative to traditional Pledged Asset Lines for clients needing liquidity from concentrated positions." — [[wiki/sources/at this tax conference-keynote_-tax-aware-portfolio-transitions-and-derivative-etf-risks-s_682211]]
