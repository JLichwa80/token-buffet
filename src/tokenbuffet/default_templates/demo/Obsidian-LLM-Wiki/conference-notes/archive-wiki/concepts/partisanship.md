## Description
Partisanship in tax policy describes the governance dynamic where major tax legislation is enacted almost exclusively along strict party-line votes during periods of unified government. Rather than building durable bipartisan compromise, tax policy experiences dramatic volatility and swings depending on which party holds power. Under this environment, Republican platforms focus on eliminating property and income taxes, while Democratic platforms champion wealth taxes and high-earner surtaxes. Crucially, bipartisan consensus rarely acts as a mechanism for fiscal restraint, remaining largely confined to bipartisan agreements on deficit spending.
