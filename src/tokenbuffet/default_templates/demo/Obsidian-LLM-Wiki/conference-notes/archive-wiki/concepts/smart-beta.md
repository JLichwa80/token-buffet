## Description
Smart beta refers to rules-based, non-market-cap-weighted index strategies designed to systematically capture specific factor premia such as value, size, momentum, quality, low volatility, or fundamental metrics. Over a 25-year horizon, smart beta strategies in aggregate added approximately 120 basis points per year gross and roughly 50 basis points net of fees in live funded accounts. However, tax drag frequently eliminates these gains, leaving average factor strategies roughly 30 basis points per year behind the benchmark after taxes. An exception highlighted in the research is [[Fundamental Indexing]], which originated in 2004 and credited with sparking the smart beta movement, maintaining approximately 50 basis points of outperformance even post-liquidation and after all taxes.
