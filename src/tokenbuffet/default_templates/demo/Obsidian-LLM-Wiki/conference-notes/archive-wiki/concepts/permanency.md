## Description
Permanency is the legislative strategy and policy design of enacting tax provisions—particularly corporate rate cuts, research and development expensing, and full deductibility for capital investments—as indefinite law rather than measures subject to expiration or sunset clauses. Pro-growth economic modeling indicates that temporary tax relief fails to stimulate long-term growth because capital commitment requires durability and predictability. Organizations such as the [[Tax Foundation]] spearheaded multi-year educational campaigns across party lines, advising key lawmakers like former Speaker [[Paul Ryan]] during the formulation of the [[Tax Cuts and Jobs Act]] and Senator [[Steve Daines]] on subsequent legislative packages. These efforts demonstrated that permanent rate adjustments provide the highest economic return compared to ephemeral cuts, driving ongoing advocacy to eliminate statutory cliffs for foundational investment deductions.
