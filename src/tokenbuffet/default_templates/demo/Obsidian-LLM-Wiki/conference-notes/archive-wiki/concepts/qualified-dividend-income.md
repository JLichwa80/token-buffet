## Description

Qualified Dividend Income (QDI) is a tax-favored form of dividend income that may be taxed at lower rates than ordinary distributions. In Sam’s discussion of [[Preferred Securities]], about half of the preferred securities universe generates QDI, which the source describes as taxed at 0–20%. A skilled manager can construct a portfolio with a high share of QDI in its distributions, improving its after-tax income profile and making its [[Taxable Equivalent Yield]] attractive to tax-sensitive investors. This supports the broader use of [[Tax-Aware Public Market Solutions]] and a [[Taxes-First Philosophy]] in portfolio construction. Preferential treatment depends on IRS criteria, including holding-period requirements.
