## Description
The Grandmother Effect is an intuitive fiduciary standard of conduct used by advisors to evaluate novel and complex financial products by asking: "Would I put my grandmother in that fund?" If the answer is no, the product's risk profile is deemed too high for client adoption. This ethical benchmark prompts practitioners to look beyond headline distribution yields and closely scrutinize structural risks, counterparty concentrations, and tax equity. In practice, this standard directly guided the choice to operate taxable ETFs with equitable tax treatment rather than relying on aggressive Return of Capital accounting mechanisms.
