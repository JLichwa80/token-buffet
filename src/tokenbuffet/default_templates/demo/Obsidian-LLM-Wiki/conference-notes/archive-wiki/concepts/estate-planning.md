# Estate Planning

## Definition

Estate planning is the consideration of how assets may be passed to heirs. In the keynote, Casey presents the ability to pass operating units to heirs as a potential benefit for investors who reach the REIT stage. The source does not provide legal, tax, or inheritance details.

## Key Characteristics

- The stated benefit is tied to passing operating units to heirs.
- The keynote links this possibility to transitioning from directly or trust-held property into a perpetual non-traded REIT structure.
- The source presents this as an estate-planning advantage without describing how the transfer works.

## Applications

The concept is raised as a potential consideration when evaluating the transition from direct or trust-held real estate into a [[Perpetual Non-Traded REITs]] structure. [[Delaware Statutory Trust (DST)]] is also a related concept in the real estate structures discussed.
