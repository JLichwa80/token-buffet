## Description
The After-Tax Architect is a professional role framing for modern wealth advisors who orchestrate holistic household balance sheets rather than merely selecting pre-tax investment products or off-the-shelf models. Rather than operating in isolated silos, the advisor coordinates client behavior, CPAs, estate counsel, and legal counsel into a unified portfolio implementation design. This framework shifts advisor value away from commoditized asset allocation—which clients can largely replicate on their own through simple ETF portfolios—toward proactive tax drag diagnosis, multi-account coordination (encompassing taxable accounts, trusts, DAFs, IRAs, and business interests), customized transition roadmaps, and lot-level execution. Backed by institutional infrastructure and automation, the architect treats tax design as an ongoing client dialogue built on the core principle that the future of taxable wealth is engineered rather than defaulted.
