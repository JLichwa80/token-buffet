# Buffer Funds
## Definition
Buffer funds, commonly known as defined-outcome ETFs, are packaged investment vehicles that utilize options combinations to provide downside protection up to a specified percentage while capping upside potential over a set outcome period. Packaged inside a registered 1940 Act fund wrapper, they eliminate operational, legal, and compliance barriers that typically prevent financial advisors and retail investors from trading options directly.
