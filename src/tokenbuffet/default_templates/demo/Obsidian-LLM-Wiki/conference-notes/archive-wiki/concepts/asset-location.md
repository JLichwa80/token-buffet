## Description
Asset location is the deliberate practice of allocating specific asset classes and investment strategies across taxable, tax-deferred, and tax-exempt vehicles to maximize after-tax compounding and generate structural alpha. Highly tax-inefficient exposures—such as private credit, high-turnover trading strategies producing short-term capital gains, and public strategies subject to turbulent distributions—are relocated into tax-exempt structures like Private Placement Life Insurance (PPLI) to completely shield returns from ordinary income and short-term tax drag. Conversely, lower-turnover core equity or tax-aware long-short strategies remain in taxable accounts (such as SMAs or single-member series LLCs) to systematically generate capital losses that shield realized gains. Over multi-decade horizons, this structural segregation delivers significant compounding uplifts; for example, a $10M allocation growing at a 10% IRR over 30 years accumulates to approximately $125M inside a PPLI structure compared to ~$50M in a taxable account, unlocking an annualized ~335 bps IRR pickup while preserving lifetime liquidity via tax-free policy loans.
