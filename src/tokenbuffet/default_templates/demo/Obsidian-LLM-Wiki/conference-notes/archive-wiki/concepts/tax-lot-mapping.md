## Description
Tax Lot Mapping is an operational custodial process where the original purchase dates and cost bases of contributed securities are carried directly over to newly issued ETF shares following a [[Section 351 Exchange]]. When investors exchange appreciated holdings for ETF shares, participating custodians like [[schwab]] "map over" each original tax lot under the newly assigned ETF ticker (such as [[aaub]]), eliminating basis discrepancies and avoiding immediate tax recognition upon future liquidations. While asset managers and supporting firms facilitate the operational framework, the final determination to execute cost basis mapping rests with the individual custodian. This mechanism ensures seamless continuity of tax accounting records after clients receive [[ETF Shares as Receipts]] for their contributed property.
