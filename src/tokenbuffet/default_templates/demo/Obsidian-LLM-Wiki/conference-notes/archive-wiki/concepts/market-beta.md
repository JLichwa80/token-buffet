## Description
Market beta measures a portfolio's systematic sensitivity and co-movement relative to benchmark movements, typically targeted around 1.0 across both long-only direct indexing and extended long-short strategies (such as 150/50, 200/100, and 250/150 variants relative to the Russell 1000). In tax-aware portfolio analysis, broad market return alone scales both long assets and short liabilities proportionally, leaving net percentage leverage unchanged. Consequently, pure market beta does not trigger position creation or replenish tax cost basis on its own. Only active manager skill producing pretax alpha creates asymmetric value changes between longs and shorts that make portfolios under-levered, necessitating rebalancing and fresh position adds.
