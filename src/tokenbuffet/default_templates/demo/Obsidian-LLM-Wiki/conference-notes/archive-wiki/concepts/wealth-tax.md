## Description
A wealth tax is a direct levy imposed on the accumulated net wealth or assets of high-net-worth individuals, rather than on their annual realized income. In the context of contemporary fiscal debate, it is categorized alongside other populist, politically expedient proposals that challenge traditional capital formation incentives and risk shrinking the broader tax base. Driven by progressive political movements, prominent proponents like [[Bernie Sanders]] have championed wealth taxation at the national level, while parallel initiatives have gained traction at the state level through proposals in California and Washington state. Rather than fostering sustainable, pro-growth economic policy, these measures reflect broader political pressures and partisanship that complicate an already fragile fiscal outlook.
Although framed as a mechanism to address wealth inequality, critics emphasize that such taxes generate substantial economic distortions and encourage capital flight, further exacerbating the narrowing of the tax base.
