## Description
Position creation is a portfolio dynamic unique to tax-aware long-short strategies where generating successful pretax alpha causes the portfolio's net asset value (NAV) to grow faster than its gross exposure, leaving it under-levered relative to its target leverage ratio (such as 150/50, 200/100, or 250/150). Broad market appreciation moves long and short legs proportionally without altering overall leverage percentages, meaning only idiosyncratic alpha triggers this under-leverage dynamic. To restore target leverage during rebalancing, the manager must establish fresh long and short positions at current market prices, introducing new holdings at full cost basis. This constant replenishment of cost basis counteracts the accumulation of built-in gains, suppresses unrealized gains as a percentage of gross exposure, and generates continuous opportunities to harvest cumulative net capital losses. Consequently, position creation functions as one of three competing effects of pretax alpha—joining larger NAV against built-in gains—transforming alpha into an engine for long-term tax efficiency and after-tax wealth rather than locking up the portfolio.
