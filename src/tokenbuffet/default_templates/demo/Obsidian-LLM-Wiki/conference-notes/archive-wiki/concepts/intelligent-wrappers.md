# intelligent wrappers
## Definition
Intelligent wrappers are deliberately designed investment structures that combine legal, tax, and operational features to improve investor outcomes. They aim to preserve or improve tax results while supporting better trading, lower costs, tighter spreads, or greater liquidity.
Intelligent wrappers align investment access, tax treatment, trading efficiency, and operational execution.
Intelligent wrappers package exposure, trading, ownership, and tax treatment into a more efficient investment implementation.
## Key Characteristics
- Integrate tax planning with investment structure and operations.
- Address fragmented tax policy and regulatory complexity.
- Seek tax alpha through policy arbitrage and financial innovation.
- Can improve trading efficiency, liquidity, spreads, and total investor outcomes.
- May incorporate technologies such as [[tokenization]].
- Make complicated tax rules work in the investor's favor over a 20 to 30 year horizon despite fragmented policy, scheduled sunsets, and extensive regulations.
- Frame the gap between Wall Street innovation and Washington policy as an opportunity to improve after-tax portfolio outcomes.
- Aim to remain useful even as specific tax policies change or expire.
## Applications
Intelligent wrappers may be applied to tax-efficient investment products, [[ETF trading efficiency]], tokenized assets, and other structures that combine tax optimization with lower costs or improved liquidity.
FM Investments pioneered dual share class structures that combine ETF trading efficiency with mutual fund ownership.
Tokenized T-bills represent another example of wrapper innovation alongside dual share class structures.
