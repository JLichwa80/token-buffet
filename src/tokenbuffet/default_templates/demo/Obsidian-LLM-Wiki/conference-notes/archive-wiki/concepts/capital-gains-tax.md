# Capital Gains Tax

## Definition

A tax liability that may arise when an investor sells an asset for more than its cost basis.

## Key Characteristics

- Selling appreciated assets can create a significant tax bill.
- Potential tax liability can constrain investment decisions and portfolio changes.
- Strategies may seek to defer or offset the tax while moving toward a desired allocation.

## Applications

Capital-gains tax is a consideration when transitioning concentrated stock holdings or investment properties. It can influence whether an investor sells or retains an appreciated holding, as in Riley’s decision to keep a concentrated position.
