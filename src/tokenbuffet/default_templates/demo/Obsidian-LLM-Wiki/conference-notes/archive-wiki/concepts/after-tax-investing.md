## Description

After-tax investing is an overarching investment philosophy asserting that portfolio decisions, allocations, and quantitative optimizations must prioritize post-tax wealth accumulation over gross pretax performance. Rather than treating tax liabilities as a retrospective accounting consideration, this modern framework embeds tax drag directly into Capital Market Assumptions (CMAs) prior to optimization. It unifies public and private market structures, incorporating algorithmic tools and tax-aware transitions to minimize frictional costs across the entire portfolio lifecycle.

This approach is particularly suited for high-net-worth clients managing concentrated holdings, illiquid assets, or navigating the complexities of proceeds from major liquidity events, where tax efficiency can significantly compound long-term wealth outcomes.
