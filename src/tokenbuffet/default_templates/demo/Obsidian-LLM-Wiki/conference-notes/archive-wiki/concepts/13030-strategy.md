# 130/30 Strategy
## Definition
A 130/30 strategy is a leveraged long-short portfolio structure that maintains 130% long exposure and 30% short exposure, resulting in 100% net market beta while expanding gross exposure to 160%. Within tax-aware wealth management, it serves as an aggressive implementation to generate tax alpha by harvesting deductible capital losses from individual short positions.
## Key Characteristics
- **100% Net Market Beta**: Preserves full market participation without altering target equity beta.
- **160% Gross Exposure**: Expands the active risk budget and return distribution via leverage.
- **Accelerated Loss Generation**: Unlocks bilateral loss-harvesting capabilities across both long and short sleeves.
## Applications
- **Aggressive Tax-Aware Overlays**: Implemented for high-net-worth accounts facing substantial continuous realized capital gains from outside investments or business liquidity events.
- **Core Equity Replacement**: Utilized as an active, tax-advantaged alternative to standard long-only index replication.
