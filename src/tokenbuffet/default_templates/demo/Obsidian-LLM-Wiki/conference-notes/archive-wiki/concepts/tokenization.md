## Description
Tokenization is the conversion of financial assets or investment vehicles into digital tokens on blockchain or distributed ledger systems, representing a fundamental operational innovation in asset management. By eliminating traditional settlement, custody, and intermediary layers, tokenization collapses operational costs, tightens bid-ask spreads, and enables 24/7 market liquidity, while preserving critical tax advantages such as in-kind creation/redemption mechanisms and step-up basis treatment. FM Investments pioneered this approach with tokenized Treasury bills as a proof of concept, demonstrating how tokenized structures can reduce friction in fund creation, redemption, and trading while maintaining or improving tax outcomes. Tokenization represents the next frontier in [[tax-alpha|tax alpha]] capture by combining operational efficiency with preservation of tax-efficient wrappers, making sophisticated tax strategies accessible to a broader investor base at dramatically lower cost. The innovation sits at the intersection of blockchain technology and tax policy arbitrage, enabling [[investment-wrappers|fund wrappers]] to deliver both [[dual-share-class|dual share class]] trading efficiency and liquidity simultaneously.
