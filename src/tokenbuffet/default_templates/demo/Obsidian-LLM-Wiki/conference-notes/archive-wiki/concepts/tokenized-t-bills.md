## Description
Tokenized T-bills are Treasury-bill exposures represented or administered through tokenization technology. FM Investments presents them as a forward-looking product area intended to reduce counterparties and operational layers, improve NAV efficiency, tighten spreads, support fractional access, and enable 24/7 liquidity. The concept sits alongside ETFs, dual share class structures, in-kind creation and redemption, and other wrappers designed to preserve or improve tax outcomes. It reflects FM Investments’ broader strategy of using financial-infrastructure innovation to create tax-aware portfolio advantages.
Their importance comes from combining a familiar short-term government instrument with new settlement and distribution technology.
Specifically, tokenization allows markets to break the traditional penny spread, narrowing bid-ask spreads below one cent. By eliminating legacy clearinghouse layers, the structure directly compresses operational expense ratios and transaction friction.
By bypassing traditional clearing and custody intermediaries, tokenization collapses operational overhead from typical ETF levels of 3–6 basis points down to fractions of a basis point.
