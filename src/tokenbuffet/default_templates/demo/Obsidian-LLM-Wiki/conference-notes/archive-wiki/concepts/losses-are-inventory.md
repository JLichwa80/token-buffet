## Description
Losses as inventory is an investment philosophy and after-tax portfolio strategy that reframes realized tax deductions as valuable economic assets to be harvested, stored, and strategically deployed. Rather than treating market dips and volatility purely as downside risk, this framework treats them—alongside cross-sectional stock dispersion—as ongoing opportunities to systematically restock harvestable losses. In doing so, it elevates tax-loss harvesting from a mechanical year-end exercise into an active method of monetizing volatility within an advisor's overarching tax stack. The resulting inventory of harvested losses is subsequently deployed against embedded capital gains from legacy mutual funds, concentrated equity positions, planned business sales, and broader transition roadmaps to minimize overall tax drag.
