## Description
A Separately Managed Account (SMA) is an investment vehicle consisting of directly owned individual securities tailored to an individual investor's specific mandate and [[Tax Persona]]. In fixed income, SMAs have historically served as the vehicle of choice for ultra-high-net-worth investors and billionaires since the 1950s and 1960s, enabling bespoke credit screening, direct tax-loss harvesting, and cross-sector optimization across municipal, corporate, and Treasury bonds. While traditional SMA operations relied on manual, "common-denominator" bond allocations that constrained manager capacity and limited customization, algorithmic and coded search architectures now allow managers to scale capacity from hundreds to thousands of bespoke portfolios per professional. Operationally, SMAs serve as the preferred threshold vehicle for portfolios above roughly $350,000, where individualized tax management outweighs the pooled convenience of bond ETFs.
