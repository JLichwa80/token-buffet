## Description
Tax design is the proactive, engineered construction of an investment portfolio that treats taxation as an upfront architectural parameter rather than an ad-hoc, year-end afterthought. It marks the industry's shift away from generic, pre-tax product selection and one-size-fits-all models toward an integrated after-tax implementation platform structured around the entire household balance sheet. Under this paradigm, advisors engineer client-specific portfolios at the tax-lot level to minimize tax drag across complex holding structures—including concentrated private business interests, legacy mutual funds, trusts, DAFs, and retirement accounts. By coordinating risk parameters, asset location, loss harvesting, gain budgets, and transition trajectories with liquidity needs, tax design operates on the core principle that the future of taxable wealth is deliberately designed rather than defaulted.
