## Description
Tax drag is the difference between the pre-tax return of an asset or portfolio and the after-tax return the client actually experiences. Aaron treats it as a hidden cost that quietly erodes wealth when portfolios are managed without tax awareness. It is measured at the [[Capital Market Assumption]] level before any optimization by analyzing each asset class’s source of return (income versus capital appreciation) and its holding style (active versus passive). Aaron argues it is not one problem but many problems layered together: pooled-product distributions, cash lock-ins from appreciated positions, realized gains and losses, portfolio-risk changes from tax trades, and family-level outcomes, and it also appears through embedded gains, transition costs, cash-flow needs, concentration, and poor household coordination. [[Rob Arnott]]’s 1993 evidence supplies a historical scale for the same leak, with taxes helping push average mutual-fund results toward roughly minus 2.5 percent, and about minus 3 percent after liquidation. Diagnosing it inside a [[Tax Stack]] that designs around the family balance sheet and implements client-specific portfolios at the tax-lot level means inferring, realizing, and offsetting drag while keeping the client comfortable with the trade-offs, because clients live in after-tax outcomes and [[Tax Design]] is the deliberate work of minimizing that drag.
For high-net-worth investors, tax drag serves as one of two primary wealth-eroding frictions—contrasted with upfront realization penalties—by inflicting cumulative reinvestment losses from the annual taxation of realized capital gains, dividends, and interest income. These ongoing compounding losses can be systematically mitigated through advanced structural solutions such as [[private-placement-life-insurance|Private Placement Life Insurance (PPLI)]] and [[wiki/sources/at this tax conference-panel-discussion_-variable-prepaid-forwards-tax-aware-long-short-an_58d676|at this tax conference Panel Discussion: Variable Prepaid Forwards, Tax-Aware Long-Short, and PPLI for Concentrated Assets - Summary]] equity strategies.
