# tighten spreads
## Definition
Tightening spreads means reducing the difference between the prices at which an instrument can be bought and sold. Narrower spreads can improve trading efficiency and reduce the cost of entering or exiting positions.
## Key Characteristics
- Reduces bid-ask price differences.
- Lowers trading friction for investors.
- May improve market efficiency and liquidity.
- Is presented as a potential benefit of tokenization.
- Seeks market improvements without sacrificing tax outcomes.
