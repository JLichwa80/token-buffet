## Description
Cross-sectional dispersion is the degree of performance variation and divergence among individual constituent securities within a broader market index or asset class over a given time horizon. In tax-aware portfolio design and direct indexing, it serves as the mathematical foundation for continuous [[Tax-Loss Harvesting|tax-loss harvesting]] across market cycles. Even during flat or strong bull markets where headline benchmark indices post positive net gains, underlying single stocks experience downward volatility and negative price swings. By harvesting these individual decliners as capital loss inventory, advisors systematically monetize stock-level volatility without requiring broad market drawdowns or abandoning overall asset class exposure.
