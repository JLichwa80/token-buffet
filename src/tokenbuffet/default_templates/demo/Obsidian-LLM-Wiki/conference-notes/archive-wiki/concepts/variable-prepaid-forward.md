## Description
A Variable Prepaid Forward (VPF) is an integrated derivative contract that packages a put option, a call option, and an upfront cash advance secured by underlying appreciated shares into a single instrument for tax efficiency. Serving as a tax-efficient monetization tool, it allows holders of concentrated, low-basis assets to draw liquidity representing 80–90% of the position's market value upfront without triggering an immediate taxable event or constructive sale under IRC § 1259. Setting forward maturity dates across multiple future tax years grants investors time and optionality to orchestrate offsetting losses, bridging concentrated holdings into diversified strategies like [[wiki/sources/at this tax conference-panel-discussion_-variable-prepaid-forwards-tax-aware-long-short-an_58d676|at this tax conference Panel Discussion: Variable Prepaid Forwards, Tax-Aware Long-Short, and PPLI for Concentrated Assets - Summary]] and [[Private Placement Life Insurance]].
By establishing a collar with explicit floor and cap levels on future asset values, the structure eliminates early exercise risk and margin calls while locking in a predictable deferral window through final contract settlement.
