## Description
Private Placement Life Insurance (PPLI) is an institutionally priced, variable universal life insurance vehicle designed for accredited investors and qualified purchasers to shelter high-yielding or high-turnover investments from income taxes. Functioning as the tax-aware destination vehicle for monetized wealth, PPLI solves the “back half” of the tax problem by enabling tax-deferred compounding during the accumulation phase and tax-free wealth transfer at death. These "skinny" policies feature low institutional pricing with no surrender charges, ensuring liquidity terms directly mirror the underlying assets. During the policyholder's lifetime, non-MEC structures provide tax-free liquidity through policy loans against the cash value, effectively eliminating tax drag on tax-inefficient strategies such as private credit and active long/short trading.
