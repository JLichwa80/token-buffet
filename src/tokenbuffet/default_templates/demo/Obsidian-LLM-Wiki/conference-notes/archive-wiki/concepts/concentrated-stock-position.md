# Concentrated Stock Position

## Definition

A large holding in a single company’s stock that exposes an investor to company-specific risk and may carry substantial unrealized capital gains.

## Key Characteristics

- Selling may trigger a significant tax bill when the shares have appreciated.
- Holding the shares preserves exposure to a decline in that company’s stock.
- Investors may be emotionally attached to a concentrated position.

## Applications

This concept helps describe the tradeoff investors face when managing a large single-company holding, such as Riley’s inherited Wachovia shares. The keynote presents tax-neutral transitions as one approach to addressing the dilemma.
