# long-term tax-aware investing
## Definition
Long-term tax-aware investing is an investment orientation for investors with 20–30 year horizons that integrates tax considerations into portfolio design, product selection, and long-term implementation.
## Key Characteristics
- Assumes tax policy will remain fragmented, temporary, and operationally complex.
- Emphasizes durable advantages rather than short-term tax trades.
- Uses product wrappers and innovation to adapt across changing policy environments.
- Connects technical portfolio structures to long-term investment objectives.
- Treats the gap between Wall Street and Washington as a field of arbitrage that can convert temporary tax rules into enduring portfolio advantages.
## Applications
This approach can guide retirement planning, multidecade wealth accumulation, estate planning, and portfolio construction where tax outcomes materially affect long-term returns.
