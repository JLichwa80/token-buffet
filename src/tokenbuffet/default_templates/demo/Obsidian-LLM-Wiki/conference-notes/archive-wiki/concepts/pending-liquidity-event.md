## Description

A pending liquidity event refers to an anticipated monetization of concentrated wealth, such as the sale of a privately held business or an initial public offering (IPO). Rather than treating liquidity as an isolated product transaction, proactive tax design frames it as a household balance sheet planning opportunity. Advisors can deploy aggressive loss-generating strategies—such as long-only direct indexing, tax-loss harvesting portfolios, and tax-aware long-short strategies—well in advance of the transaction to build an inventory of capital losses. This accumulated inventory directly shields the imminent windfall gain from substantial tax drag, significantly improving after-tax outcomes for the client.
