## Description
In-kind creation and redemption is a fund-operational process in which securities or other underlying assets, rather than cash, are exchanged when fund shares are created or redeemed. In ETF creation, authorized participants deposit underlying assets directly into a fund in exchange for newly created shares, avoiding the need to liquidate those assets for cash. The presentation identifies in-kind creation/redemption as a key mechanism for tax-efficient investment structures, linking it to [[etfs|ETFs]], [[dual-share-class|dual share class]], [[tax-alpha|Tax Alpha]], [[step-up-basis|step-up basis]], and [[investment-wrappers|investment wrappers]]. This process supports structures designed to preserve or improve investor tax outcomes, including the ETF trading efficiency associated with [[fm-investments|FM Investments]]’ dual share class approach; the presentation names the process but does not provide a step-by-step implementation.
The panel discussion describes in-kind ETF creation as an operational bridge from native [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] and concentrated digital asset holdings into traditional finance through a [[crypto-etf-bridge|Crypto ETF Bridge]]. The source page presents this conversion as avoiding taxable realization events and making the resulting ETF shares eligible for institutional derivative monetization tools, including [[variable-prepaid-forward|Variable Prepaid Forwards]], alongside [[tax-aware-long-short-equity|tax-aware long-short strategies]], with the intended benefits of diversification, liquidity, and transitions across asset classes for investors with low-basis crypto assets while minimizing upfront tax drag; the supplied excerpts do not specify the transaction requirements or establish these tax and eligibility outcomes for every holder.
