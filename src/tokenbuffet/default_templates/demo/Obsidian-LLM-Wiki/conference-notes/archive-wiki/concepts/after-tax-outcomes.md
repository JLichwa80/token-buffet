## Description
After-tax outcomes are the final tax consequences produced by a portfolio structure or investment transaction. They are the practical measure of whether a strategy creates lasting investor value, so preservation or improvement of tax treatment is a requirement for wrapper innovation and tokenization. Operational gains such as tighter spreads, lower costs, and continuous liquidity are valuable only when they do not undermine those tax outcomes. FM Investments pioneered dual share class structures that give mutual fund buyers ETF trading efficiency and is advancing tokenization to enable 24/7 liquidity while preserving or improving tax results. Related strategies include tax alpha, step-up basis planning, and the use of 351/1031 provisions to turn complex and changing tax rules into durable portfolio advantages.
Fundamentally, this serves as an advisory framework dictating that investment performance and client wealth are measured by spendable, transferable, and usable capital rather than pre-tax benchmark returns.
