## Description
Pro-Growth Reform refers to a tax policy framework designed empirically to expand macroeconomic output by boosting long-term [[Capital Formation]] and increasing [[Labor Supply]]. Rather than focusing solely on nominal rate cuts or politically expedient carve-outs, this framework prioritizes structural measures such as the full expensing of [[R&D Expensing|research and development]] costs and accelerated depreciation for capital investments. Grounded in macroeconomic modeling, the approach emphasizes that statutory [[permanency]] is essential, as temporary tax reductions fail to provide the certainty businesses require to commit to sustained investment. Think tanks such as the [[Tax Foundation]] lay the intellectual groundwork for these policies, educating lawmakers during legislative efforts like the [[Tax Cuts and Jobs Act]] on high-yield, pro-growth mechanisms.
