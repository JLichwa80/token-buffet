# Regulatory Complexity
## Description
Regulatory complexity refers to the detailed administrative rules that implement and clarify [[tax policy]] after legislation is enacted. Long-term portfolio outcomes are shaped not only by statutes and the [[tax code]], but also by millions of words of regulation, fragmented rules, scheduled sunsets, and a spider's web of acronyms. This complexity makes regulatory interpretation and product design important parts of investing, especially for [[investment-wrappers]] and [[tax alpha]] strategies that seek to turn temporary tax rules into lasting portfolio advantages. [[FM Investments]] operates in this space by developing investment structures that respond to the gap between Wall Street and [[washington-state]].
