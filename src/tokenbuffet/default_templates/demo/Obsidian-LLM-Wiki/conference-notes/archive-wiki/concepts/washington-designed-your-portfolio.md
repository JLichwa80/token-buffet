## Description
“Washington designed your portfolio” is a catchphrase asserting that tax policy and regulation actively shape portfolio construction, investment wrappers, and investor behavior, rather than serving merely as background constraints. It captures the presentation’s “Wall Street vs. Washington is 230 miles and an infinite number of misunderstandings” framing, along with metaphors such as the “Harry Potter-length tax code,” “the spider’s web of acronyms,” “race to die,” the “Cobra effect,” and “ETF not done yet.” Tax rules are written in fragments, expire through scheduled sunsets, and are implemented through millions of words of regulation, creating policy-driven opportunities for arbitrage and tax alpha. [[FM Investments]] uses intelligent wrappers, including dual share class structures and tokenized Treasury bill concepts, to improve trading efficiency, reduce operational costs, tighten spreads, support continuous liquidity, and preserve or improve tax outcomes. The broader mission is to help investors compound for 20–30 years by turning complicated and often temporary policy rules into durable portfolio advantages.
