## Description
Integrated portfolio management treats tax optimization as a holistic, systematic process across equities, bonds, cash flows, public assets, private market vehicles, and advanced technology strategies rather than a set of isolated tactics. It starts with tax-adjusted portfolio construction, including asset-class tax drag, income versus appreciation, active versus passive implementation, and tools such as municipal bonds, preferred securities, private real estate structures, and 1031 exchanges. The source presents this unified approach as the true source of after-tax alpha because it can meet clients with concentrated positions, illiquid assets, or liquidity-event proceeds and transition them toward target allocations tax-efficiently. In the keynote framing, [[sam]] establishes the public-market foundation, [[casey]] adds the private real estate toolkit, and [[taylor]] connects both through technology-enabled implementation. The [[nuveen]] platform is described as operationalizing this framework through custom allocations, tax-loss harvesting, proprietary signals, transition automation, tax-neutral transitions, and after-tax reporting.
