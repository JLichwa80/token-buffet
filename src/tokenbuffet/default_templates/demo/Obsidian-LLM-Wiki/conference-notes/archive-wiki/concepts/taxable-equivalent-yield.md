## Description

Taxable Equivalent Yield is the pre-tax yield an investor must earn from a fully taxable investment to match the net after-tax return generated by a tax-advantaged instrument. In after-tax portfolio construction, it serves as a critical benchmark for evaluating foundational fixed-income solutions such as [[municipal-bond|municipal-bond]] and [[Preferred Securities]]. By actively tilting preferred allocations toward [[Qualified Dividend Income]] (QDI), which benefits from preferential 0–20% tax rates, advisors can produce high taxable equivalent yields. Furthermore, adding a 15–20% allocation of QDI-focused preferred securities into a municipal bond portfolio elevates the overall taxable equivalent yield without demanding additional credit or duration risk.
