## Description

The Qualified Business Income deduction is presented as a tax advantage used in private real estate strategies, especially [[perpetual-non-traded-reit|perpetual-non-traded-reit]]. In the source, investors can deduct 20% of dividends earned through QBI, making it one of the two major tax benefits associated with perpetual non-traded REIT structures. The keynote states that the deduction is now a permanent part of the tax code. Casey frames QBI as part of a broader private real estate tax-efficiency toolkit alongside [[Return of Capital]] and [[Section 1031 Exchange]] strategies.
