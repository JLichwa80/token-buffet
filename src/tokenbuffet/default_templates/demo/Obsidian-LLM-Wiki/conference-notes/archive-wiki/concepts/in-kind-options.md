## Description
In-Kind Options refer to an operational breakthrough that permits standardized options contracts to be transferred directly into and out of an ETF creation and redemption basket, analogous to the handling of traditional equity shares. Developed through collaboration between asset managers and [[cboe]], this mechanism prevents derivative-based ETFs from generating taxable distributions during options overlay rebalancing. By mirroring the tax-advantaged in-kind transfer process used for equities, this structural innovation significantly enhances the after-tax efficiency, scalability, and viability of 1940 Act buffer and defined-outcome funds.
