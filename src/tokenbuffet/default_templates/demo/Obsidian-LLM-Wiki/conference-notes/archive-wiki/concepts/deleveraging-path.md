## Description

The deleveraging path is the planned process for reducing leverage after an aggressive [[tax-aware-long-short-equity|Tax-Aware Long-Short Equity]] has been used to offset or defer a large capital gain. [[taylor]] emphasizes that advisors should discuss this path before implementation, because a client may begin in a high-leverage posture such as 250/150 but ultimately want to move toward a more traditional allocation such as 115/15. The concept connects the initial tax objective to the client’s long-term portfolio target, making the strategy a transition plan rather than a permanent allocation. In the keynote, this fits within a broader whole-portfolio approach in which technology-enabled tools from the [[brevan-howardnuveen-platform|Brevan Howard/Nuveen platform]] can customize tax-neutral transitions, de-risk concentrated or liquidity-event portfolios, and methodically move clients toward their ideal allocation.

The [[brevan-howardnuveen-platform|Brevan Howard/Nuveen platform]] models a multi-year deleveraging path to unwind the leverage and return the portfolio to a more traditional allocation such as 115/15. Taylor says this process should be modeled and discussed with the client before implementation.
