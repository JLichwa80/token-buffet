## Description

A Section 1031 Exchange is a capital gains deferral strategy for owners of highly appreciated investment real estate, especially when they want to move away from active property management. The keynote frames it as part of a broader after-tax investing toolkit that treats taxes as an input to portfolio construction rather than an afterthought. Casey emphasized that the strategy can apply to various investment properties, but not to a primary residence. In the private real estate pathway, a 1031 exchange can help transition clients from direct ownership toward more passive structures such as [[delaware-statutory-trust]]s and [[upreit]]s. The source also distinguishes related disaster-loss planning through a exchange.

A 1031 exchange can also transition an owner from a Delaware Statutory Trust to a perpetual non-traded REIT through an upREIT structure. This makes the exchange part of the private real estate toolkit for tax-aware transitions.
