# SEC

## Description

The SEC (Securities and Exchange Commission) is the U.S. securities regulator cited in the presentation as the body whose wrapper rules can leave the investor as the winner. The speaker points to a rare case in which SEC rules for a [[Dual Share Class|dual share class]] put mutual fund and ETF investors on one portfolio with the same NAV and the same rights. A recent executive order is described as having pushed the SEC and the [[CFTC|CFTC]] to create tokenization schemes immediately. [[FM Investments|FM Investments]] treats that push as a policy tailwind for [[Tokenized T-Bills|tokenized T-bills]]. In the talk, the SEC is tied both to the existing dual share class breakthrough, including the first [[Vanguard|Vanguard]]-approved structure, and to the next step of tokenized trading, fractional spreads, and 24/7 liquidity. The presentation does not cite a specific rule number.
