# Herr Schmaltz

## Description

Herr Schmaltz is [[entities/sam|Sam]]’s grandfather and a decorated World War II fighter pilot whose historical investment serves as the central case study illustrating concentrated equity risk. Over his lifetime, he accumulated a significant equity position in a local banking institution. Following extensive industry consolidation, this stake converted into a concentrated holding in [[entities/wachovia|Wachovia]]. Following his death, his widow [[entities/riley|Riley]] was presented with an advisor ultimatum offering only a fully taxable liquidation or maintaining the concentrated position, which subsequently suffered catastrophic losses. Within the presentation, Herr Schmaltz functions not as an active market practitioner, but as the foundational real-world case pattern underscoring the necessity of a [[concepts/tax-neutral-transition|Tax-Neutral Transition]].
