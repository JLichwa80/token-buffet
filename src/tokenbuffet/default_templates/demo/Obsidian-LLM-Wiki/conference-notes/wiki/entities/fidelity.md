# Fidelity

## Description

Fidelity is mentioned as the organization where [[Jamie]] previously worked as a fixed income professional and managed $18 billion. The keynote uses this experience to illustrate the bond market’s scale and its capacity for large managers. It also describes technology increasing the number of separately managed account portfolios one professional could manage, from approximately 400–500 to as many as 5,000. These figures are presented as examples from the speaker’s experience.
