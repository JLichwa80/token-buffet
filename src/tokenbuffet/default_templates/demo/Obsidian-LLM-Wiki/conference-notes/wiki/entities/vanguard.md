# Vanguard

## Description

Vanguard appears in this presentation as the incumbent whose approval marks the legitimacy of [[FM Investments]]’ dual share class design. The speaker claims [[FM Investments]] was first on a Vanguard-approved dual share class: one portfolio, the same NAV, and the same shareholder rights across mutual fund and ETF [[wrapper]]. That approval is used as proof that ETF trading efficiency can be delivered to mutual fund buyers without splitting the underlying portfolio. The same claim is tied to Treasury products along the yield curve, including the [[TBMax]] comparison that is offered as evidence of an equivalent investor experience. The source does not describe Vanguard’s own product lineup beyond this approval and issuance role. Vanguard therefore functions in the talk as the gatekeeper that made the dual-wrapper structure available to investors and as part of the case for [[Tax Alpha]].
