The Russell 1000 is a major benchmark index tracking the 1,000 largest publicly traded U.S. companies. In the presented keynote study, it serves as the benchmark against which both long-only direct indexing and long-short portfolio archetypes target a beta of approximately 1.0. Portfolios in the simulation evaluate their tracking error, pretax alpha, and tax-loss harvesting behavior relative to this benchmark.
