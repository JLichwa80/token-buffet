# Sam

## Description

Sam is a CPA and keynote speaker who presents the foundational section on after-tax investing, establishing public markets as the essential groundwork before transitioning into alternatives. She advocates for the strategic allocation of [[entities/municipal-bonds|Municipal Bonds]] for tax-sensitive, low-risk portfolios and highlights preferred securities as an overlooked asset class capable of generating [[concepts/qualified-dividend-income|Qualified Dividend Income]] taxed at preferential rates. Central to her investment framework is a taxes-first approach, asserting that [[concepts/tax-drag|Tax Drag]] must be evaluated at the capital market assumption phase prior to portfolio optimization. Sam frames the real-world consequence of limited portfolio options through the financial history of her grandfather [[entities/herr-schmaltz|Herr Schmaltz]], his concentrated stake in [[entities/wachovia|Wachovia]], and the subsequent 2008 capital losses experienced by his widow, [[entities/riley|Riley]]. Throughout the address alongside co-speakers [[entities/casey|Casey]] and [[entities/taylor|Taylor]], she emphasizes her identity as a pragmatic practitioner rather than a product marketer.
