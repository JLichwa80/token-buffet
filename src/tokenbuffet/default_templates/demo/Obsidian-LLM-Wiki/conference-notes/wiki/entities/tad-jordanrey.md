Tad Jordanrey is a co-author alongside Rob Arnott of the seminal 1993 research paper analyzing mutual fund after-tax returns. Their collaborative empirical work investigated 25 years of mutual fund performance across thousands of funds tracked by Morningstar. The findings demonstrated that fund returns were drastically diminished once fees, taxes, and final liquidations were factored in. Jordanrey's co-authored paper is recognized in the keynote as having helped ignite the ongoing revolution in tax-aware wealth management.
