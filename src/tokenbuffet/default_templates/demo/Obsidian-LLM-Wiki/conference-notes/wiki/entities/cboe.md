# Cboe

## Description

Cboe is a major options exchange that serves as critical market infrastructure enabling the scaling of derivative-based ETFs. In collaboration with partners such as [[entities/first-trust|First Trust]] and [[entities/vest|Vest]], Cboe facilitated the operational mechanism for the in-kind transfer of options for ETF creators and redeemers. This breakthrough allows derivative contracts to move in and out of funds similarly to traditional equities, substantially enhancing fund tax efficiency. Additionally, Cboe supported extending options trading hours until 4:15 p.m. to align options markets directly with equity ETF creation and redemption windows, resolving a key operational mismatch.
