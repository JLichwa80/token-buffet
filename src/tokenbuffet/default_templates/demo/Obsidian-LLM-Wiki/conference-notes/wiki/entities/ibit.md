# IBIT

## Description

IBIT is the iShares Bitcoin Trust, the spot Bitcoin ETF the panel used to show why a [[Variable Prepaid Forward]] matters when a very volatile position is monetized near year-end. The concrete case was a $10 million sale of IBIT on December 25, which would leave only a few days in the tax year to harvest offsetting losses. A variable prepaid forward instead pushes recognition into the following year, so a full year of [[Tax-Aware Long-Short]] losses can shield the gain. Once [[Bitcoin]] sits inside an ETF, it crosses the [[Crypto ETF Bridge]] and traditional tools such as variable prepaid forwards and long-short diversification become available to holders who previously sat outside that system. The room treated the December 25 case as the moment the variable prepaid forward runway became immediately practical, especially for assets the panel put near 100 volatility, and it fits the sequencing in the [[Laddered Diversification Framework]].
