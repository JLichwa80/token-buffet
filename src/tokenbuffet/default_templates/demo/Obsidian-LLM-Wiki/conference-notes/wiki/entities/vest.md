# Vest

## Description

Vest is an investment management firm known for packaging complex options strategies inside registered 1940 Act funds in partnership with [[entities/first-trust|First Trust]]. This fund packaging resolves key compliance barriers for financial advisors, allowing them to implement outcome-oriented portfolios and hedging strategies at scale because the client holds the registered fund rather than managing underlying options positions directly. Alongside [[entities/first-trust|First Trust]], Vest played a central role in collaborating with [[entities/cboe|Cboe]] on [[concepts/in-kind-options|in-kind options]] mechanisms and synchronizing options market closing times with ETF create-and-redeem cycles. These innovations helped propel the broader derivative-fund category from 58th in flows in 2018 into the top ten, positioning buffer funds for substantial long-term asset growth following renewed market demand for downside protection.
