Treasury bonds and bills are sovereign debt obligations issued by the United States Department of the Treasury to finance federal government operations. While exempt from state and local income taxes, Treasurys are fully subject to federal income taxation. Within the keynote's dynamic asset allocation framework, Treasurys represent high-quality taxable benchmarks that can yield superior net returns relative to municipal bonds during market dislocations or when municipal spreads compress.
