# Aaron

## Description

Aaron is a keynote speaker and wealth management practitioner who advocates for actively designing taxable wealth rather than defaulting clients into pre-tax portfolio models. He positions the financial advisor as an [[entities/aaron|after-tax architect]] responsible for orchestrating the [[concepts/household-balance-sheet|household balance sheet]] across the client's CPA, estate counsel, legal advisors, behavioral tendencies, and portfolio execution. In his framework, Aaron establishes the concept of the [[concepts/tax-stack|Tax Stack]], distinguishes between [[concepts/tax-drag|Tax Drag]] and deliberate [[concepts/tax-design|Tax Design]], and details core operational principles including the view that [[concepts/losses-are-inventory|Losses Are Inventory]]. He oversees investment and tax operations across roughly $33 billion and 16,000 accounts, supported by global overnight reconciliation workflows, to address complex client scenarios such as ossified portfolios, concentrated executive positions, liquidity events, municipal bond allocations, and tax-aware long-short strategies.
