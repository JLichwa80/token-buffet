Emerging Market Debt refers to fixed income debt instruments issued by developing countries or corporations within those nations. In the presentation, it is highlighted as an example of a hard-to-structure asset class where individual bond purchasing is impractical for standard accounts. Consequently, the speaker points to emerging market debt as a specific scenario where pooled vehicles like ETFs remain the preferred exposure mechanism.
