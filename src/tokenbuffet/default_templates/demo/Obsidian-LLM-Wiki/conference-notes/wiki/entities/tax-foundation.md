# Tax Foundation

## Description

The Tax Foundation is a nonpartisan think tank that provides macroeconomic modeling and analysis to lawmakers who lack the staff or time for specialized fiscal research. Its stated purpose is to lay the groundwork for [[concepts/pro-growth-reform|Pro-Growth Reform]] by evaluating policy impact on capital formation and labor supply. During the formulation of the [[entities/tax-cuts-and-jobs-act|Tax Cuts and Jobs Act]], its modeling demonstrated that temporary corporate rate cuts failed to generate long-term growth, a finding cited by former House Ways and Means Chairman [[entities/kevin-brady|Kevin Brady]] as pivotal in shifting policy focus toward [[concepts/permanency|Permanency]]. In subsequent legislative debates, Senator [[entities/steve-daines|Steve Daines]] leveraged Foundation research to advocate for permanent R&D and capital expensing provisions. The organization operates as a policy educator, socializing tax concepts years before major bills are drafted.

Specifically, during the [[entities/tax-cuts-and-jobs-act|TCJA]] negotiations, then-Speaker of the House [[entities/paul-ryan|Paul Ryan]] engaged the Foundation directly to evaluate the macroeconomic impact of a temporary corporate rate reduction. The Tax Foundation's modeling confirmed that temporary relief would not generate sustained expansion, prompting leadership to commit decisively to a permanent rate cut.
