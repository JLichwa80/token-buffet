# Variable Prepaid Forward

## Description

A variable prepaid forward is an integrated over-the-counter instrument that packages a put, a call, and a loan so a holder of appreciated stock or a crypto ETF can raise cash without a current taxable sale. Panelists treat it as the bridge inside the [[ETS Playbook]] from a concentrated, low-basis position to a tax-aware destination, including [[Tax-Aware Long-Short]] accounts and [[Private Placement Life Insurance]]. Proceeds are described at roughly 80 to 90 cents on the dollar, with no early-exercise risk, no margin calls, and unrestricted use of the cash. Duration can be set so gain recognition falls in a chosen later tax year, which matters near year-end and for assets with volatility near 100 percent, such as [[Bitcoin]], once they sit inside a [[Crypto ETF Bridge|crypto ETF]]. Layered contracts often settle on January 1 of successive years under the [[Laddered Diversification Framework]], and the structure is discussed as a way to manage [[Constructive Sale]] risk relative to a [[Box spread]] or a listed [[Collar]].
