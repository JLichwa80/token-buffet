# CUSIP

## Description

A CUSIP identifies a security, including an individual bond. The keynote estimates that the bond universe contains 1.5 million CUSIPs, illustrating why fixed income analysis involves a much larger inventory than choosing among a few thousand equity tickers. The speaker connects this scale to the bond market’s fragmented, over-the-counter structure and says searching across the universe can reveal bonds priced at better value. See also [[The Hunt and Find Market]].
