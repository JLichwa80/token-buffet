# Casey

## Description

Casey is a financial speaker and institutional practitioner who presents real estate as an essential component of tax-aware portfolio construction. In her private-markets address, she highlights a recovery marked by seven consecutive quarters of positive private real estate returns following the interest rate shock. She details how [[concepts/perpetual-non-traded-reit|perpetual non-traded REITs]] serve as strategic vehicles combining a 20 percent qualified business income (QBI) deduction with return-of-capital deferral derived from depreciation. Furthermore, Casey explains the mechanics of [[concepts/1031-exchange|Section 1031]] and 1033 exchanges alongside the [[concepts/upreit|upREIT]] (Section 721) pathway, which facilitates rolling appreciated properties through a Delaware Statutory Trust (DST) into a perpetual non-traded REIT. Her presentation emphasizes liquidity features—such as monthly liquidity capped at 2 percent of NAV following a 2–3 year DST hold—and estate planning benefits, including the transfer of operating units to heirs.
