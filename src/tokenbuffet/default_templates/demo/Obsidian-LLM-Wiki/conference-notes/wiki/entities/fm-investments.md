# FM Investments

## Description

[[entities/fm-investments|FM Investments]] is an investment management firm specializing in engineering tax-efficient fund wrappers and structural strategies designed to exploit policy gaps between federal regulations and financial markets. The firm leverages deep public policy and diplomatic expertise to translate complex regulatory frameworks into structural portfolio advantages such as [[concepts/tax-alpha|tax alpha]]. Notably, FM pioneered the first [[entities/vanguard|Vanguard]]-approved [[concepts/dual-share-class|dual share class]] structures outside of Vanguard's original patent, providing unified mutual fund and ETF access to identical investment portfolios. In addition, FM develops Treasury ETFs across the yield curve, manages strategies including [[concepts/cpmgcphy|CPMG/CPHY]], and actively pursues regulatory filings with the [[entities/sec|SEC]] for [[concepts/tokenized-t-bills|tokenized T-bills]] and related short-term Treasury tokenization schemes.
