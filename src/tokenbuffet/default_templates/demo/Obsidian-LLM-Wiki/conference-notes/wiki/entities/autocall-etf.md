# Autocall ETF

## Description

An **Autocall ETF** is an exchange-traded fund structure designed to package structured notes featuring autocallable payout profiles into a transparent, liquid 1940 Act investment vehicle. Emerging as an innovation in derivative-based ETFs, the wrapper is intended to resolve substantial operational friction that wealth advisors face when managing discrete over-the-counter (OTC) structured products. By wrapping autocall mechanisms inside an ETF, issuers provide centralized custody, daily pricing transparency, and enhanced liquidity for wealth management portfolios. Fund sponsors and structured outcome specialists such as [[entities/vest|Vest]] and [[entities/first-trust|First Trust]] are actively involved in developing and distributing these derivative-driven wrapper strategies.
