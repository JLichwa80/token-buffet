The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) is the revenue service of the United States federal government responsible for collecting taxes and administering the Internal Revenue Code. In the context of the keynote, the IRS sets the specific tax guidelines that govern bond discounts and capital gains, notably establishing the de minimis rule. Understanding and navigating IRS regulations is critical for fixed income managers to avoid unfavorable tax treatment such as ordinary income taxation on market discounts. Compliance with these rules determines whether bond management achieves tax avoidance or incurs unexpected tax penalties.
