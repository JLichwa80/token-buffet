# Private Credit

## Description

Private credit is presented as an alternative investment a client might compare with a fixed income SMA. The keynote uses a hypothetical 12% return offer to illustrate that yield alone does not capture the differences between private credit and traditional fixed income. The speaker compares the choice to skydiving versus flying commercially, suggesting the investments have different characteristics. In this source, private credit appears briefly as a client objection rather than as a detailed subject. Related entities include [[Canby Capital|Canby Capital]] and [[Jamie|Jamie]], and the comparison relates to [[Fixed Income|Fixed Income]].
