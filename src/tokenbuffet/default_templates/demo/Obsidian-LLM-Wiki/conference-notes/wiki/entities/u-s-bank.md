# U.S. Bank

## Description

U.S. Bank serves as the destination ETF custodian in the operational workflow of [[Section 351 Exchange|Section 351 exchanges]]. During a syndicated or closed 351 launch, securities contributed by investors transfer from retail custodians like [[Schwab]] to U.S. Bank two days prior to the ETF launch. This custody transfer represents the mechanical bridge converting client stock positions into newly minted shares of funds such as [[AAUB]]. Through this mechanism, U.S. Bank facilitates tax-deferred portfolio transitions while safeguarding underlying contributed collateral.
