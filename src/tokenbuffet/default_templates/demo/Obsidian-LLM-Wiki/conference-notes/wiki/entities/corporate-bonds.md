# Corporate bonds

## Description

Corporate bonds are taxable fixed income securities that may offer a better after-tax outcome than municipal bonds, depending on an investor’s tax rate and the prices of available municipal bonds. The keynote notes that scarce home-state municipal bonds in high-tax states can be priced high enough to affect that comparison. Investors should compare corporate and municipal bonds after accounting for their individual tax rates, as discussed in [[After-Tax Return]], [[Tax Persona]], and [[The Tax Trap]]. Corporate bonds are also among the securities active managers can evaluate when building customized portfolios.

Debt obligations issued by private and public corporations to finance ongoing operations, capital expenditures, or growth initiatives, corporate bonds generate interest payments that are generally subject to federal, state, and local taxation.
