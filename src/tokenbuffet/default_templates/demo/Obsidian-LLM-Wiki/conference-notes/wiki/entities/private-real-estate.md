# Private Real Estate

## Description

Private real estate encompasses non-publicly traded equity and debt investments in commercial, residential, and industrial real property. In market presentations, the asset class has been noted for entering a "quiet recovery," delivering multiple consecutive quarters of positive returns following macroeconomic interest rate adjustments. Beyond capital appreciation, it provides key tax advantages for investors, including depreciation pass-through and Qualified Business Income (QBI) deductions. Private real estate is frequently utilized by high-net-worth investors seeking steady passive income, capital gains tax deferral via mechanisms like [[1031 Exchange]], and structured estate planning solutions.

Additionally, it serves as an effective destination asset class for advanced tax-deferral structures, specifically [[concepts/upreit|Section 721 UPREIT exchanges]], while qualifying investors for Section 199A deductions alongside offering defensive return profiles during market shifts.
