# Massachusetts municipal bonds

## Description

Massachusetts municipal bonds are a home-state bond allocation that may appeal to Massachusetts residents seeking state tax advantages. The keynote challenges the assumption that they always outperform national municipal bonds after taxes. In a 25-year comparison cited by the keynote, national municipal bonds outperformed Massachusetts municipal bonds on an after-tax basis about 53% of the time. The source connects this result to constrained local bond supply and high demand from tax-sensitive investors, alongside [[Insensitive Demand]], [[The Tax Trap]], and [[After-Tax Return]].
