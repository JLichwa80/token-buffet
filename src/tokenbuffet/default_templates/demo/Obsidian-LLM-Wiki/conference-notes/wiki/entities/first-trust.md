# First Trust

## Description

First Trust is an asset management sponsor that packages options-based strategies inside 1940 Act funds alongside [[entities/vest|Vest]], enabling financial advisors to deploy hedges and defined-outcome exposures without directly managing options contracts. This fund structure directly addresses compliance constraints, which represent the primary operational friction preventing advisors from utilizing options strategies. In collaboration with [[entities/vest|Vest]] and [[entities/cboe|Cboe]], First Trust contributed to establishing [[concepts/in-kind-options|In-Kind Options]] transfers for derivative ETFs, introducing structural tax-efficiency breakthroughs and helping extend options trading hours to 4:15 p.m. to synchronize with equity ETF creation and redemption windows. Their category experienced substantial growth, advancing from 58th in flows in 2018 to the top ten, amidst a broader buffer-fund market projected toward $600 billion, while also managing structural considerations such as [[concepts/oscillation-risk|Oscillation Risk]] alongside peers like [[entities/alpha-architect|Alpha Architect]].
