# Rob Arnott

## Description

Rob Arnott is the founder and chairman of [[Research Affiliates]]. He is recognized as a pioneer in tax-aware investing, having co-authored the seminal 1993 paper "Is Your Alpha Big Enough to Cover Its Taxes?" with Tad Jordanrey. Arnott emphasizes that investment management fees are not the only significant friction for investors, demonstrating that taxes exert a substantial drag on returns. Examining historical mutual fund performance across thousands of Morningstar funds over a 25-year horizon, he documented that fund returns fell by roughly 20 basis points before fees, 1 percent net of fees, 2.5 percent net of taxes, and 3 percent annually following full liquidation. He grounds these findings in Bill Sharpe's arithmetic of active management to highlight the critical importance of evaluating [[Tax Drag]] and optimizing [[After-Tax Outcomes]].
