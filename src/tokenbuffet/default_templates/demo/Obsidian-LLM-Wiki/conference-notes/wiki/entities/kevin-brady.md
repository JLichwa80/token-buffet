# Kevin Brady

## Description

Kevin Brady is a former U.S. Representative who served as the Chairman of the House Ways and Means Committee, leading the House tax-writing process. In discussions surrounding the enactment of the [[entities/tax-cuts-and-jobs-act|Tax Cuts and Jobs Act]], Brady serves as a key witness to the influential role played by the [[entities/tax-foundation|Tax Foundation]] in shaping federal tax policy. He highlighted the organization's economic modeling and analysis as the pivotal element that led lawmakers to prioritize long-term economic growth over superficial political messaging. Working alongside congressional leaders such as [[entities/paul-ryan|Paul Ryan]], Brady championed [[concepts/pro-growth-reform|Pro-Growth Reform]] by utilizing rigorous preparatory data to fundamentally alter legislative intent.
