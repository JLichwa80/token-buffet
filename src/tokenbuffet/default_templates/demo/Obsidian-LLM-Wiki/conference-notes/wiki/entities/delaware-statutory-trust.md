# Delaware Statutory Trust

## Description

A Delaware Statutory Trust (DST) is a legally recognized trust entity used to hold title to income-producing real estate while allowing fractional ownership for multiple investors. Real estate owners seeking relief from day-to-day property management can execute a [[concepts/1031-exchange|1031 Exchange]] into a DST as an intermediary vehicle. Typically, the trust structure holds the real estate asset for an initial period of two to three years before converting into an operating unit of a [[concepts/perpetual-non-traded-reit|Perpetual Non-Traded REIT]] via a Section 721 [[concepts/upreit|upREIT]] exchange. This mechanism facilitates a fully tax-deferred transition for investors moving from active property management into diversified institutional real estate ownership.

Following the transition into operating partnership units of a [[concepts/perpetual-non-traded-reit|Perpetual Non-Traded REIT]], investors gain access to monthly liquidity capped at up to 2% of net asset value (NAV). Additionally, these operating units can be passed down to heirs, offering significant estate planning advantages.
