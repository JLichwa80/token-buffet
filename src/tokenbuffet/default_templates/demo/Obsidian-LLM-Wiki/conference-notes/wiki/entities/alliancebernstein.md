# AllianceBernstein

## Description

AllianceBernstein is a global investment management firm recognized for implementing a patented, deterministic optimization engine tailored for [[entities/municipal-bonds|municipal bond]] portfolios. Operating on a daily cadence, the platform ingests fundamental research, quantitative models, and live liquidity metrics to systematically manage over 50,000 separate accounts while adhering to portfolio manager constraints. The engine evaluates trade feasibility across fixed-income and [[entities/equities|equities]] markets by verifying whether projected [[concepts/tax-loss-harvesting|tax loss harvesting]] benefits exceed OTC transaction frictions and odd-lot costs. It also factors in structural tax regulations such as the [[concepts/de-minimis-rule|de minimis rule]] to optimize post-tax outcomes. Empirical data from 2022 to 2025 demonstrates that this systematic workflow delivered 36 basis points of annualized [[concepts/tax-alpha|tax alpha]].
