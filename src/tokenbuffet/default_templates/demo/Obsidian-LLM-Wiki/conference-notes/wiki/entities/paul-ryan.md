# Paul Ryan

## Description

[[entities/paul-ryan|Paul Ryan]] served as Speaker of the United States House of Representatives during the legislative debates surrounding the [[entities/tax-cuts-and-jobs-act|Tax Cuts and Jobs Act]]. During the bill's drafting, Ryan consulted the [[entities/tax-foundation|Tax Foundation]] to evaluate the economic impact of temporary corporate tax rate reductions versus permanent policy changes. When modeling indicated that a temporary rate cut would fail to generate long-term economic growth, Ryan and House leadership pivoted to ensure the corporate rate reduction was structured as permanent [[concepts/permanency|permanency]] in the final statute. Ryan's engagement illustrates the mechanism through which independent economic modeling directly informs live federal tax policy and [[concepts/pro-growth-reform|pro-growth reform]], alongside parallel legislative efforts led by Ways and Means Chairman [[entities/kevin-brady|Kevin Brady]].
