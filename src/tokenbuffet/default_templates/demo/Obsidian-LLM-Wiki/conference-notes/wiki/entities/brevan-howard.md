# Brevan Howard

## Description

Brevan Howard is an investment management firm and the original developer of the whole-portfolio technology designed to translate after-tax investment theory into customized transitions, tools that have since become part of [[entities/nuveen|Nuveen]]. The platform enables tax-neutral transitions out of concentrated equity positions or business-sale windfalls by funding accounts with marginable assets, deploying proprietary signals to avoid crowded trades, harvesting tax losses daily, and targeting allocations across direct indexing, active models, and exchange-traded funds. Within the narrative, the firm functions simultaneously as the product vendor and as the liquidity event behind a substantial capital gain realized by [[entities/taylor|Taylor]], which was subsequently neutralized via a 275/275 long-short book.

During the sale of his business, [[entities/taylor|Taylor]] leveraged Brevan Howard's tech-enabled long-short strategy to eliminate 75% of his capital gains tax liability within five months.
