# Jamie

## Description

Jamie is a fixed income portfolio manager and representative of Canby Capital who delivers the keynote. He describes how conventional bond investing can default to home-state municipal bonds, creating tax and market inefficiencies. He uses a warehouse metaphor to explain how fragmentation in the bond market creates opportunities for active managers. He also argues that technology can help scale individualized separately managed account (SMA) management.

Prior to his role at Canby Capital, he managed an $18 billion fixed income allocation at [[entities/fidelity|Fidelity]]. At the Tax Management Conference, he championed active cross-asset tax-loss harvesting and systematic portfolio customization.
