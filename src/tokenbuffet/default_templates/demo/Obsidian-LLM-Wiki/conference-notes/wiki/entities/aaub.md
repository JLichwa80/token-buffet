# AAUB

## Description

AAUB is the flagship destination exchange-traded fund (ETF) within the [[entities/alpha-architect|Alpha Architect]] [[concepts/syndicated-351-exchange|Syndicated 351]] program. Positioned as a low-cost, 9.45-basis-point, market-cap-weighted U.S. large-cap fund, it operates alongside sibling tickers AUS, AAQ, and AAUA. Unlike a closed single-firm branded ETF, AAUB serves as a pooled structure open to qualified advisors executing a [[concepts/section-351-exchange|Section 351 Exchange]]. Under its [[concepts/look-through|Look-Through Basis]] mechanics, contributed tax lots map directly onto the new ticker while preserving their original acquisition dates and cost basis. Contributions for AAUB close on July 8, 2026, with investor shares framed economically as receipts for the contributed underlying securities.

Major custodians like [[entities/schwab|Schwab]] facilitate this implementation by mapping over original tax lots directly under the AAUB ticker, preserving identical acquisition dates and cost basis for each contributed position.
