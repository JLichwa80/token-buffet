# Bitcoin

## Description

Bitcoin is treated on this panel as a concentrated, low-basis source asset that sits on the same planning bridge as single-name equities. Speaker 5 compares it to a startup cap table because a relatively small group of insiders holds a large fraction of the capitalization. Approval of Bitcoin ETFs and in-kind creations is described as the on-ramp that brings previously siloed crypto capital into traditional tools, including [[Variable Prepaid Forward|variable prepaid forwards]] and [[Tax-Aware Long-Short|tax-aware long-short]]. With volatility around 100 percent, a [[Variable Prepaid Forward|VPF]] is said to create time runway and control over the recognition year. The concrete illustration is a $10 million sale of IBIT on December 25, which would leave only days to harvest offsetting losses, whereas a forward can push recognition into the following year so a full year of shielding is available. That path is part of the [[ETS Playbook|ETS playbook]] and can be sequenced with the [[Laddered Diversification Framework|laddered diversification framework]] through the [[Crypto ETF Bridge|crypto ETF bridge]].
