# Steve Daines

## Description

Steve Daines is a Republican U.S. Senator representing Montana who served as a pivotal legislative actor in securing permanent status for key investment tax provisions. Armed with research from the [[entities/tax-foundation|Tax Foundation]], Daines withheld support for the major tax package known as the "One Big Beautiful Bill" until permanency for investment rules was guaranteed. He took this stance directly to the White House and argued to colleagues that the bill could only credibly be designated as pro-growth with these provisions made permanent. The Senate version containing permanent treatment ultimately became law, illustrating how a single well-briefed lawmaker can decisively shape federal tax legislation.

Specifically, Daines conditioned his support on making research and development (R&D) deductibility and full capital expenditure expensing permanent components of the legislation.
