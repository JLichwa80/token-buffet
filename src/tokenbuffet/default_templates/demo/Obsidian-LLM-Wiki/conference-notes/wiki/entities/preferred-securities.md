# Preferred Securities

## Description

Preferred securities are hybrid investment instruments possessing characteristics of both equities and fixed-income debt, offering priority in dividend payments and liquidation over common equity. Approximately half of the preferred securities universe distributes [[concepts/qualified-dividend-income|Qualified Dividend Income]], which is taxed at preferential capital gains rates rather than standard income tax brackets. In tax-aware portfolio construction, preferred allocations can be paired with holdings such as [[entities/municipal-bonds|Municipal Bonds]] to optimize after-tax yield without taking on excessive credit or duration risk. Incorporating preferred securities helps mitigate [[concepts/tax-drag|Tax Drag]], serving as a foundational public market component in sophisticated private wealth strategies.

Specifically, incorporating a targeted 15–20% allocation of QDI-focused preferred securities alongside [[entities/municipal-bonds|Municipal Bonds]] substantially elevates after-tax yield potential while maintaining a balanced risk profile.
