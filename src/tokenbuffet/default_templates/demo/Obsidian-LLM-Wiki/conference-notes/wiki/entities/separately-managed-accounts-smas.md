# Separately Managed Accounts (SMAs)

## Description

Separately Managed Accounts (SMAs) hold securities directly for an individual client, allowing portfolio customization and tax-loss harvesting. The keynote describes SMAs as a long-standing vehicle used by wealthy investors and argues they may suit larger, tax-sensitive clients better than pooled funds. It identifies a $350,000 account minimum as a threshold below which the firm recommends considering ETFs. The speaker argues that technology can help managers optimize each account individually at scale. [[Canby Capital]] recommends considering [[Fidelity]] and ETFs for smaller accounts, according to the keynote.
