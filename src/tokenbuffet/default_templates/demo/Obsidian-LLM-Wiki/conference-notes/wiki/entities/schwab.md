# Schwab

## Description

Schwab is a major financial services provider and the largest vendor of [[concepts/fundamental-indexing|RAFI]] (fundamental index) products, managing over $100 billion across fundamental index ETFs and mutual funds, including the [[entities/fndx|FNDX]] ETF. In research conducted jointly with the speaker's firm across three non-overlapping historical windows from 2008 to 2023, Schwab modeled [[concepts/tax-alpha|tax alpha]] comparing fundamental index strategies against traditional market-cap-weighted large-cap indices. In these simulations, the cap-weighted [[concepts/active-side-of-indexing|active side of indexing]] delivered approximately 170 basis points of tax alpha, whereas RAFI yielded roughly 190 basis points, framed as additive to roughly 2% of Fama–French alpha. However, when evaluating returns net of management fees, the tax alpha advantage between RAFI and cap-weighted approaches tends to converge, making true excess return ([[concepts/fundamental-indexing|fundamental indexing]] alpha) rather than tax savings alone the key differentiator.

Operationally, Schwab serves as an advisor custodian in [[concepts/section-351-exchange|Section 351 exchange]] transitions, executing security transfers to the ETF destination custodian two days prior to fund launch and mapping original tax lots—preserving acquisition dates and cost basis—under the newly issued ETF ticker.
