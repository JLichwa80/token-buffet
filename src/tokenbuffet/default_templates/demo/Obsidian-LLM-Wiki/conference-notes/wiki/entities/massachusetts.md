# Massachusetts

## Description

Massachusetts is presented as a high-tax state where investors may be advised to hold home-state municipal bonds. The keynote challenges that default, citing that national municipal bonds outperformed Massachusetts municipal bonds on an after-tax basis for a Massachusetts resident about 53% of the time over 25 years. It attributes this result to supply and demand, including constrained local issuance and strong demand. The example shows why an investor’s state tax situation and bond pricing both matter to portfolio choices, and relates to [[The Tax Trap]], [[Insensitive Demand]], and [[Tax Persona]].
