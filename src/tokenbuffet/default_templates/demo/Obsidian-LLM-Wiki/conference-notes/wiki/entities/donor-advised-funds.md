# Donor-Advised Funds

A donor-advised fund (DAF) is a charitable giving vehicle that allows donors to make a charitable contribution, receive an immediate tax deduction, and subsequently recommend grants from the fund. In the keynote, DAFs are cited as an essential component of a high-net-worth family's holistic balance sheet. Advisors must account for DAFs alongside taxable accounts, trusts, and retirement vehicles to build coordinated tax designs. Leveraging DAFs enables advisors to plan strategic charitable donations using highly appreciated assets to avoid capital gains taxes.

> "The industry was built around pre-tax portfolios, one-size-fits-all models, and broad product selection, but high-net-worth clients arrive with concentrated stock, legacy mutual funds, donor-advised funds, trusts, 529s, IRAs, business interests, and pending liquidity events." — [[sources/10-09-keynote_-after-tax-wealth-management-and-tax-stack-design-summary-1_d01a81]]
