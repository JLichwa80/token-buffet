# Bill Sharpe

## Description

Bill Sharpe is an influential economist and Nobel laureate renowned for his foundational contributions to financial economics and modern portfolio theory. In the context of after-tax wealth management, [[entities/rob-arnott|Rob Arnott]] explicitly invokes Sharpe's arithmetic of active management to contextualize why investment costs and taxes are structurally decisive. Because active managers collectively hold the market portfolio before costs, their aggregate performance inevitably underperforms the index by the exact sum of fees and transaction frictions. Sharpe's theoretical framing reinforces that generating true alpha is mathematically demanding, meaning [[concepts/tax-drag|Tax Drag]] and expenses must be systematically minimized to preserve capital.
