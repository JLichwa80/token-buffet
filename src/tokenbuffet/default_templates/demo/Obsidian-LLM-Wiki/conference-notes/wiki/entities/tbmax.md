# TBMax

## Description

TBMax is the companion share class paired with [[T-Bill]] in the dual share class example from [[FM Investments]]. The presentation shows a two-line total-return chart of T-Bill and TBMax that overlaps, offered as proof that mutual fund and ETF buyers receive an equivalent investor experience. The pair is presented as one portfolio with the same NAV and the same rights, so ETF trading efficiency reaches mutual fund buyers under the [[Dual Share Class]] structure associated with [[Vanguard]]. Action points tell advisors to map client holdings to available dual share products such as T-Bill/TBMax and migrate where suitable. The source does not publish a ticker, expense ratio, or inception date for TBMax.
