## Core Content

This conference presentation, titled *"Wrappers, Washington, and What’s Next: Turning Tax Policy into Portfolio Power"*, examines how asset managers can capitalize on structural discrepancies between federal tax legislation and market implementation to generate [[concepts/tax-alpha|tax alpha]]. Rather than treating tax policy as a friction point, the discussion demonstrates how intelligent [[concepts/wrapper|wrapper]] design converts pre-tax investment returns into durable after-tax compounding. Key innovations include [[concepts/dual-share-class|dual share class]] fund structures that allow a [[entities/mutual-fund|mutual fund]] and an [[concepts/etf|etf]] to share the same portfolio and NAV while leveraging [[concepts/in-kind-creationredemption|in-kind creation and redemption]] mechanics, as well as emerging [[concepts/tokenized-t-bills|tokenized T-bills]] that reduce operational costs and enable 24/7 liquidity. Furthermore, the presentation outlines systematic [[concepts/dividend-avoidance|dividend avoidance]] strategies such as [[concepts/cpmgcphy|CPMG/CPHY]], which rotate into a [[concepts/proxy-fund|proxy fund]] around ex-dividend dates to capture daily [[concepts/nav-accrual|NAV accrual]] while transforming ordinary income distributions into long-term capital gains.

## Main Points

- Tax policy is fragmented, slow-moving, and frequently produces unintended outcomes (such as the [[concepts/cobra-effect|Cobra effect]]), creating structural arbitrage opportunities for tax-aware product design.
- Investment [[concepts/wrapper|wrappers]] determine tax flow-through and practical investor outcomes, acting as the mechanism that converts raw investment alpha into holdable [[concepts/tax-alpha|tax alpha]].
- [[concepts/dual-share-class|Dual share class]] architectures combine mutual funds and ETFs onto a single NAV, bringing institutional [[concepts/in-kind-creationredemption|in-kind creation/redemption]] tax efficiencies directly to mutual fund investors.
- [[concepts/tokenized-t-bills|Tokenized T-bills]] eliminate intermediary layers, subvert traditional penny bid-ask spreads, and provide continuous 24/7 secondary liquidity.
- Strategies such as [[concepts/cpmgcphy|CPMG/CPHY]] utilize brief rotations into a [[concepts/proxy-fund|proxy fund]] during ex-dividend windows to bypass ordinary income tax drag while preserving continuous [[concepts/nav-accrual|NAV accrual]].
