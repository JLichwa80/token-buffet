## Core Content

This keynote address examines the critical discrepancy between theoretical gross investment returns and realized, post-liquidation performance in taxable portfolios. Emphasizing that conventional factor strategies and [[concepts/smart-beta|smart-beta]] premia largely evaporate after accounting for friction and taxation, the presentation introduces [[concepts/after-tax-benchmarking|after-tax-benchmarking]] to reflect an inherent ~170 bps tax drag. The analysis critiques [[concepts/cap-weighted-indexing|cap-weighted-indexing]] as harboring an [[concepts/active-side-of-indexing|active-side-of-indexing]] that cyclically over-allocates to overvalued growth assets while divesting from discounted securities. In contrast, [[concepts/fundamental-indexing|fundamental-indexing]] breaks the link to price by weighting firms according to economic footprint, capturing [[concepts/rebalancing-alpha|rebalancing-alpha]] via disciplined [[concepts/mean-reversion|mean-reversion]]. Furthermore, vehicle architecture proves paramount: [[entities/mutual-fund|mutual-funds]] suffer catastrophic post-liquidation drag relative to ETFs. Finally, disciplined execution via [[concepts/cash-flow-rebalancing|cash-flow-rebalancing]] and tax-loss harvesting generates significant [[concepts/tax-alpha|tax-alpha]], while avoiding the [[concepts/correlation-trap|correlation-trap]] ensures true portfolio differentiation.

## Main Points

- Most conventional investing alpha and smart beta premia disappear or turn negative once real-world fees, taxes, and post-liquidation liabilities are applied.
- Realistic performance evaluation requires [[concepts/after-tax-benchmarking|after-tax-benchmarking]] that factors in an approximate 170 bps annual tax drag; beating benchmark minus 1.70% represents real-world value creation.
- Investment vehicle structure drives massive tax divergence: [[entities/mutual-fund|mutual-funds]] trail benchmarks by ~3.5% post-liquidation due to forced capital gains distributions, whereas ETFs trail by only ~1.1%.
- Traditional [[concepts/cap-weighted-indexing|cap-weighted-indexing]] is not purely neutral; its ~5% annual turnover functions as an [[concepts/active-side-of-indexing|active-side-of-indexing]] that acts like momentum chasing by buying expensive growth stocks and selling discounted value stocks.
- [[concepts/fundamental-indexing|fundamental-indexing]] (RAFI) and equal-weight strategies generate durable [[concepts/rebalancing-alpha|rebalancing-alpha]] by systematically exploiting market mispricing and [[concepts/mean-reversion|mean-reversion]].
- Avoiding the [[concepts/correlation-trap|correlation-trap]] requires severing constituent weights from market capitalization; screening fundamentally but weighting by price preserves a ~99.9% correlation to standard indices.
- Systematic execution through [[concepts/cash-flow-rebalancing|cash-flow-rebalancing]] and continuous tax-loss harvesting can capture 170 to 190 bps of [[concepts/tax-alpha|tax-alpha]], safeguarding long-term compounding.
