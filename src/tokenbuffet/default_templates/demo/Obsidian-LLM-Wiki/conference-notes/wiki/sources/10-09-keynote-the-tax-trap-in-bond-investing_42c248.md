## Core Content

At the Tax Management Conference, keynote speaker [[entities/jamie|Jamie]] (formerly managing an $18 billion book at [[entities/fidelity|Fidelity]]) challenges conventional fixed income wisdom. The presentation exposes the [[concepts/the-tax-trap|tax-trap]]—the systematic underperformance caused by treating bonds as passive allocations and reflexively buying 100% home-state [[entities/municipal-bonds|municipal-bond]] issues. 

Due to inelastic municipal issuance and [[concepts/insensitive-demand|insensitive-demand]] in high-tax states, out-of-state munis, [[entities/treasury-bond|treasury-bond]] issues, and taxed [[entities/corporate-bonds|corporate-bond]] securities frequently deliver higher net after-tax returns. While pooled vehicles like [[entities/mutual-fund|mutual-fund]] products suffer from tax drag and cannot harvest losses, and ETFs cannot tailor to an investor's unique [[concepts/tax-persona|tax-persona]], technology-driven [[entities/separately-managed-accounts-smas|separately-managed-account]] structures enable [[concepts/systematized-customization|systematized-customization]] at scale. Operating across an [[concepts/over-the-counter-trading|over-the-counter-trading]] market of over 1.5 million distinct [[entities/cusip|cusip]] debt securities, algorithmic SMA management brings equity-style [[concepts/direct-indexing|direct-indexing]] and continuous [[concepts/tax-loss-harvesting|tax-loss-harvesting]] to fixed income without running into [[concepts/capacity-constraints|capacity-constraints]].

## Main Points

- The traditional "buy 100% home-state munis and hold" strategy for high-net-worth investors is flawed due to severe supply-demand imbalances in high-tax jurisdictions.
- Over a 25-year period, national municipal bonds outperformed Massachusetts municipal bonds on an after-tax basis for Massachusetts residents approximately 53% of the time.
- Municipal bond issuance is inelastic and project-tied, while tax-sensitive demand is rigid and price-insensitive, driving yields down to where corporate or Treasury bonds often beat munis after tax.
- Pooled vehicles are structurally sub-optimal for tax-sensitive wealth: mutual funds cannot pass through realized capital losses, and ETFs cannot accommodate an individual's distinct tax persona.
- The bond market features over 1.5 million CUSIPs traded over-the-counter, operating as an inefficient marketplace where algorithmic search and optimization unlock substantial alpha.
- Systematized customization replaces reactive "common denominator" allocations with personalized mathematical solutions for thousands of SMA client portfolios at scale.
