# Household Tax Plan

> Stub created by the ingest candidate gate (aside+covered) — [[sources/10-09-keynote_-after-tax-wealth-management-and-tax-stack-design-summary-1_d01a81]] names this without treating it. Will be filled by the next ingest of a source that does.

A Household Tax Plan is a coordinated blueprint that unifies tax management across all entities, structures, and accounts owned by a family unit. Rather than treating accounts in isolation, it accounts for differences between taxable, tax-deferred, and tax-exempt wrappers. It incorporates projected liquidity needs, concentrated stock unwinds, and multi-year tax brackets. The plan serves as the foundational diagnostic and strategic layer of an advisor's overall tax stack.

> "The tax stack includes:
- A household tax plan
- A view of the family balance sheet
- Awareness of different tax statuses" — [[sources/10-09-keynote_-after-tax-wealth-management-and-tax-stack-design-summary-1_d01a81]]
