# Tax Persona

## Definition

An investor’s specific profile of federal, state, and local tax rates and related tax constraints, used to assess the after-tax outcomes of investments.

## Key Characteristics

- The profile can affect whether municipal, corporate, or Treasury bonds offer the best after-tax outcome.
- It provides a basis for optimizing a fixed income portfolio to an investor’s circumstances rather than to a generic model.
- It is a key reason pooled ETFs cannot offer the same account-level customization as separately managed accounts.

## Applications

Use a tax persona to compare bond types by their after-tax returns and guide the customization of fixed income holdings in an individual account.
