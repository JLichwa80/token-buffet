# Pending Liquidity Event

## Definition

A pending liquidity event is an action playbook and tax design framework applied before a business owner realizes a major capital gain—such as through an outright business sale, a public-market liquidity transaction, or a generational transfer. It involves proactively structuring and allocating assets prior to the closing date so that loss-generating strategies can build an offset against the anticipated tax liability.

## Key Characteristics

- **Pre-Event Timing**: Asset allocation and harvesting mechanisms are executed well ahead of the liquidity event to maximize loss generation before gains are realized.
- **Holistic Household Scope**: Evaluates the business equity interest alongside all taxable and non-taxable securities accounts rather than treating the business in isolation.
- **Loss Inventory Generation**: Leverages structured vehicles to manufacture capital losses that directly counterbalance the lump-sum capital gain.
- **Client-Centric Discovery**: Prioritizes deep alignment with the business owner's personal goals, psychological timeline, and lifestyle needs alongside pure tax minimization.

## Applications

- **Business Sale Offsets**: Allocating capital into loss-harvesting portfolios ahead of an M&A transaction to reduce net taxable proceeds.
- **Public-Market Transitions**: Preparing concentrated corporate founder or executive shares for secondary offerings or lockup expirations.
- **Generational Business Transfers**: Offsetting taxable events triggered during succession planning and restructuring of closely held family businesses.
