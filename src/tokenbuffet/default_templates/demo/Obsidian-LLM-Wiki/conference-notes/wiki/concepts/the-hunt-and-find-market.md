# The "Hunt and Find" Market

## Definition

The “Hunt and Find” Market is a model of fixed income as a fragmented over-the-counter market, where active managers search among many individual securities for relative value.

## Key Characteristics

- Fixed income includes approximately 1.5 million bond CUSIPs, compared with a few thousand equity tickers, according to the keynote.
- Bonds trade across a fragmented over-the-counter market without a central exchange.
- Searching a warehouse for a mispriced item illustrates the role of active discovery in bond management.
- Technology and electronic trading may lower trading costs while leaving opportunities to identify relative value.

## Applications

Active bond managers can use research and security selection to find mispriced bonds or relative value across a large, fragmented market. Electronic trading can support this work by reducing transaction costs.
