# Electronification

## Definition

Electronification is the use of electronic systems to facilitate bond trading.

## Key Characteristics

- It has reduced trading costs, including bid-ask spreads, and contributed to deeper market liquidity.
- It has not removed the bond market’s over-the-counter structure.
- It has not eliminated opportunities for managers to search for mispriced securities.
- It is linked to bond ETFs’ market activity and to changes in the trading environment for fixed income managers.

## Applications

Electronic systems facilitate bond transactions and support trading in bond ETFs. Fixed income managers operate in this changing environment while continuing to search for mispriced securities.
