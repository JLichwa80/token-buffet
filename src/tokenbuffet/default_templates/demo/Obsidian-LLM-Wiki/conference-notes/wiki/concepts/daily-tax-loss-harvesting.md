# Daily Tax-Loss Harvesting

## Definition

Daily Tax-Loss Harvesting is an algorithmic portfolio management practice that continuously monitors individual positions within separately managed accounts (SMAs) or direct indexing structures to identify and capture capital losses on a daily frequency. By systematically harvesting short- and long-term capital losses as market fluctuations occur, the strategy accumulates an ongoing reserve of tax alpha that can be matched against realized gains to facilitate continuous, tax-efficient portfolio rebalancing.

## Key Characteristics

- **High-Frequency Execution**: Continuously scans holdings and evaluates price movements on a daily schedule rather than relying on traditional quarterly or year-end rebalancing cadences.
- **Direct Indexing & SMA Integration**: Leverages separate managed account architectures to enable granular, security-level trading rather than holding bundled fund products.
- **Systematic Loss Accumulation**: Generates ongoing tax offsets from downward price volatility across individual component securities.
- **Wash-Sale Avoidance**: Employs algorithmic substitution or tracking-proxy selection to maintain target market beta without violating wash-sale rules.
- **Dynamic Rebalancing Enabler**: Provides harvested losses to shield gains generated from trimming concentrated or appreciated stock positions.

## Applications

- **Tax-Efficient Portfolio Transitions**: Offsetting embedded capital gains when transitioning legacy holdings or concentrated executive stock into diversified allocation models.
- **Tax Alpha Generation**: Enhancing after-tax net returns for high-net-worth investors across taxable equity and multi-asset accounts.
- **Systematic Risk Management**: Trimming outperforming positions or rebalancing factor and sector weights continuously without incurring friction from tax liabilities.
