# 1033 Exchange

A Section 1033 exchange is a tax deferral provision under the Internal Revenue Code that applies when property is involuntarily converted, such as through natural disaster, eminent domain, or condemnation. Casey references Section 1033 in the keynote alongside Section 1031 as an essential tool in real estate tax planning. It enables property owners facing involuntary losses or destruction to defer capital gains taxes by reinvesting insurance or compensation proceeds into qualifying replacement property. This provision broadens the advisor's toolkit when handling distressed or compulsory real estate liquidations.
