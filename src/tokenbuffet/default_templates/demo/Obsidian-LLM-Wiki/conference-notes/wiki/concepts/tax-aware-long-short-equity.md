# Tax-Aware Long-Short Equity

> Stub created by the ingest candidate gate (aside+covered) — [[sources/10-09-panel-discussion_-variable-prepaid-forwards-tax-aware-long-short-an_0f1ad3]] names this without treating it. Will be filled by the next ingest of a source that does.

Tax-Aware Long-Short Equity is a quantitative investment methodology designed to match a broad equity market benchmark while systematically harvesting persistent, substantial capital losses. Delivered through separately managed accounts (SMAs) or single-member series LLCs to bypass at-risk limitations, the strategy provides losses that directly shield taxable capital gains elsewhere in an investor's portfolio. It can be funded with cash from a forward or funded directly with appreciated single-name stock to achieve an orderly transition over time.

> "Tax-aware long-short is an old technique—what’s different is it’s now in vehicles individuals can consume, without at-risk limitations." — [[sources/10-09-panel-discussion_-variable-prepaid-forwards-tax-aware-long-short-an_0f1ad3]]
