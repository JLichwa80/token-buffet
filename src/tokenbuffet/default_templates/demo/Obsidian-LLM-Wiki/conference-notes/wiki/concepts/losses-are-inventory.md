# Losses Are Inventory

## Definition

"Losses are inventory" is a core principle of passive tax-loss harvesting and tax-aware portfolio management framing harvested capital losses not as negative investment outcomes or bookkeeping leftovers, but as stored, valuable tax assets that can be inventoried to offset future gains and monetize volatility.

## Key Characteristics

- **Losses as Assets:** Harvested losses function as strategic reserves on the balance sheet rather than mere records of underperformance.
- **Monetizing Volatility:** Converts market fluctuations into tangible tax alpha by harvesting losses during downturns or periods of cross-sectional dispersion.
- **Cross-Sectional Dispersion:** Harvestable losses can be generated even in flat or rising benchmark markets when individual underlying securities move in divergent directions.
- **Utility Prerequisite:** Harvested losses provide no intrinsic economic benefit unless there is an intentional plan or capacity to deploy them against taxable events.

## Applications

- **Offsetting Capital Gains:** Neutralizing realized gains from targeted sales, rebalancing, or liquidation of appreciated assets.
- **Portfolio Transitions:** Offsetting embedded capital gains when transitioning legacy or low-basis portfolios into modern target allocations.
- **Funding Liquidity & Balance Sheet Events:** Providing tax shelter and flexibility ahead of major liquidity events, such as business sales, private asset realizations, or concentrated stock liquidations.
- **Tax-Aware Long-Short Strategies:** Building and managing tax reserves within long-short structures to eliminate tax drag, provided the client has a concrete plan for loss deployment.
