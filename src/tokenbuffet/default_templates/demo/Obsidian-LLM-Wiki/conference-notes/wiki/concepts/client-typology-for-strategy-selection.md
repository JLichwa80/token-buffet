# Client Typology for Strategy Selection

> Stub created by the ingest candidate gate (aside+covered) — [[sources/10-09-panel-discussion_-variable-prepaid-forwards-tax-aware-long-short-an_0f1ad3]] names this without treating it. Will be filled by the next ingest of a source that does.

The panel sorts clients who might combine variable prepaid forwards with long-short into three conviction buckets rather than offering one structure to everyone. If the client wants certainty that the stock will rise—for example, about 10 percent—the advice is not to use a VPF, because the forward gives away part of that upside. If the client would sell except for the tax cost, the fit is a non-constructive-sale hedge with the cash deployed into tax-aware long-short. If the client is bullish on a high-volatility name such as Nvidia, the design keeps upside participation, meaningful downside protection, and the ability to roll. The governing principle is to match the instrument to goals, volatility, and the timeline on which losses will actually be used. A separate simplicity path exists for clients who reject the ladder’s complexity.
