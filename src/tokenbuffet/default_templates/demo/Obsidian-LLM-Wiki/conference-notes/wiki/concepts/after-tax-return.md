# After-Tax Return

## Definition

The return remaining after accounting for an investor’s applicable taxes.

## Key Characteristics

- Depends on the investor’s individual tax profile.
- Comparisons with taxable fixed income depend on the relative pricing of the bonds.
- The keynote uses comparisons involving Massachusetts and national municipal bonds, and municipal bonds versus corporate or Treasury bonds, to challenge the assumption that municipal bonds always outperform for high-tax investors.

## Applications

Use after-tax comparisons to evaluate fixed-income investments based on an investor’s tax situation and the relative pricing of available bonds.
