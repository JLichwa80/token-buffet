# Look-Through

## Definition

Look-through is the process of analyzing and seeing through a pooled investment vehicle (such as a commingled fund or mutual fund) to identify, evaluate, and unpack the underlying holdings, asset exposures, and tax characteristics within it.

## Key Characteristics

- **De-aggregates Pooled Assets**: Bypasses the aggregate fund level to evaluate the individual securities and positions held inside commingled structures.
- **Exposure and Tax Transparency**: Reveals embedded tax liabilities, unrealized capital gains, factor exposures, and asset allocations that are otherwise obscured in pooled vehicles.
- **Dual Complexity**: Represents both a complex quantitative optimization challenge and a demanding data and technology infrastructure problem.
- **Prerequisite for Tax-Aware Transitions**: Serves as the critical diagnostic first step required before designing a migration strategy away from legacy or ossified fund positions.
- **Lack of Tax Lot Control**: Highlights the asymmetry where an advisor or investor can inspect the underlying holdings but cannot trade or manage individual tax lots directly inside the commingled fund.
- **Regulatory Diversification Testing**: Serves as the required statutory evaluation method in Section 351 exchanges, inspecting underlying ETF holdings rather than treating the fund as a single position to test concentration constraints (such as the largest position remaining ≤ 25% and the top five positions remaining ≤ 50%).
## Applications

- **Tax-Aware Transition Analysis**: Used by wealth managers and financial advisors to inspect existing client portfolios and quantify embedded gains before recommending an orderly liquidation or staged transition.
- **Portfolio Health & Risk Auditing**: Applied to determine the true underlying risk, concentration, and factor factor exposures across diverse legacy fund managers.
- **Direct Indexing & Custom SMA Migration**: Acts as the justification and blueprint for transitioning assets out of pooled structures into direct ownership vehicles where discrete tax-loss harvesting and custom control can be executed.
- **Section 351 ETF-for-ETF Swaps**: Applied to evaluate underlying holdings and ensure compliance with household-level diversification rules, enabling investors to execute tax-deferred contributions of appreciated legacy ETFs into a new target ETF vehicle.
