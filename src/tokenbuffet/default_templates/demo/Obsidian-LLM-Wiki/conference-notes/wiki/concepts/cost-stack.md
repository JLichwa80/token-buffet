## Description

The cost stack represents the cumulative structural and operational expenses inherent in executing an active leveraged long-short overlay strategy. These friction costs encompass financing and borrow rates, short rebate dynamics, transaction turnover expenses, and base asset management fees. In a leveraged overlay, this friction creates a compounding performance hurdle that heavily taxes overall results. Without sufficient gross pretax alpha, a manager burdened by the cost stack can realize a deeply negative net Information Ratio (IR)—falling as low as -0.6. Consequently, establishing demonstrable gross alpha capabilities is an essential structural prerequisite before deploying leveraged long-short architecture to overcome these baseline frictions.
