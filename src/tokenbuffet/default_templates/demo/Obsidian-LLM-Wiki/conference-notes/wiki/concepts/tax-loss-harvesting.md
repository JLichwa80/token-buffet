# Tax-Loss Harvesting

## Definition

Tax-loss harvesting is the realization of investment losses to help manage an investor’s tax outcome.

## Key Characteristics

- Access to losses can distinguish mutual funds from individually held bonds in a separately managed account.
- Direct ownership can allow managers to harvest losses within accounts tailored to each client.
- It supports account-level customization in taxable fixed income portfolios.

## Applications

Managers may use tax-loss harvesting in taxable fixed income portfolios to manage each client’s tax outcome, particularly when bonds are held directly in a separately managed account.
