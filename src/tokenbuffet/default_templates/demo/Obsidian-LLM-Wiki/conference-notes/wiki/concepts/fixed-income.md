# Fixed Income

## Definition

Fixed income refers to investments, including municipal, corporate, and Treasury bonds, that pay income under specified terms.

## Key Characteristics

- The market is large and fragmented, with roughly 1.5 million CUSIPs and over-the-counter trading.
- Compared with equities, its complexity may create more opportunities for mispricing.
- Advisors may treat it as a passive allocation, though active, tax-aware management can improve after-tax outcomes.
- Municipal bond supply and demand, taxable alternatives, and customized separately managed accounts influence investment choices.

## Applications

Investors and advisors can use active, tax-aware fixed-income management to evaluate municipal bonds against taxable alternatives and tailor separately managed accounts to an investor’s tax situation. This connects to [[After-Tax Return]], [[The "Hunt and Find" Market]], and [[Tax Persona]].
