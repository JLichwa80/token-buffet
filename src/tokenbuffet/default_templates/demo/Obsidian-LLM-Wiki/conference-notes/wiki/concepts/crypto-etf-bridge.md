# Crypto ETF Bridge

> Stub created by the ingest candidate gate (aside+covered) — [[sources/10-09-panel-discussion_-variable-prepaid-forwards-tax-aware-long-short-an_0f1ad3]] names this without treating it. Will be filled by the next ingest of a source that does.

The crypto ETF bridge is the path that moves Bitcoin and similar holdings into the traditional brokerage and planning system through in-kind creations of crypto ETFs. Speaker 5 says ETFs now provide a bridge from the crypto world to the traditional financial world, after which variable prepaid forwards and tax-aware long-short can be applied to capital that had been siloed. The economic rationale is concentration: Bitcoin is likened to a startup in which insiders hold a large share of the capitalization and therefore a large unrealized gain. Once the position is an ETF such as IBIT, a December 25 sale of $10 million would leave almost no time in that tax year to harvest offsets, so the bridge is typically followed by a VPF that chooses the recognition year. The panel also notes record-fast ETF growth and expects further access if a Clarity Act and closer bank-crypto integration expand the product set.
