# Tax-Aware Long-Short Overlay

> Stub created by the ingest candidate gate (aside+covered) — [[sources/10-09-keynote_-tax-aware-long-short-investing-pretax-alpha-and-after-tax-_8ca74c]] names this without treating it. Will be filled by the next ingest of a source that does.

A tax-aware long-short overlay is an investment structure where an investor pledges existing portfolio assets as collateral to run leveraged long and short extensions around a benchmark beta. The archetype aims to beat the benchmark on a pretax basis while systematically realizing capital losses and deferring capital gains. Key advantages include using short positions to express negative views and harvesting tax losses in diverse market environments due to negative correlation with equities. Empirically, it acts as a dual engine where pretax alpha compounds tax benefits through rebalancing mechanics.

> "In tax-aware long-short portfolios, higher pretax alpha doesn’t just increase wealth—it boosts tax efficiency." — [[sources/10-09-keynote_-tax-aware-long-short-investing-pretax-alpha-and-after-tax-_8ca74c]]
