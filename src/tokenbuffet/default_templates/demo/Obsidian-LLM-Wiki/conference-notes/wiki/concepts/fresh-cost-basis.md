# Fresh Cost Basis

> Stub created by the ingest candidate gate (aside+covered) — [[sources/10-09-keynote_-tax-aware-long-short-investing-pretax-alpha-and-after-tax-_8ca74c]] names this without treating it. Will be filled by the next ingest of a source that does.

Fresh cost basis denotes the newly established baseline purchase price of securities acquired during portfolio rebalancing. In tax-aware long-short strategies, generating positive pretax alpha causes leverage to drift downward, triggering rebalancing actions that introduce new long and short positions at prevailing market prices. These new tax lots possess an unappreciated cost basis that is highly sensitive to subsequent price volatility, making them prime candidates for future tax-loss harvesting. Consequently, continuously establishing fresh cost basis prevents portfolio ossification and fuels the long-term tax engine of the portfolio.

> "Position creation (positive—unique to long-short; alpha makes portfolios under-levered, prompting fresh positions and fresh cost basis)." — [[sources/10-09-keynote_-tax-aware-long-short-investing-pretax-alpha-and-after-tax-_8ca74c]]
