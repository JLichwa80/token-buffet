# Insensitive Demand

## Definition

Insensitive demand is demand for home-state municipal bonds that remains strong even when their prices become unattractive, often because investors value their tax treatment.

## Key Characteristics

- Investors in high-tax states may continue buying local municipal bonds for their tax benefits.
- Issuance is limited and tied to specific projects, which can constrain supply.
- Strong demand and limited supply can inflate local bond prices and reduce their relative value.
- National municipal bonds or taxable securities may offer a better after-tax return.

## Applications

Investors can use this concept to assess whether the tax benefits of home-state bonds justify their prices. Comparing them with national municipal bonds and taxable securities on an after-tax basis may reveal better-value alternatives.
