# Capabilities Map

## Definition

The capabilities map is a structured execution sequence designed to institutionalize after-tax design within an advisory practice. It outlines how an advisory firm transitions from identifying client needs to operationalizing systematic after-tax portfolio implementation, automating mechanical reconciliation, and positioning the advisor as the central coordinator and architect of client tax decisions.

## Key Characteristics

- **Sequential Operational Framework**: Follows a repeatable five-stage progression:
  1. Identify the core client need.
  2. Determine the required technical and operational capabilities.
  3. Build the tailored solution within the after-tax implementation ecosystem.
  4. Automate routine processes and portfolio mechanics as much as possible.
  5. Preserve advisor judgment over strategic decision levers.
- **Advisor as After-Tax Architect**: Positions the wealth advisor at the center of client affairs, orchestrating collaboration across CPAs, estate attorneys, legal counsel, internal investment teams, clients, custodians, and technology platforms.
- **Decision Levers as Dialogue Tools**: Uses gain, risk, liquidity, and time budgets not merely as rigid algorithmic system constraints, but as active communication tools to frame client trade-offs.
- **Scale Grounded in Infrastructure**: Relies on robust backend operating infrastructure (e.g., automated overnight reconciliation across tens of thousands of accounts) to free advisors from operational friction.

## Applications

- **Practice Institutionalization**: Transitioning an advisory practice from ad hoc tax harvesting to a standardized, repeatable after-tax service offering across the entire client base.
- **Multidisciplinary Coordination**: Serving as the operational blueprint when leading multi-professional reviews with external CPAs, estate attorneys, and custodians.
- **High-Volume Portfolio Administration**: Implementing automated overnight account reconciliation and tax tracking across large account volumes while maintaining customized client mandates.
- **Client Onboarding & Transition Management**: Structuring transition budgets and after-tax roadmaps for new clients with legacy low-basis holdings.
