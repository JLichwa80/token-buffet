# tax-year control

## Definition

Tax-year control is the use of a [[Variable Prepaid Forward]] term to choose the year in which gain is recognized. The holder receives liquidity when the forward is entered, while the contract’s duration sets when the gain is recognized.

## Key Characteristics

- Duration is the control lever: the forward’s term sets the deferral timeline, so recognition follows the contract calendar rather than the day liquidity is taken.
- Proceeds are unrestricted and can fund [[Tax-Aware Long-Short]], [[Private Placement Life Insurance]], and other vehicles while recognition remains deferred.
- The panel lists control over the tax deferral period among [[Variable Prepaid Forward]] benefits, alongside no margin calls and unrestricted proceeds.
- The stake is highest for assets near 100 volatility, such as [[Bitcoin]], and when a sale would otherwise fall at year-end.
- A sale of $10 million of [[IBIT]] on December 25 would leave only days to harvest offsetting losses. A [[Variable Prepaid Forward]] can push recognition into the next year so a full year of long-short losses can shield the gain.
- Ladder design lines January 1 settlements up with that shielding calendar, matching loss generation to each maturity.

## Applications

Advisors use tax-year control when a concentrated holder needs cash before year-end but wants the gain recognized in a later tax year. For a December sale of [[IBIT]], the forward can move recognition into the following year, giving [[Tax-Aware Long-Short]] a full year to realize losses that offset the gain. In a [[Laddered Diversification Framework]], forwards settle on January 1 in successive years so each maturity meets a prepared stock of harvested losses. The same proceeds can fund [[Private Placement Life Insurance]] or other destination vehicles while the term keeps the original gain off the current-year return.
