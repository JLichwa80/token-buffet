# ETS Playbook

## Definition
The ETS Playbook is the panel’s name for an end-to-end process that moves low-basis concentrated assets into a diversified, tax-efficient portfolio without immediate gain recognition. Concentrated equity or Bitcoin is the source side of the bridge. Tax-aware long-short and private placement life insurance are the destination side. The [[Variable Prepaid Forward]] is the connecting instrument. The playbook is described as newly cohesive because long-standing variable prepaid forwards are now combined with separately managed accounts, single-member series LLCs, crypto ETFs, and institutionally priced private placement life insurance.

## Key Characteristics
- It treats monetization and reinvestment as one bridge: source assets on one side, tax-aware destinations on the other.
- The variable prepaid forward supplies liquidity and a hedge while deferring sale treatment, so the embedded gain is not recognized at the start.
- Proceeds and remaining exposure are placed in tax-aware long-short allocations and [[Private Placement Life Insurance]], which address ongoing tax drag after the position is diversified.
- Separately managed accounts and single-member series LLCs make the long-short sleeve usable by individual taxable clients, including funding with appreciated single-name securities.
- For Bitcoin, a crypto ETF is the on-ramp that lets the same instruments apply to crypto that previously sat outside traditional brokerage tools.
- The stated outcome is certainty, optionality, and [[Structural Alpha]] that can compound for decades.
- Sequencing matters: forwards can be laddered, and loss harvesting in the long-short sleeve is matched to later recognition dates.
- A plain illustration of the first friction is a $10 million zero-basis sale in California that leaves roughly $6 million after tax.

## Applications
- Employees and insiders with large low-basis stock positions, such as FAANG employees and SpaceX insiders, who would diversify but face a large embedded gain.
- Bitcoin holders who use a crypto ETF creation path so a variable prepaid forward and a tax-aware long-short allocation can diversify a concentrated coin position.
- Taxable investors who reinvest monetization proceeds into tax-aware long-short or institutionally priced private placement life insurance so later gains and distributions are shielded.
- Clients who want a staged exit: a ladder of forwards, matched loss harvesting, and the option to sell what remains when losses are available.
- Situations where a direct contribution of appreciated single names into a long-short account is preferred when an immediate hedge and cash raise are less important than simplicity.
