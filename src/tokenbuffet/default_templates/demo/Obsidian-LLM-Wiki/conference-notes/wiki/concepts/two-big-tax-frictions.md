# Two Big Tax Frictions

> Stub created by the ingest candidate gate (aside+covered) — [[sources/10-09-panel-discussion_-variable-prepaid-forwards-tax-aware-long-short-an_0f1ad3]] names this without treating it. Will be filled by the next ingest of a source that does.

The Two Big Tax Frictions are the panel’s name for the paired problems that arise when a client holds a low-basis concentrated asset. The first friction is the tax bill on a sale: the summary’s worked example is a $10 million zero-basis sale in California that leaves roughly $6 million. The second friction is what happens after the sale, when reinvestment choices can create a fresh series of taxable events and ongoing tax drag. The metaphor is a bridge with concentrated equity or Bitcoin on one side and tax-aware destinations, long-short and PPLI, on the other. The integrated ETS approach is said to address both sides at once by avoiding a constructive sale when the position is monetized and by reducing tax drag in the portfolio the cash is moved into. The chapter is used to set the audience’s context before the instruments are introduced.

> "Core idea: Selling low-basis concentrated assets triggers severe taxes and raises reinvestment tax complications." — [[sources/10-09-panel-discussion_-variable-prepaid-forwards-tax-aware-long-short-an_0f1ad3]]
