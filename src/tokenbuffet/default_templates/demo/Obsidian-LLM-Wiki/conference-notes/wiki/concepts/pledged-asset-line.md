# Pledged Asset Line

A Pledged Asset Line (PAL) is a traditional securities-backed line of credit offered by banks and brokerages that enables clients to borrow against their investment portfolios without selling assets. In wealth management, advisors often turn to PALs to generate liquidity for clients with concentrated stock positions. The keynote highlights synthetic borrowing via an option box as an innovative, lower after-tax cost alternative to traditional PALs.

> "For High-Net-Worth Clients, Explore Synthetic Borrowing: Investigate using 'synthetic borrowing' via an option box as an alternative to traditional Pledged Asset Lines for clients needing liquidity from concentrated positions." — [[sources/10-09-keynote_-tax-aware-portfolio-transitions-and-derivative-etf-risks-s_3a1193]]
