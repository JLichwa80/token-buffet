# Lot-Level Implementation

## Definition

Lot-level implementation is an investment management strategy where portfolio construction, trading, and rebalancing decisions are executed on individual tax lots rather than aggregated at the fund, strategy, or model level. Positioned at the core of the tax stack, it enables granular control over after-tax outcomes by targeting specific cost-basis parcels for tax-loss harvesting, gain minimization, transition management, and client-specific customization.

## Key Characteristics

- **Granular Execution**: Trades and position sizing target specific purchase lots rather than aggregate security positions or pooled vehicles.
- **Center of the Tax Stack**: Functions as the foundational execution layer supporting household diagnosis, portfolio destination design, and after-tax performance measurement.
- **Active Loss and Gain Pairing**: Evaluates capital gains and unrealized losses lot by lot to systematically neutralize tax impact during portfolio changes.
- **High-Throughput Operational Pipeline**: Relies on robust backend data infrastructure—including overnight custodial reconciliation, lot accounting files, and automated trading engines—to process large-scale customizations.
- **Advisory Shift to Architecture**: Removes the operational friction of manual rebalancing, allowing financial advisors to act as portfolio architects rather than standard product selectors.

## Applications

- **Systematic Tax-Loss Harvesting**: Identifying and selling specific underwater tax lots while maintaining target market exposure and avoiding wash sales.
- **Portfolio Transitions**: Developing gradual transition roadmaps for transferring low-basis legacy assets into customized target allocations without triggering catastrophic tax realization events.
- **Bespoke Restrictions and Factor Tilts**: Accommodating personalized mandate constraints (e.g., ESG exclusions, concentrated stock holdings, cash-flow requirements) tailored to the exact cost basis of existing holdings.
- **Household Tax Management**: Coordinating lot disposition across multiple taxable and non-taxable client accounts to optimize overall household after-tax wealth accumulation.
