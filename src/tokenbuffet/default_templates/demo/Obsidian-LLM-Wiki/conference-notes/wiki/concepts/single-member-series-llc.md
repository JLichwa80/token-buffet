# Single-member series LLC

## Definition

A single-member series LLC is an individual investment vehicle, alongside the separately managed account, through which a taxable client can hold a tax-aware long-short allocation and use the losses that allocation produces. In the panel’s account, the long-short technique itself is established. The change is the wrapper: individuals can consume it, and losses are available to the client rather than trapped by at-risk limitations.

## Key Characteristics

- It is grouped with separately managed accounts as a flexible individual vehicle for tax-aware long-short.
- It is the access path that lets taxable clients use losses from the allocation, without the at-risk limitations that can trap those losses in other structures.
- In the updated variable prepaid forward workflow, it sits between a concentrated source asset and a tax-aware destination.
- It can be funded with cash or with appreciated single-name equities.
- The panel summary treats the vehicle as the reason loss utilization is available. It does not describe series segregation, the legal mechanics of a series LLC, or how the interest is taxed.
- It establishes segregated series or cells under an overarching LLC umbrella, treated as a disregarded entity for federal tax purposes.
- It provides dedicated liability protection while avoiding partnership at-risk rules and passive loss limitations.
## Applications

- Advisors place tax-aware long-short inside a single-member series LLC so an individual client can harvest persistent capital losses and apply them against gains elsewhere in the portfolio.
- Proceeds from a [[Variable Prepaid Forward]] can fund the vehicle as the client moves from a concentrated holding toward a diversified, tax-aware portfolio.
- A client who wants a simpler path can fund the same vehicle directly with appreciated single-name equities and transition the exposure over time, using the losses the allocation realizes.
