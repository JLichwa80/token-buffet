# The Tax Trap

## Definition

The Tax Trap is the missed after-tax value that can result from treating fixed income as a passive allocation. A static preference for home-state municipal bonds may overlook better-priced national municipal or taxable bonds. The term also describes the bond industry’s historic neglect of individualized tax management.

## Key Characteristics

- Compares bond opportunities by their after-tax value, not just their stated yield or category.
- Challenges static home-state municipal bond preferences when national municipal or taxable bonds may offer better value.
- Calls for account-specific analysis based on each investor’s tax situation and available market opportunities.

## Applications

- Evaluating home-state municipal bonds against national municipal and taxable alternatives.
- Tailoring bond portfolios to an investor’s tax circumstances and account.
- Identifying missed after-tax return from passive or standardized fixed-income allocations.
