State Tax Regime Sensitivity describes how state-level tax codes deviate from federal tax guidelines, altering the efficacy of wealth planning playbooks. While federal capital gains taxes permit short-term capital losses to offset long-term gains, certain jurisdictions—such as Washington State—disallow or limit these offsets and curtail charitable deductions. Effective wealth strategies must evaluate state-specific constraints alongside federal rules before implementing derivative overlays.
