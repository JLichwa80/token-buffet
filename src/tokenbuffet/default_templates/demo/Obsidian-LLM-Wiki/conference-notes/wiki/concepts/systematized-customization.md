# Systematized Customization

## Definition

Systematized customization encodes portfolio-specific decisions in automated processes so that distinct portfolios can be managed at scale.

## Key Characteristics

- Evaluates a broad universe of available bonds against each client’s constraints and tax persona.
- Repeats portfolio evaluation daily.
- Contrasts with manually allocating a shared trade across many accounts.
- The keynote says this approach can enable one professional to manage thousands of distinct portfolios; this capacity claim reflects the speaker’s account.
- Operates under the paradigm "if you can code it, you can customize it," replacing reactive single-trade allocations with algorithmic portfolio construction that simultaneously solves distinct optimization problems for each account across hundreds of thousands of market opportunities.
## Applications

- Managing separately managed accounts with individualized bond selections.
- Scaling portfolio-specific decisions across many clients while accounting for their constraints and tax personas.
