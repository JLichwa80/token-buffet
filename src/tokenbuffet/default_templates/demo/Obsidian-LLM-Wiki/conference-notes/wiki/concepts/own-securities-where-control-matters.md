# Own Securities Where Control Matters

## Definition

"Own securities where control matters" is a foundational portfolio design principle stating that direct ownership of underlying securities should be prioritized over pooled vehicles whenever granular control generates distinct client value. Rather than mandating universal direct ownership across every asset, it advocates selectively taking direct control to bend the portfolio around a client's tax circumstances, existing holdings, and bespoke constraints.

## Key Characteristics

- **Selective Direct Ownership**: Standardizes direct security ownership where control creates tangible client value, rather than requiring all assets to be held directly.
- **Granular Tax & Lot Management**: Enables lot-level tax-loss harvesting and custom realization timing that pooled investment vehicles cannot provide.
- **Constraint & Holding Adaptability**: Facilitates seamless portfolio construction around legacy concentrated positions, executive blackout windows, and liquidity requirements.
- **Customized Factor & Preference Alignment**: Allows precise tailoring for specific cash-flow needs, active/passive tilts, factor exposures, and ethical or client-mandated restrictions.

## Applications

- **Direct Indexing**: Implementing tailored index strategies where individual constituent equities are owned directly to optimize tax-alpha and factor tilts.
- **Concentrated Position Transition**: Constructing diversified portfolios around large, low-basis single-stock positions while avoiding unnecessary capital gains realization.
- **Executive & Restricted Account Management**: Managing portfolios subject to corporate blackout windows, insider trading restrictions, or custom liquidity timing.
- **Lot-Level Tax Harvesting**: Strategically liquidating specific tax lots to harvest losses against realized gains across a client's broader balance sheet.
