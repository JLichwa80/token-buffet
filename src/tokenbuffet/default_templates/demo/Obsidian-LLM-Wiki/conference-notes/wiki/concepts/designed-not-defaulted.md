# Designed, Not Defaulted

## Definition

**Designed, Not Defaulted** is an advisory thesis in taxable wealth management positing that tax-aware wealth stewardship requires deliberate balance sheet architecture rather than passive reliance on default products and pre-tax conventions. It establishes that tax efficiency is achieved through proactive diagnosis, capital budgets, lot-level implementation, and ongoing governance, rather than treating tax implications as a retrospective reporting afterthought.

## Key Characteristics

- **Proactive Architecture vs. Passive Default**: Replaces default pre-tax models, generic fund wrappers, and unexamined legacy holdings with intentionally constructed household portfolios.
- **Lot-Level Precision**: Employs tax-budgeted transitions, systematic tax-loss harvesting, and granular tax-lot customization to actively manage tax drag.
- **Centrality of Advisory Control**: Elevates the advisor's role from product allocator to balance sheet architect who exercises deliberate operational control over after-tax outcomes.
- **Foundational Triad**: Grounded in three core principles:
  1. Taxable wealth requires distinct management methodologies compared to tax-deferred or pre-tax wealth.
  2. Granular operational and tax control generates measurable financial value.
  3. The advisor is central to orchestrating the client's balance sheet and tax stack.

## Applications

- **Taxable Wealth Management Strategy**: Structuring client onboarding and discovery around multi-account balance sheet diagnostics rather than single-account asset allocation.
- **Tax-Aware Transition Management**: Phasing out legacy, low-basis, or concentrated positions across custom-calibrated tax budgets instead of executing blanket liquidations or remaining frozen in default holdings.
- **Operating Model Redesign**: Shifting firm practice from standardized, pre-tax product selection to customized, multi-asset direct indexing and lot-level execution.
