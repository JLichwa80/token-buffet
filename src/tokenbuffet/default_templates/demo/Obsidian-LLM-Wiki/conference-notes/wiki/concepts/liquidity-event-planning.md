# Liquidity Event Planning

> Stub created by the ingest candidate gate (aside+covered) — [[sources/10-09-keynote_-after-tax-wealth-management-and-tax-stack-design-summary-1_d01a81]] names this without treating it. Will be filled by the next ingest of a source that does.

Liquidity event planning involves structuring and allocating assets well in advance of a significant capital realization event, such as the sale of a privately held business or an initial public offering. In the keynote, this is characterized as an exceptionally high-value planning opportunity where advisors intentionally run loss-generating strategies—such as aggressive direct indexing or long-short portfolios—to build a tax-loss inventory that offsets the massive capital gain generated upon exit.

> "The planning opportunity is to allocate assets in advance of a business sale in order to generate losses that can offset the large capital gain from the liquidity event." — [[sources/10-09-keynote_-after-tax-wealth-management-and-tax-stack-design-summary-1_d01a81]]
