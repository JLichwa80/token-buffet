# Sector/country distortion under cap weights

## Definition

Sector and country distortion under cap weights is the structural tendency of market-capitalization-weighted indexes to shift their geographical and sector exposures according to market price narratives and speculative manias, rather than tracking underlying economic footprints. While conventional market-cap indexing is labeled "passive" by industry convention, its weights fluctuate dynamically with valuations, leading to over-concentration in overvalued sectors or countries and under-allocation to undervalued ones.

## Key Characteristics

- **Divergence from Economic Reality**: Portfolio allocations expand in sectors or countries experiencing valuation multiples expansion (e.g., tech during the dot-com bubble and AI surge, or Japan in the late 1980s), decoupling from actual economic size and cash-flow generation.
- **Pro-Cyclical Concentration / Bubble Embedding**: Cap-weighted mechanisms systematically buy more of assets as they become more expensive and trim assets as they decline, inadvertently concentrating exposure at market peaks.
- **Mean-Reversion Vulnerability**: When narrative-driven market multiples normalize, cap-weighted benchmarks experience sharp reversals and sustained drag as outsized sector or national allocations deflate.
- **Rhetorical Inversion of "Passive"**: While cap weighting is operationally passive regarding turnover and trading rules, fundamental or macro-anchored weighting is arguably passive relative to the broader macro economy because it maintains stable exposure to fundamental economic footprints.

## Applications

- **Benchmark Evaluation**: Assessing country and sector risk concentration within broad market indices (such as the S&P 500 or MSCI World) during market cycle extremes.
- **Fundamental Index Construction**: Designing non-market-cap weighted strategies that link index weights to objective measures of economic scale (e.g., sales, cash flow, dividends, book value) to counteract price distortions.
- **Contrarian Rebalancing & Value Tilts**: Implementing systematic rebalancing rules across sectors and countries to capture rebalancing alpha by trimming overextended narrative weights and reallocating to neglected segments.
