## Description

Position creation is a structural portfolio rebalancing mechanic unique to tax-aware long-short strategies where positive pretax alpha causes the portfolio to become under-levered relative to its target gross exposure. When alpha increases net asset value (NAV) through gains across longs and shorts, percentage gross leverage declines (for example, falling from a 200/100 gross leverage ratio to 186/86), whereas pure market beta leaves percentage leverage unchanged. Restoring target gross exposure requires managers to establish fresh long and short positions with full, current cost bases rather than locked-in legacy holdings. These newly introduced tax lots continuously replenish the portfolio's capacity for subsequent tax-loss harvesting, transforming pretax alpha into a direct engine for ongoing tax benefits rather than locking up the portfolio with built-in gains as typically occurs in long-only direct indexing.
